# 2025 Nissan Kicks — 2.0L inline-4, CVT, AWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, axle/equipment codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Nissan Motor Oil 0W-20 SP or equivalent; if unavailable, Nissan permits synthetic 0W-20 meeting API SP and ILSAC GF-6A.[4]
- **Engine oil drain-and-refill capacity:** 4.1 L (4 3/8 US qt) with filter, or 3.8 L (4 US qt) without filter.[4]
- **Engine coolant:** Pre-diluted Genuine Nissan Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant (blue) or equivalent; approximate capacity with reservoir is 7.4 L (2 US gal).[4]
- **CVT fluid:** Genuine Nissan CVT Fluid NS-3 or equivalent only; service quantity is **Needs research** because the owner manual does not publish one.[4]
- **Rear differential oil:** Genuine Nissan HYPOID FLUID S1 GL-5 75W-80 or equivalent; capacity is **Needs research**.[4]
- **Transfer fluid:** Genuine Nissan Differential Oil Hypoid Super-S GL-5 synthetic 75W-90 or equivalent; capacity is **Needs research**.[4]
- **Coupling oil:** Genuine Nissan LSC Coupling Transmission Fluid 12-301 or equivalent; capacity is **Needs research**.[4]
- **Brake fluid:** Genuine Nissan Super Heavy Duty Brake Fluid or equivalent DOT 3 or DOT 4; fill by the reservoir procedure because no capacity is published.[4]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 83 ft-lb (113 N·m); recheck after 600 miles (1,000 km) following wheel removal and at tire-rotation intervals.[4]
- **Cold tire pressure:** 230 kPa (33 psi) for 215/65R16 or 215/60R17 tires and 240 kPa (35 psi) for 225/45R19 tires; the actual Tire and Loading Information label controls.[4]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 5,000 miles (8,000 km). Rotation pattern is **Needs research** because it is not stated in the cited passage.[4]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Dry-paper element; replace under the maintenance schedule and check/replace more frequently in prolonged dusty operation. Exact service part number is **Needs research**.[4]
- **Cabin air filter:** In-cabin microfilter in the instrument panel; exact service part number is **Needs research**.[4]
- **Oil filter:** Genuine Nissan oil filter or equivalent; exact service part number is **Needs research**.[4]
- **Battery:** Sealed maintenance-free 12-volt battery; exact group size and CCA are **Needs research**.[4]
- **Spark plugs:** SXE22H11C, nominal gap 1.1 mm (0.043 in); Nissan says not to clean or regap the iridium-tipped plugs.[4]
- **Bulbs and LED assemblies:** Headlamp replacement is identified as LED service. The table lists W10W for one map-light configuration and W5W for vanity-mirror and license-plate lamps; most exterior assemblies are dealer-service items. Trim-specific bulb inventory remains **Needs research**.[4]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Standard replacement is listed at 10,000 miles/12 months, subject to the oil-control indicator; when displayed, change within two weeks or 500 miles (800 km).[4]
- **Tires:** Rotate every 5,000 miles (8,000 km).[4]
- **Cabin filter:** Replace at 15,000-mile/18-month intervals; dusty use can require more frequent replacement.[4]
- **Engine air filter:** Replace at 30,000-mile/36-month intervals; inspect more often in dusty conditions.[4]
- **CVT fluid:** Replace at 60,000 miles (96,000 km); rough or muddy-road and car-top-carrier use is specifically called out.[4]
- **AWD driveline fluids:** Inspect transfer fluid, differential oil and coupling oil at scheduled intervals. Under severe operation, the schedule calls for replacement at 20,000-mile/24-month intervals.[4]
- **Coolant:** First replacement at 105,000 miles (168,000 km) or 84 months, then every 75,000 miles (120,000 km) or 60 months.[4]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 105,000 miles (168,000 km) or 126 months.[4]
- **Severe operation:** Repeated short trips, below-freezing short trips, hot stop-and-go use, extensive idling/low-speed commercial use, dusty roads, rough/muddy/salt roads and a car-top carrier trigger the severe-use schedule.[4]

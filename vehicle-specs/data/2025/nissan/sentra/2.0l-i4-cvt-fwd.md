# 2025 Nissan Sentra — 2.0L inline-4, CVT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, axle/equipment codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Nissan Motor Oil 0W-20 SP or equivalent; if unavailable, Nissan permits synthetic 0W-20 meeting API SP and ILSAC GF-6A.[3]
- **Engine oil drain-and-refill capacity:** 4.1 L (4 3/8 US qt) with filter, or 3.8 L (4 US qt) without filter.[3]
- **Engine coolant:** Pre-diluted Genuine Nissan Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant (blue) or equivalent; the CVT model's approximate capacity with reservoir is 7.1 L (1 7/8 US gal).[3]
- **CVT fluid:** Genuine Nissan CVT Fluid NS-3 or equivalent only; capacity is **Needs research** because the owner manual publishes no quantity.[3]
- **Brake fluid:** Genuine Nissan Brake Fluid or equivalent DOT 3 or DOT 4; capacity is not published.[3]
- **Transfer case and rear differential:** Not applicable to this FWD configuration.[3]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 83 ft-lb (113 N·m); recheck after 600 miles (1,000 km) following wheel removal and at tire-rotation intervals.[3]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire and Loading Information label on the driver-side center pillar; the actual vehicle placard controls. Trim/tire-specific values are **Needs research** in this sheet.[3]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 5,000 miles (8,000 km). Rotation pattern is **Needs research** because the cited manual passage does not specify one.[3]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** The standard schedule lists replacement at 10,000 miles/12 months, but the oil-control system can shorten the interval. When the indicator appears, change within two weeks or 500 miles (800 km); if the system resets prematurely or becomes inoperative, Nissan says change within six months or 5,000 miles (8,000 km) of the last service.[3]
- **Tires:** Rotate every 5,000 miles (8,000 km).[3]
- **Cabin filter:** Replace at 15,000-mile/18-month intervals; dusty operation can require more frequent replacement.[3]
- **Engine air filter:** Replace at 30,000-mile/36-month intervals; inspect more frequently in dusty conditions.[3]
- **CVT fluid:** For car-top-carrier or rough/muddy-road use, replace at 60,000 miles (96,000 km) or use Nissan CONSULT deterioration data; the standard schedule also records CVT replacement at 60,000 miles/72 months.[3]
- **Coolant:** First replacement at 105,000 miles (168,000 km) or 84 months, then every 75,000 miles (120,000 km) or 60 months.[3]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 105,000 miles (168,000 km) or 126 months.[3]
- **Severe operation:** Repeated short trips, below-freezing short trips, hot stop-and-go use, extensive idling/low-speed commercial use, dusty roads, rough/muddy/salt roads and a car-top carrier trigger the severe-use schedule.[3]

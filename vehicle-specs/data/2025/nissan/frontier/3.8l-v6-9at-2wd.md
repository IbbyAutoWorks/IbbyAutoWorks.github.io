# 2025 Nissan Frontier — 3.8L V6, 9-speed automatic, 2WD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, cab/bed/trim equipment, axle hardware, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Nissan Motor Oil 0W-20 SP or equivalent; if unavailable, Nissan permits synthetic 0W-20 meeting API SP and ILSAC GF-6A. Owner Manual, “Recommended fluids/lubricants and capacities,” p. 501.[2]
- **Engine oil drain-and-refill capacity:** 5.0 L (5 1/4 US qt) with filter or 4.7 L (5 US qt) without filter. Owner Manual, p. 501.[2]
- **Engine coolant:** Pre-diluted Genuine Nissan Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant (blue) or equivalent; approximate capacity with reservoir is 12.0 L (3 1/8 US gal). Owner Manual, p. 501.[2]
- **Automatic transmission fluid:** Genuine Nissan Matic P ATF or equivalent only. Service quantity is **Needs research** because the owner manual specifies a level procedure but no capacity. Owner Manual, pp. 442 and 501.[2]
- **Brake fluid:** Genuine Nissan Super Heavy Duty Brake Fluid or equivalent DOT 3; capacity is not published. Owner Manual, p. 502.[2]
- **Rear final drive oil:** Genuine Nissan Differential Oil Hypoid Super-CS GL-5 SAE 75W-90 or equivalent; approximate capacity is 2.01 L (4 1/4 US qt). Owner Manual, p. 502.[2]
- **Transfer fluid and front final drive oil:** Not applicable to this 2WD configuration.[2][7]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 98 ft-lb (133 N·m); recheck after 620 miles (1,000 km) following wheel removal and at lubrication intervals. Owner Manual, p. 414.[2]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Check the Tire and Loading Information label on the driver-side center pillar; the actual placard is the authority for the cab, bed, tire and load configuration. Owner Manual, pp. 461–463.[2]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 7,500 miles (12,000 km); the extracted manual passage does not state a pattern, so the rotation pattern is **Needs research**. Owner Manual, pp. 469 and 482.[2]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles/24 months; prolonged dusty operation may require more frequent service. Exact service part number is **Needs research**. Owner Manual, pp. 479 and 485.[2]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 15,000 miles/12 months; dusty operation can require more frequent replacement. Exact service part number is **Needs research**. Owner Manual, pp. 479 and 483.[2]
- **Oil filter:** Genuine Nissan oil filter or equivalent; exact service part number is **Needs research**. Owner Manual, pp. 437 and 501.[2]
- **Battery specification:** Exact 12-volt battery group, chemistry and CCA are **Needs research**; the reviewed owner-manual sections describe battery care and variable-voltage charging but not a replacement specification. Owner Manual, pp. 445–447.[2]
- **Spark plugs:** FXE22HR-11 with a nominal gap of 1.1 mm (0.043 in); do not clean or regap the iridium-tipped plugs. Owner Manual, pp. 448 and 508.[2]
- **Replaceable bulbs versus lamp assemblies:** Halogen Type A headlamps use HB5 low/high and 7444NA turn/park bulbs. Listed replaceable options also include H8 fog, W21W DRL and 912 high-mounted stop/cargo bulbs. Type B headlamps/turn/DRL, bed lamps, rear combination lamps and license-plate lamps are shown without bulb numbers and require trim-specific assembly confirmation. Owner Manual, p. 458.[2]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Standard schedule: every 7,500 miles/6 months. Severe-use schedule: every 5,000 miles/6 months. Owner Manual, pp. 482–497.[2]
- **Brake fluid:** Standard replacement at 30,000 miles/24 months and recurring equivalent milestones; severe-use replacement appears at 10,000 miles/12 months and subsequent severe milestones. Owner Manual, pp. 483–497.[2]
- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles/24 months; dusty use may require more frequent service. Owner Manual, pp. 479 and 485.[2]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 15,000 miles/12 months. Owner Manual, p. 483.[2]
- **Coolant:** First replacement at 105,000 miles (168,000 km) or 84 months, then every 75,000 miles (120,000 km) or 60 months. Owner Manual, p. 495.[2]
- **Automatic transmission fluid:** Inspect at 15,000-mile/12-month schedule points and use CONSULT deterioration data; under severe use, replace at 30,000 miles/24 months and recurring equivalent milestones. Service quantity remains **Needs research**. Owner Manual, pp. 483–497.[2]
- **Rear differential oil:** Inspect at 15,000-mile/12-month schedule points; under severe use, replace at 20,000 miles/24 months and recurring equivalent milestones. Owner Manual, pp. 483–497.[2]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 105,000 miles (168,000 km) or 84 months. Owner Manual, p. 495.[2]
- **Severe/off-road operation:** Repeated short trips, freezing short trips, hot stop-and-go use, extensive idling/low-speed commercial use, dusty/rough/muddy/salt roads, towing/camper/roof-carrier use and off-road/deep-water operation trigger additional maintenance. Owner Manual, pp. 480–481.[2]

## Variant warnings

- This sheet covers the VQ38DD, automatic 9-speed and 2WD/rear-wheel-drive Frontier. EPA vehicle 48509 confirms the 3.8L six-cylinder, automatic 9-speed and rear-wheel-drive identity.[7]
- 4WD front-final-drive and transfer fluids do not apply; use the separate 4WD sheet.[2][7]
- Cab, bed, trim, wheel/tire, towing and axle equipment remain VIN/placard checks even when the engine and drivetrain identity match.[2]
- EPA data confirms configuration identity but is not mechanical-service authority.[7]

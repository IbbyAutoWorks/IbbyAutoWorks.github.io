# 2025 Nissan Rogue — 1.5L VC-Turbo inline-3, CVT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, axle/equipment codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Nissan Motor Oil 0W-20 SP or equivalent; if unavailable, Nissan permits synthetic 0W-20 meeting API SP and ILSAC GF-6A. Owner Manual, “Capacities and recommended fluids/lubricants,” p. 567.[1]
- **Engine oil drain-and-refill capacity:** 4.7 L (5 US qt) with filter, or 4.6 L (4 7/8 US qt) without filter. Owner Manual, p. 567.[1]
- **Engine coolant:** Pre-diluted Genuine Nissan Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant (blue) or equivalent; approximate capacity with reservoir is 8.5 L (9 US qt). Owner Manual, p. 567.[1]
- **Intercooler coolant:** The same blue Nissan long-life coolant or equivalent; approximate capacity with reservoir is 3.0 L (3 1/8 US qt), including a 0.5 L (1/2 US qt) reservoir. Owner Manual, p. 567.[1]
- **CVT fluid:** Genuine Nissan CVT Fluid NS-3 or equivalent only. Service quantity is **Needs research** because the owner manual does not publish one. Owner Manual, pp. 511 and 567.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Genuine Nissan Super Heavy Duty Brake Fluid or equivalent DOT 3; capacity is not published. Owner Manual, pp. 512 and 568.[1]
- **Transfer fluid, rear differential oil and coupling oil:** Not applicable to this FWD configuration.[1]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 80 ft-lb (108 N·m); recheck after 600 miles (1,000 km) following wheel removal and at tire-rotation intervals. Owner Manual, pp. 484 and 537.[1]
- **Cold tire pressure:** The vehicle’s Tire and Loading Information label controls. The manual lists 235/65R17 all-season tires at 36 psi front/33 psi rear, while 235/60R18 and 235/55R19 tires are 33 psi front/30 psi rear. Verify the actual tire, trim and placard before use. Owner Manual, pp. 530–532 and 573.[1]
- **Tire rotation:** Standard schedule: every 7,500 miles/12 months. Severe-use schedule: every 3,750 miles/6 months. The extracted manual passage does not specify a rotation pattern, so pattern is **Needs research**. Owner Manual, pp. 550–551.[1]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Replace at 30,000 miles/48 months under the published schedule; inspect or replace more frequently in dusty operation. Exact service part number is **Needs research**. Owner Manual, pp. 547 and 554.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace at 11,250 miles/18 months and repeat at the same interval; dusty operation can require more frequent replacement. Exact service part number is **Needs research**. Owner Manual, pp. 547 and 552–562.[1]
- **Oil filter:** Genuine Nissan oil filter or equivalent; exact service part number is **Needs research**. Owner Manual, p. 571.[1]
- **Battery:** Sealed maintenance-free 12-volt battery; exact group size and CCA are **Needs research**. Owner Manual, pp. 514 and 546.[1]
- **Spark plugs:** ILMAR8G8GS with a nominal gap of 0.8 mm (0.031 in). Owner Manual, p. 573.[1]
- **Replaceable bulbs versus LED assemblies:** High/low headlamps, parking/daytime-running lamps, stop/tail lamps, side turn signals and high-mounted stop lamp are LED assemblies. Listed replaceable bulbs include 7444NA front turn signal where bulb-equipped, W5W front/rear side-marker and license-plate lamps, W21W rear turn signal, W16W back-up lamp, W8W map/room lamps, and W5W rear personal/vanity lamps. Trim-specific verification remains required. Owner Manual, pp. 526–527.[1]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** The oil-control system governs the interval; the standard schedule reaches 7,500 miles/12 months, while severe operation is shown at 3,750 miles/6 months. When the oil-replacement indicator appears, Nissan says change within two weeks or 500 miles (800 km); if the indicator resets prematurely or becomes inoperative, change within six months or 3,750 miles (6,000 km). Owner Manual, pp. 549–551.[1]
- **Tires:** Rotate every 7,500 miles/12 months normally or 3,750 miles/6 months under severe use. Owner Manual, pp. 550–551.[1]
- **Cabin filter:** Replace every 11,250 miles/18 months; dusty use can require more frequent replacement. Owner Manual, pp. 547 and 552–562.[1]
- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles/48 months; dusty use can require more frequent service. Owner Manual, pp. 547 and 554.[1]
- **CVT fluid:** Inspect at scheduled intervals. For car-top-carrier, trailer/camper, rough-road or muddy-road use, replace every 60,000 miles (96,000 km) or use Nissan CONSULT deterioration data; service quantity remains **Needs research**. Owner Manual, p. 548.[1]
- **Coolant:** First engine-coolant replacement at 105,000 miles (168,000 km) or 84 months, then every 75,000 miles (120,000 km) or 60 months. Intercooler coolant is described as maintenance-free. Owner Manual, pp. 547, 562–563.[1]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 60,000 miles (96,000 km) or 96 months. Owner Manual, p. 558.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** The exact replacement interval needs a cleaner OEM table extraction before publication as a numeric interval; inspect level and condition at scheduled service. Owner Manual, pp. 512 and 550–562.[1]
- **Severe operation:** Repeated short trips, below-freezing short trips, hot stop-and-go traffic, extensive idling or low-speed commercial use, dusty/rough/muddy/salt roads, a car-top carrier, trailer towing or camper use trigger additional maintenance. Owner Manual, pp. 549–550.[1]

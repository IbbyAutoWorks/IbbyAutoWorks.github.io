# 2025 Nissan Pathfinder — 3.5L V6, 9-speed automatic, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, tow-package equipment, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Nissan Motor Oil 0W-20 SP or equivalent; if unavailable, Nissan permits synthetic 0W-20 meeting API SP and ILSAC GF-6A. Owner Manual, “Recommended fluids/lubricants and capacities,” p. 607.[1]
- **Engine oil drain-and-refill capacity:** 4.8 L (5 1/8 US qt) with filter or 4.5 L (4 3/4 US qt) without filter. Owner Manual, p. 607.[1]
- **Engine coolant:** Pre-diluted Genuine Nissan Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant (blue) or equivalent. Approximate capacity with reservoir is 11.9 L (3 1/8 US gal) with the tow package or 11.6 L (3 1/8 US gal) without it; confirm equipment before filling. Owner Manual, p. 607.[1]
- **Automatic transmission fluid:** Genuine Nissan Matic R ATF only. The owner manual does not publish a service-fill quantity, so capacity is **Needs research**. Owner Manual, p. 607.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Genuine Nissan Super Heavy Duty Brake Fluid or equivalent DOT 3; capacity is not published. Owner Manual, p. 608.[1]
- **Transfer fluid, differential gear oil and coupling oil:** Not applicable to this FWD configuration.[1][5]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 83 ft-lb (113 N·m); recheck after 600 miles (1,000 km) following wheel removal and at tire-rotation intervals. Owner Manual, p. 579.[1]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Check the Tire and Loading Information label on the driver-side center pillar; the actual placard is the authority. Owner Manual, pp. 569–571.[1]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 10,000 miles (16,000 km); the extracted manual passage does not state a pattern, so the rotation pattern is **Needs research**. Owner Manual, pp. 579 and 591.[1]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles/36 months; inspect or replace more frequently in prolonged dusty operation. Exact service part number is **Needs research**. Owner Manual, pp. 587 and 593.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 15,000 miles/18 months; dusty operation can require more frequent replacement. Exact service part number is **Needs research**. Owner Manual, pp. 587 and 592.[1]
- **Oil filter:** Genuine Nissan oil filter or equivalent; exact service part number is **Needs research**. Owner Manual, pp. 547 and 607.[1]
- **Battery specification:** Exact 12-volt battery group, chemistry and CCA are **Needs research**; the owner manual describes a variable-voltage charging system but does not publish a replacement battery specification in the reviewed sections. Owner Manual, pp. 553–555.[1]
- **Spark plugs:** FXE22HR-11 with a nominal gap of 1.1 mm (0.043 in); do not clean or regap the iridium-tipped plugs. Owner Manual, pp. 556 and 613.[1]
- **Replaceable bulbs versus lamp assemblies:** Listed replaceable bulbs include 7444NA front turn, W5W vanity/rear side-marker, WY21W rear turn and W16W backup lamps. Headlamp high/low, park/DRL, front side marker, fog, mirror turn, high-mounted stop, stop/tail and license-plate entries are shown without bulb numbers and require assembly/trim-specific confirmation. Owner Manual, p. 567.[1]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Oil-control-system based; the normal schedule reaches 10,000 miles/12 months. When the indicator appears, change within two weeks or 500 miles (800 km); if it resets prematurely or becomes inoperative, change within six months or 5,000 miles (8,000 km). Owner Manual, pp. 589–591.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Replace every 10,000 miles/12 months in the published schedule; the same replacement also appears at intervening severe-use milestones. Owner Manual, pp. 591–603.[1]
- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles/36 months; dusty use may require more frequent service. Owner Manual, pp. 587 and 593.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 15,000 miles/18 months. Owner Manual, p. 592.[1]
- **Coolant:** First replacement at 105,000 miles (168,000 km) or 84 months, then every 75,000 miles (120,000 km) or 60 months. Owner Manual, p. 599.[1]
- **Transmission fluid:** Inspect for leakage at specified intervals; a numeric normal or severe replacement interval and service quantity are **Needs research** for this FWD sheet. Owner Manual, pp. 588 and 591–603.[1]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 105,000 miles (168,000 km). Owner Manual, p. 599.[1]
- **Severe operation:** Repeated short trips, freezing short trips, hot stop-and-go use, extensive idling/low-speed commercial use, dusty/rough/muddy/salt roads, towing, camper use or a roof carrier trigger additional maintenance. Owner Manual, p. 589.[1]

## Variant warnings

- This sheet covers the VQ35DD, 9-speed automatic and front-wheel-drive Pathfinder. EPA vehicle 48606 confirms the 3.5L six-cylinder, automatic 9-speed and FWD identity.[5]
- Rock Creek fuel guidance, 4WD axle/coupling/transfer fluids and all 4WD service items are excluded; use the separate 4WD sheet.[1][5]
- Tow-package status changes the published coolant capacity and must be confirmed before service.[1]
- EPA data confirms configuration identity but is not mechanical-service authority.[5]

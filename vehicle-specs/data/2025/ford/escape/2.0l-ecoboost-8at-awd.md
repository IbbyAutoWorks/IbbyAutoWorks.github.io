# 2025 Ford Escape — 2.0L EcoBoost, 8-speed automatic, AWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, trim, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Motorcraft SAE 5W-30 Motor Oil, WSS-M2C961-A1. For ambient temperatures of -22°F (-30°C) or below, Ford lists SAE 0W-30 meeting WSS-M2C963-A1 as the alternative; Ford also calls for the API gasoline-engine certification mark when Motorcraft oil is unavailable.[4]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 6.13 qt (5.8 L).[4]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Antifreeze meeting Ford WSS-M97B57-A2; the exact-year page identifies this specification as yellow coolant.[6]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 2.25 gal (8.5 L).[6]
- **Transmission fluid:** Motorcraft MERCON ULV Automatic Transmission Fluid XT-12-QULV, WSS-M2C949-A.[7]
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** 11.6 qt (11 L) is the published approximate dry-fill capacity for the 8F35; Ford states the actual amount can vary during fluid changes. An exact drain-and-fill service quantity remains **Needs research**.[7]
- **Brake fluid:** Motorcraft DOT 4 LV High Performance Motor Vehicle Brake Fluid PM-20, WSS-M6C65-A2 or ISO 4925 Class 6.[8]
- **Transfer case / power-transfer-unit fluid and capacity:** **Needs research**; no exact PTU fluid specification or capacity was accepted from the inspected exact-year pages.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** No separately labeled front-differential service fill was accepted from the exact-year source; independent quantity is **Needs research**.[7]
- **Rear differential / disconnect rear-drive-unit fluid:** Motorcraft Disconnect Rear Drive Unit Fluid XY-75W-QL; capacity 0.8 qt (0.75 L). Ford states routine changes or level checks are not required unless a leak is suspected or the assembly has been submerged.[9]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 100 lb-ft (135 N·m) for M12 x 1.5 wheel nuts with clean, rust-free threads; retighten within 100 mi (160 km) after wheel disturbance.[10]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Inflate to the value on the actual Tire Label/placard for the installed tires; the physical vehicle placard controls. Trim-specific reference values remain **Needs research**.[21]
- **Tire rotation:** Rotate at every Intelligent Oil-Life Monitor oil-change interval and follow the exact owner-manual diagram; after rotation, check/adjust pressures and reset the tire-pressure system when the label specifies different front/rear pressures. Dusty or sandy operation calls for rotation every 5,000 mi (8,000 km).[12][15][16]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Motorcraft FA-2064; replace every 30,000 mi (48,000 km) normally, with frequent inspection/replacement in dusty or dirty operation.[14][15][16]
- **Cabin air filter:** Motorcraft FP-100-A; replace every 20,000 mi (32,000 km) normally, with frequent inspection/replacement in dusty, dirty, extensive-idling or low-speed commercial operation.[14][15][16]
- **Oil filter:** Motorcraft FL-2127; if unavailable, Ford specifies a filter meeting SAE/USCAR-36, Filter Type B.[14]
- **Battery specification:** Motorcraft BAGM-48H6-760. Confirm current supersession and the installed electrical package before ordering.[14]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Motorcraft SP-597-X; exact gap is **Needs research**.[14]
- **Exterior bulbs versus LED assemblies:** **Needs research**; no exact replaceable-bulb versus LED-assembly table was accepted from the inspected exact-year source.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the Intelligent Oil-Life Monitor. Ford's examples are 7,500–10,000 mi for normal use, 5,000–7,500 mi for severe use and 3,000–5,000 mi for extreme use; do not exceed one year or 10,000 mi. Dusty/sandy operation calls for 5,000 mi (8,000 km) or six months.[15][16]
- **Brake fluid:** Replace every 3 years.[15]
- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 mi (48,000 km) normally; inspect and replace more frequently in dusty/dirty operation.[15][16]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 20,000 mi (32,000 km) normally; inspect and replace more frequently in dusty/dirty or extensive-idling/low-speed commercial operation.[15][16]
- **Coolant:** Initial replacement at 10 years or 200,000 mi (322,000 km), then every 5 years or 100,000 mi (160,000 km).[15]
- **Transmission fluid:** Inspect the automatic-transmission fluid level at each oil-change interval. Replace at 150,000 mi (240,000 km) normally; towing, extensive idling/low-speed commercial use, and dusty/sandy operation call for replacement every 30,000 mi (48,000 km).[15][16]
- **Rear drive unit:** Inspect the rear axle at every oil-change interval. The exact-year capacity page says routine fluid changes/level checks are not required unless a leak is suspected or the unit was submerged.[9][15]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace every 100,000 mi (160,000 km) normally; towing and extensive-idling/low-speed commercial use call for 60,000 mi (96,000 km).[15][16]

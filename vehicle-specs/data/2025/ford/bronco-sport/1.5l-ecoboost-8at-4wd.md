# 2025 Ford Bronco Sport — 1.5L EcoBoost, 8-speed automatic, 4WD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, trim/equipment, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Motorcraft SAE 0W-20 meeting Ford WSS-M2C962-A1 — Owner's Manual, pp. 306–307.[1]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 5.0 US qt (4.7 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 307.[1]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Motorcraft Yellow Prediluted Antifreeze/Coolant VC-13DL-G meeting Ford WSS-M97B57-A2 — Owner's Manual, p. 315.[1]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 7.9 US qt (7.5 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 315.[1]
- **Transmission fluid:** Motorcraft MERCON ULV Automatic Transmission Fluid XT-12-QULV meeting Ford WSS-M2C949-A — Owner's Manual, p. 166.[1]
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** **Needs research.** The exact-year owner manual specifies the fluid but not a service-fill quantity.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Motorcraft DOT 4 LV High Performance Motor Vehicle Brake Fluid PM-20 meeting Ford WSS-M6C65-A2 or ISO 4925 Class 6; fill as required — Owner's Manual, p. 173.[1]
- **Transfer case / power-transfer-unit fluid and capacity:** **Needs research.** The manual provides PTU service messages and maintenance cautions but not a fluid specification or quantity.[1]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** **Needs research.** Do not infer a separate fill from another Ford model.[1]
- **Rear differential / rear-drive-unit fluid and capacity:** **Needs research.** Exact installed hardware and service fill require current VIN-specific OEM service information.[1]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 100 lb-ft (135 N·m), M12 × 1.5; retighten within 100 mi (160 km) after wheel disturbance — Owner's Manual, p. 376.[1]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire Label or safety-certification label on the driver-side B-pillar/driver-door edge; the physical vehicle placard controls — Owner's Manual, pp. 335, 346.[1]
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate at every oil-change interval, follow the manual's four-tire diagram, exclude a dissimilar temporary spare, adjust pressures afterward and reset TPMS when front/rear placard pressures differ — Owner's Manual, pp. 352–353, 419.[1]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Motorcraft FA-2065 — Owner's Manual, p. 326.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Motorcraft FP-100-A — Owner's Manual, p. 326.[1]
- **Oil filter:** Motorcraft FL-910-S; if unavailable, Ford specifies SAE/USCAR-36 Filter Type B — Owner's Manual, p. 326.[1]
- **Battery specification:** **Needs research.** The manual directs the reader to the dealer for the current part number and requires battery-management-system configuration to match the replacement — Owner's Manual, p. 326.[1]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Motorcraft SP-597-X; 0.028 in (0.7 mm) — Owner's Manual, pp. 324, 326.[1]
- **Exterior bulbs versus LED assemblies:** Replaceable Y16W rear turn-signal, P27 stop and 21W reverse bulbs. Headlamps, fog lamps, front/rear side markers, mirror turn signals, high-mounted stop lamp, license-plate lamps and tail lamps are LED; Ford says LED lamps are not serviceable — Owner's Manual, p. 322.[1]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the Intelligent Oil-Life Monitor; service at the message and never exceed one year or 10,000 mi (16,000 km) — Owner's Manual, p. 419.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Replace every 3 years — Owner's Manual, p. 420.[1]
- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 mi (48,000 km); inspect/replace more frequently in dusty or dirty severe service — Owner's Manual, pp. 420–421.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 20,000 mi (32,000 km); inspect/replace more frequently in dusty or dirty severe service — Owner's Manual, pp. 420–421.[1]
- **Coolant:** Initial replacement at 10 years or 200,000 mi (320,000 km), then every 5 years or 100,000 mi (160,000 km) — Owner's Manual, p. 420.[1]
- **Transmission/axle/PTU fluids:** Automatic-transmission fluid at 150,000 mi (240,000 km) normally; severe service calls for earlier transmission-fluid service without a fixed mileage in the table. Ford states the PTU and rear axle have no scheduled maintenance, but submersion, leakage or extended extreme/severe duty can require service — Owner's Manual, pp. 420–421.[1]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 100,000 mi (160,000 km), or 60,000 mi (96,000 km) for severe service — Owner's Manual, p. 420.[1]

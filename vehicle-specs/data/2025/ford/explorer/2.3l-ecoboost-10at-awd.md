# 2025 Ford Explorer — 2.3L EcoBoost, 10-speed automatic, part-time 4WD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, trim/equipment, axle, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Motorcraft SAE 5W-30 meeting Ford WSS-M2C961-A1; Ford permits SAE 0W-30 meeting WSS-M2C963-A1 for the manual's specified extremely cold/high-altitude conditions — Owner's Manual, pp. 426–428.[1]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 5.0 US qt (4.8 L) — Owner's Manual, pp. 426.[1]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Motorcraft Yellow Prediluted Antifreeze/Coolant VC-13DL-G meeting Ford WSS-M97B57-A2 — Owner's Manual, pp. 432–433.[1]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 14.3 US qt (13.5 L) — Owner's Manual, pp. 432–433.[1]
- **Transmission fluid:** Motorcraft MERCON ULV Automatic Transmission Fluid XT-12-QULV meeting Ford WSS-M2C949-A — Owner's Manual, p. 441.[1]
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** **Needs research.** The exact-year owner manual specifies the fluid but does not publish a transmission service quantity in the inspected capacity section.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Motorcraft DOT 4 LV High Performance Motor Vehicle Brake Fluid PM-20 meeting Ford WSS-M6C65-A2 or ISO 4925 Class 6 — Owner's Manual, pp. 441–442.[1]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Motorcraft MERCON LV meeting WSS-M2C938-A; 1.1 US qt (1.0 L) for four-wheel drive — Owner's Manual, p. 442.[1]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Motorcraft SAE 75W-140 Synthetic Rear Axle Lubricant meeting WSL-M2C192-A; 0.6 US qt (0.6 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 443.[1]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Motorcraft SAE 75W-85 Premium Synthetic Hypoid Gear Lubricant meeting WSS-M2C942-A. The manual lists 54.1 fl oz (1.6 L) for non-Timberline and 60.9 fl oz (1.8 L) for Timberline, with 3 fl oz (90 mL) friction modifier additionally required for the limited-slip differential; exact trim/axle mapping remains **Needs research** before filling. — Owner's Manual, pp. 443–444.[1]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 150 lb-ft (204 N·m), M14 × 1.5; retighten within 100 mi (160 km) after wheel disturbance — Owner's Manual, p. 417.[1]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire Label or safety-certification label on the driver-side B-pillar/driver-door edge; the physical vehicle placard controls the front and rear cold pressures. Trim-specific reference pressures remain **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, p. 385.[1]
- **Tire rotation:** Follow the owner-manual rotation diagram, check/adjust pressures after rotation, and reset the tire-pressure system when front/rear label pressures differ. The manual refers to a recommended interval but does not state that fixed mileage in the inspected pages; exact interval is **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, p. 402.[1]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Motorcraft FA-2067 — Owner's Manual, p. 423.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Motorcraft FP-100-A — Owner's Manual, p. 423.[1]
- **Oil filter:** Motorcraft FL-2127; Ford specifies SAE/USCAR-36 Filter Type B if the Motorcraft filter is unavailable — Owner's Manual, p. 423.[1]
- **Battery specification:** Motorcraft BAGM-48H6-760 — Owner's Manual, p. 423.[1]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Motorcraft SP-597-X; gap 0.026–0.030 in (0.65–0.75 mm) — Owner's Manual, pp. 419–423.[1]
- **Exterior bulbs versus LED assemblies:** Replaceable rear direction indicator 3757NAK/PY27/7W and reversing lamp 21W. Front markers/signature/direction indicator/DRL/headlamp/fog lamps, side indicators, rear side markers, brake/tail lamp, center high-mounted stop lamp and license-plate lamp are LED assemblies; Ford says LED lamps are not serviceable — Owner's Manual, pp. 374–375.[1]

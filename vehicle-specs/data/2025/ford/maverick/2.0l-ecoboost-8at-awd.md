# 2025 Ford Maverick — 2.0L EcoBoost, 8-speed automatic, AWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain/trim equipment, tow package, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Motorcraft SAE 5W-30 meeting Ford WSS-M2C961-A1; SAE 0W-30 meeting WSS-M2C963-A1 is the extremely-cold/high-altitude alternative described by the manual — Owner's Manual, pp. 347–348.[1]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 6.1 US qt (5.77 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 348.[1]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Motorcraft Yellow Prediluted Antifreeze/Coolant VC-13DL-G meeting Ford WSS-M97B57-A2 — Owner's Manual, p. 356.[1]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 9.0 US qt (8.5 L) without trailer-tow package; 9.5 US qt (9.02 L) with trailer-tow package — Owner's Manual, p. 356.[1]
- **Transmission fluid:** Motorcraft MERCON ULV Automatic Transmission Fluid XT-12-QULV meeting Ford WSS-M2C949-A — Owner's Manual, p. 175.[1]
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** **Needs research.** The exact-year manual does not publish a service-fill quantity.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Motorcraft DOT 4 LV High Performance Motor Vehicle Brake Fluid PM-20 meeting Ford WSS-M6C65-A2 or ISO 4925 Class 6; fill as required — Owner's Manual, pp. 181–182.[1]
- **Transfer case / power-transfer-unit fluid and capacity:** **Needs research.** The manual discusses PTU maintenance but publishes no service fluid/quantity.[1]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** **Needs research.** Do not infer a separate service fill for the transaxle.[1]
- **Rear differential / rear-drive-unit fluid and capacity:** **Needs research.** Confirm the installed AWD equipment and current OEM service information.[1]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 148 lb-ft (200 N·m), M14 × 1.5; retighten within 100 mi (160 km) after wheel disturbance — Owner's Manual, p. 410.[1]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire Label or safety-certification label on the driver-side B-pillar/driver-door edge; the actual vehicle placard controls — Owner's Manual, pp. 380, 391.[1]
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate at every oil-change interval using the owner-manual diagram; exclude a dissimilar spare, adjust pressure and reset TPMS when required — Owner's Manual, pp. 397–398, 451.[1]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Motorcraft FA-2064 — Owner's Manual, p. 370.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Motorcraft FP-100-A — Owner's Manual, p. 370.[1]
- **Oil filter:** Motorcraft FL-2127; if unavailable, Ford specifies SAE/USCAR-36 Filter Type B — Owner's Manual, p. 370.[1]
- **Battery specification:** **Needs research.** The manual directs the reader to the dealer for the current part number and requires battery-management-system configuration — Owner's Manual, p. 370.[1]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Motorcraft SP-597-X; 0.026–0.030 in (0.65–0.75 mm) — Owner's Manual, pp. 368, 370.[1]
- **Exterior bulbs versus LED assemblies:** Replaceable 5W front side marker, 4257NA amber front turn, WT21-7W rear stop/turn/marker depending lamp series, WT21W reverse and 912 center cargo/high-mounted lamp. Low/high beams, high-series rear side marker, license-plate, cargo-box and underhood lamps are LED assemblies; LEDs are not serviceable — Owner's Manual, p. 363.[1]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the Intelligent Oil-Life Monitor; service at the message and never exceed one year or 10,000 mi (16,000 km) — Owner's Manual, p. 451.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Replace every 3 years — Owner's Manual, p. 452.[1]
- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 mi (48,000 km), with more frequent dusty/dirty severe-service inspection/replacement — Owner's Manual, pp. 452–453.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 20,000 mi (32,000 km), with more frequent dusty/dirty severe-service inspection/replacement — Owner's Manual, pp. 452–453.[1]
- **Coolant:** Initial replacement at 10 years or 200,000 mi (320,000 km), then every 5 years or 100,000 mi (160,000 km) — Owner's Manual, p. 452.[1]
- **Transmission/axle/PTU fluids:** Automatic-transmission fluid at 150,000 mi (240,000 km) normally; severe service calls for earlier service without a fixed mileage in the table. PTU/rear axle have no normal scheduled service, with severe/extreme duty, leakage and submersion exceptions — Owner's Manual, pp. 452–453.[1]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 100,000 mi (160,000 km), or 60,000 mi (96,000 km) in severe service — Owner's Manual, p. 452.[1]

## Variant warnings

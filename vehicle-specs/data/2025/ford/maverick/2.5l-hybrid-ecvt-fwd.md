# 2025 Ford Maverick Hybrid — 2.5L, eCVT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, hybrid powertrain, transmission, drivetrain, cooling circuits, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Motorcraft SAE 0W-20 meeting Ford WSS-M2C962-A1 — Owner's Manual, pp. 349–350.[1]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 5.7 US qt (5.4 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 350.[1]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Motorcraft Yellow Prediluted Antifreeze/Coolant VC-13DL-G meeting Ford WSS-M97B57-A2 for both circuits — Owner's Manual, pp. 356–357.[1]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 9.6 US qt (9.1 L) high-temperature circuit and 5.0 US qt (4.7 L) low-temperature circuit; keep circuits distinct — Owner's Manual, p. 356.[1]
- **Transmission fluid:** **Needs research.** The inspected owner-manual automatic-transmission fluid section applies to the conventional automatic and does not establish the hybrid eCVT fluid.[1]
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** **Needs research.** No hybrid transaxle service quantity was found.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Motorcraft DOT 4 LV High Performance Motor Vehicle Brake Fluid PM-20 meeting Ford WSS-M6C65-A2 or ISO 4925 Class 6; fill as required — Owner's Manual, pp. 181–182.[1]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD identity.[2]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** **Needs research.** Do not assume a separate service fill for the hybrid transaxle.[1]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD identity.[2]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 148 lb-ft (200 N·m), M14 × 1.5; retighten within 100 mi (160 km) after disturbance — Owner's Manual, p. 410.[1]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire Label or safety-certification label on the driver-side B-pillar/driver-door edge; the physical placard controls — Owner's Manual, pp. 380, 391.[1]
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate at every oil-change interval using the manual diagram; exclude a dissimilar spare, adjust pressure and reset TPMS when required — Owner's Manual, pp. 397–398, 451.[1]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Motorcraft FA-1948 — Owner's Manual, p. 370.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Motorcraft FP-100-A — Owner's Manual, pp. 370–371.[1]
- **Oil filter:** Motorcraft FL-910-S; if unavailable, Ford specifies SAE/USCAR-36 Filter Type B — Owner's Manual, pp. 370–371.[1]
- **Battery specification:** **Needs research.** The manual does not publish a replacement 12V rating/part number and does not use that field for the high-voltage traction battery — Owner's Manual, pp. 370–371.[1]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Motorcraft SP-530-X; 0.049–0.053 in (1.25–1.35 mm) — Owner's Manual, pp. 369–370.[1]
- **Exterior bulbs versus LED assemblies:** Replaceable 5W front side marker, 4257NA front turn, WT21-7W rear combination bulbs, WT21W reverse and 912 center cargo/high-mounted lamp. Headlamps, high-series rear marker, license-plate, cargo-box and underhood lamps are non-serviceable LED assemblies — Owner's Manual, p. 363.[1]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the Intelligent Oil-Life Monitor and never exceed one year; Ford notes hybrids may travel farther between messages, but the one-year maximum remains — Owner's Manual, p. 451.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Replace every 3 years — Owner's Manual, p. 452.[1]
- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 mi (48,000 km), more often in dusty/dirty severe service — Owner's Manual, pp. 452–453.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 20,000 mi (32,000 km), more often in dusty/dirty severe service — Owner's Manual, pp. 452–453.[1]
- **Coolant:** Initial replacement at 10 years or 200,000 mi (320,000 km), then every 5 years or 100,000 mi (160,000 km); keep both hybrid cooling circuits in scope — Owner's Manual, p. 452.[1]
- **Transmission/axle fluids:** The generic schedule states automatic-transmission fluid at 150,000 mi (240,000 km), with earlier severe service, but exact applicability and required fluid for the hybrid eCVT remain **Needs research**.[1]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 100,000 mi (160,000 km), or 60,000 mi (96,000 km) in severe service — Owner's Manual, p. 452.[1]

## Variant warnings

- This is EPA 48869 FWD; do not apply AWD PTU/rear-axle fields or the EcoBoost conventional-automatic fluid specification.[2]
- High- and low-temperature cooling circuits are separate.[1]
- The battery field must not confuse the 12V battery with the high-voltage traction battery.

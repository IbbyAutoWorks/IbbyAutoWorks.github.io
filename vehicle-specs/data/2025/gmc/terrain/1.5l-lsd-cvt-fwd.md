# 2025 GMC Terrain — 1.5L LSD turbo inline-4, CVT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, option/RPO codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** SAE 0W-20 full-synthetic engine oil meeting GM dexos1 — Owner's Manual, pp. 226–227.[4]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.2 qt (4.0 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 294.[4]
- **Coolant type/specification:** 50/50 clean drinkable water and DEX-COOL coolant — Owner's Manual, p. 291.[4]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 6.4 qt (6.1 L), entire system — Owner's Manual, pp. 294–295.[4]
- **Transmission fluid:** GM High Performance CVT Fluid (HPCVTF); obtain the current product through GM — Owner's Manual, p. 291.[4]
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Needs research; the owner manual does not publish a CVT service-fill quantity.[4]
- **Brake fluid:** GM-approved DOT 4 hydraulic brake fluid from a clean, sealed container — Owner's Manual, pp. 236, 291.[4]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD sheet.[4][8]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; no separate front-final-drive service fluid or capacity is published in the owner manual.[4]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD sheet.[4][8]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 100 lb-ft (140 N·m) — Owner's Manual, p. 295.[4]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire and Loading Information label/vehicle placard for the installed tire package; the physical placard is the authority — Owner's Manual, pp. 253, 260.[4]
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate every 7,500 mi (12,000 km), use the illustrated pattern, exclude the compact spare, reset pressures to the placard and relearn TPMS positions — Owner's Manual, pp. 260, 286–288.[4]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research; replace according to the engine-air-filter life messages and reset the life system — Owner's Manual, pp. 228–229, 287–288.[4]
- **Cabin air filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research; replace every 22,500 mi (36,000 km) or 24 months, sooner for heavy traffic, poor air quality, dust, allergens, reduced airflow, fogging or odor — Owner's Manual, p. 288.[4]
- **Oil filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research.[4]
- **Battery specification:** AGM 12-volt battery; use the replacement number on the original battery label. Group size and CCA need research — Owner's Manual, p. 236.[4]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Exact plug part number needs research; factory gap 0.024–0.028 in (0.60–0.70 mm), and re-gapping is not recommended — Owner's Manual, p. 295.[4]
- **Exterior bulb types:** The owner manual identifies several LED lamps and directs LED-assembly replacement to the dealer, but does not publish a complete trade-number inventory; exact replaceable-bulb versus LED mapping needs research — Owner's Manual, p. 240.[4]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the oil-life system; change within 600 mi (1,000 km) of the message and at least annually. If accidentally reset, service within 3,000 mi (5,000 km) of the last service — Owner's Manual, pp. 227, 287.[4]
- **Brake fluid:** Every 5 years — Owner's Manual, p. 289.[4]
- **Engine air filter:** Follow the filter-life messages; severe dust may require earlier service — Owner's Manual, pp. 228–229, 287–289.[4]
- **Cabin air filter:** Every 22,500 mi (36,000 km) or 24 months, with shorter intervals under the listed environmental conditions — Owner's Manual, p. 288.[4]
- **Coolant:** Drain and fill at 150,000 mi (240,000 km) or 6 years, whichever comes first — Owner's Manual, p. 288.[4]
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Severe service: change CVT fluid and filter every 45,000 mi (72,000 km). No FWD axle/transfer-case interval is published — Owner's Manual, p. 289.[4]
- **Spark plugs:** 60,000 mi (96,000 km); extreme service may require more frequent replacement — Owner's Manual, pp. 288–289.[4]

## Variant warnings

- This sheet is only for the 1.5L LSD engine, CVT and FWD configuration. The AWD vehicle uses an 8-speed automatic, a different oil capacity, transfer-case fluid and a rear-axle service path — Owner's Manual, pp. 291, 294–295.[4]
- EPA vehicle 48603 independently identifies the 2025 Terrain FWD as a 1.5L turbo four-cylinder with a variable-ratio automatic and regular gasoline.[8]
- Tire pressure always comes from the actual vehicle placard.

# 2025 Chevrolet Malibu — 1.5L LFV turbo inline-4, CVT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, option/RPO codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** SAE 0W-20 full-synthetic engine oil meeting GM dexos1 — Owner's Manual, pp. 230–231.[3]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.2 qt (4.0 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 307.[3]
- **Coolant type/specification:** 50/50 clean drinkable water and DEX-COOL coolant — Owner's Manual, p. 304.[3]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 7.4 qt (7.0 L), entire system — Owner's Manual, p. 307.[3]
- **Transmission fluid:** GM continuously variable ratio transmission fluid; obtain the current exact product through GM — Owner's Manual, p. 304.[3]
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Needs research; the owner manual does not publish a CVT service-fill quantity.[3]
- **Brake fluid:** GM-approved DOT 4 hydraulic brake fluid from a clean, sealed container — Owner's Manual, pp. 239, 304.[3]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD configuration.[3][7]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; no separate front-final-drive service fluid or capacity is published in the owner manual.[3]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD configuration.[3][7]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 100 lb-ft (140 N·m) — Owner's Manual, p. 307.[3]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire and Loading Information label/vehicle placard for the installed tire package; the physical placard is the authority. The manual's 38 psi high-speed value applies only to listed 245/40R19 tires during 100-mph-or-higher operation and is not a substitute for the placard — Owner's Manual, pp. 261–263.[3]
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate every 7,500 mi (12,000 km), use the illustrated pattern, exclude the compact spare, reset pressures to the placard and relearn TPMS positions — Owner's Manual, pp. 268, 299–301.[3]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research; replace according to the engine-air-filter life messages and reset the life system — Owner's Manual, pp. 232–233, 301.[3]
- **Cabin air filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research; replace every 22,500 mi (36,000 km) or 24 months, sooner for heavy traffic, poor air quality, dust, allergens, reduced airflow, fogging or odor — Owner's Manual, p. 301.[3]
- **Oil filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research.[3]
- **Battery specification:** 12-volt battery; use the replacement number on the original label. The vehicle may have AGM depending on equipment, so verify the original label before replacement; group size and CCA need research — Owner's Manual, p. 239.[3]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Exact plug part number needs research; factory gap 0.024–0.028 in (0.60–0.70 mm), and re-gapping is not recommended — Owner's Manual, p. 308.[3]
- **Exterior bulb types:** Non-Premier headlamps and several LS/LT rear, back-up and license-plate lamps are replaceable bulbs; Premier headlamps and selected LT/Premier rear lamps are LED assemblies. Exact trade numbers are not published and need research — Owner's Manual, pp. 243–246.[3]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the oil-life system; change within 600 mi (1,000 km) of the message and at least annually. If accidentally reset, service within 3,000 mi (5,000 km) of the last service — Owner's Manual, pp. 231, 301.[3]
- **Brake fluid:** Every 5 years — Owner's Manual, p. 302.[3]
- **Engine air filter:** Follow the filter-life messages; severe dust may require earlier service — Owner's Manual, pp. 232–233, 301–302.[3]
- **Cabin air filter:** Every 22,500 mi (36,000 km) or 24 months, with shorter intervals under the listed environmental conditions — Owner's Manual, p. 301.[3]
- **Coolant:** Drain and fill at 150,000 mi (240,000 km) or 6 years, whichever comes first — Owner's Manual, p. 301.[3]
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Severe service: change CVT fluid and filter every 45,000 mi (72,000 km). No rear-axle or transfer-case interval applies to this FWD configuration — Owner's Manual, p. 302.[3]
- **Spark plugs:** 60,000 mi (96,000 km); extreme service may require more frequent replacement — Owner's Manual, pp. 301–302.[3]

## Variant warnings

- This sheet applies only to the 1.5L LFV, CVT and FWD configuration; VIN code T identifies the engine in the owner-manual table — Owner's Manual, p. 308.[3]
- EPA vehicle 48015 independently identifies the 2025 Malibu as a 1.5L turbo four-cylinder, variable-ratio automatic, FWD regular-gasoline vehicle.[7]
- Lamp service differs by LS, LT and Premier equipment; confirm the actual lamp assembly before ordering parts.[3]
- Tire pressure always comes from the actual vehicle placard.

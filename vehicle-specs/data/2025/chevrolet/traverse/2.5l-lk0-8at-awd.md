# 2025 Chevrolet Traverse — 2.5L LK0 turbo inline-4, 8-speed automatic, AWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, option/RPO codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** SAE 0W-20 full-synthetic engine oil meeting GM dexos1 — Owner's Manual, pp. 266–268.[1]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 5.5 qt (5.2 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 338.[1]
- **Coolant type/specification:** 50/50 mixture of clean drinkable water and DEX-COOL coolant meeting GM Standard GMW3420 — Owner's Manual, pp. 270–271.[1]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 17.9 qt (16.9 L), entire system — Owner's Manual, p. 338.[1]
- **Transmission fluid:** DEXRON-VI automatic transmission fluid — Owner's Manual, p. 335.[1]
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Needs research; the owner manual does not publish an 8-speed service-fill quantity.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** GM-approved DOT 4 hydraulic brake fluid — Owner's Manual, pp. 276–277, 335.[1]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Product and capacity need current VIN-specific GM service information; the owner manual states no normal maintenance is required unless there is a leak or unusual noise — Owner's Manual, p. 278.[1]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; no separate front-final-drive product or service quantity is published in the owner manual.[1]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Product and capacity need current VIN-specific GM service information; the owner manual directs rear-axle fluid selection to the dealer — Owner's Manual, p. 335.[1]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 140 lb-ft (190 N·m) — Owner's Manual, p. 338.[1]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire and Loading Information label/vehicle placard for the installed tire package; the physical placard is the authority — Owner's Manual, pp. 176, 296–297.[1]
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate every 7,500 mi (12,000 km), use the illustrated pattern, exclude the compact spare, reset pressures to the placard and relearn TPMS positions — Owner's Manual, pp. 301, 330–331.[1]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research; follow the engine-air-filter life messages and reset the life system after replacement — Owner's Manual, pp. 268–270, 331.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research; replace every 22,500 mi (36,000 km) or 24 months, sooner under the listed environmental conditions — Owner's Manual, p. 331.[1]
- **Oil filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research.[1]
- **Battery specification:** Absorbent Glass Mat (AGM) 12-volt battery; exact replacement number, group and CCA require the original battery label/current parts information — Owner's Manual, p. 277.[1]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Exact plug part number needs research; factory gap 0.026–0.030 in (0.65–0.75 mm), and re-gapping is not recommended — Owner's Manual, p. 339.[1]
- **Exterior bulb types:** All exterior lamps use LED light sources in non-serviceable lamp assemblies; no incandescent exterior bulb trade number is listed — Owner's Manual, p. 281.[1]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the oil-life system; change within 600 mi (1,000 km) of the message and at least annually. If accidentally reset, service within 3,000 mi (5,000 km) of the last change — Owner's Manual, pp. 268, 331.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Every 5 years — Owner's Manual, p. 332.[1]
- **Engine air filter:** Follow the filter-life messages; severe dust or environmental conditions may require earlier service — Owner's Manual, pp. 268–270, 331.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Every 22,500 mi (36,000 km) or 24 months, whichever comes first; shorter intervals may be required — Owner's Manual, p. 331.[1]
- **Coolant:** Drain and fill at 150,000 mi (240,000 km) or 6 years, whichever comes first — Owner's Manual, p. 331.[1]
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Severe service: automatic-transmission fluid every 45,000 mi (72,000 km) and rear-axle fluid every 75,000 mi (120,000 km). Normal service: rear-axle fluid at 150,000 mi (240,000 km). No fixed normal transfer-case interval is published — Owner's Manual, pp. 278, 331–332.[1]
- **Spark plugs:** 60,000 mi (96,000 km); extreme conditions may require more frequent replacement — Owner's Manual, pp. 331–332.[1]

## Variant warnings

- This sheet is only for VIN code S, 2.5L LK0, 8-speed automatic and AWD. The FWD variant does not use this transfer-case/rear-axle service path — Owner's Manual, pp. 278, 331–339.[1]
- EPA vehicle 48117 identifies this exact variant as a 2.5L turbo four-cylinder, 8-speed automatic, AWD, Stop/Start, regular-gasoline vehicle.[2]
- Confirm transfer-case and rear-axle products and service quantities in current VIN-specific GM service information before service.[1]
- Tire pressure always comes from the actual vehicle placard.

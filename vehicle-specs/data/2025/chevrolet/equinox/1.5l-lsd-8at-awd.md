# 2025 Chevrolet Equinox — 1.5L LSD turbo inline-4, 8-speed automatic, AWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, option/RPO codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** SAE 0W-20 full-synthetic engine oil meeting GM dexos1 — Owner's Manual, pp. 239–240.[1]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 5.3 qt (5.0 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 307.[1]
- **Coolant type/specification:** 50/50 clean drinkable water and DEX-COOL coolant — Owner's Manual, p. 304.[1]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 6.4 qt (6.1 L), entire system — Owner's Manual, pp. 307–308.[1]
- **Transmission fluid:** DEXRON-VI automatic transmission fluid — Owner's Manual, p. 304.[1]
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Needs research; the owner manual does not publish an 8-speed service-fill quantity.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** GM-approved DOT 4 hydraulic brake fluid from a clean, sealed container — Owner's Manual, pp. 249, 304.[1]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** GM Transfer Case Fluid; exact current product/part specification needs VIN-specific confirmation. Approximate capacity 0.53 qt (0.5 L) — Owner's Manual, pp. 304, 307.[1]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; the owner manual does not publish a separate front-differential fluid specification or capacity.[1]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Product and capacity need research; the owner manual directs rear-axle fluid selection to the dealer/current service information — Owner's Manual, p. 304.[1]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 100 lb-ft (140 N·m) — Owner's Manual, p. 308.[1]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire and Loading Information label/vehicle placard for the installed tire package; the physical placard is the authority — Owner's Manual, pp. 266, 273.[1]
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate every 7,500 mi (12,000 km), use the illustrated pattern, exclude the compact spare, reset pressures to the placard and relearn TPMS positions — Owner's Manual, pp. 273, 298–300.[1]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research; replace according to the engine-air-filter life messages and reset the life system — Owner's Manual, pp. 241–242, 300.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research; replace every 22,500 mi (36,000 km) or 24 months, sooner for heavy traffic, poor air quality, dust, allergens, reduced airflow, fogging or odor — Owner's Manual, p. 300.[1]
- **Oil filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research.[1]
- **Battery specification:** AGM 12-volt battery; use the replacement number on the original battery label. Group size and CCA need research — Owner's Manual, p. 249.[1]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Exact plug part number needs research; factory gap 0.024–0.028 in (0.60–0.70 mm), and re-gapping is not recommended — Owner's Manual, p. 308.[1]
- **Exterior bulb types:** The owner manual identifies several LED lamps and directs LED-assembly replacement to the dealer, but does not publish a complete trade-number inventory; exact replaceable-bulb versus LED mapping needs research — Owner's Manual, p. 253.[1]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the oil-life system; change within 600 mi (1,000 km) of the message and at least annually. If accidentally reset, service within 3,000 mi (5,000 km) of the last service — Owner's Manual, pp. 240, 300.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Every 5 years — Owner's Manual, p. 301.[1]
- **Engine air filter:** Follow the filter-life messages; severe dust may require earlier service — Owner's Manual, pp. 241–242, 300–301.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Every 22,500 mi (36,000 km) or 24 months, with shorter intervals under the listed environmental conditions — Owner's Manual, p. 300.[1]
- **Coolant:** Drain and fill at 150,000 mi (240,000 km) or 6 years, whichever comes first — Owner's Manual, p. 300.[1]
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Severe service: change 8-speed automatic-transmission fluid every 45,000 mi (72,000 km) and rear-axle fluid every 75,000 mi (120,000 km). Normal service: rear-axle fluid at 150,000 mi (240,000 km). No fixed transfer-case interval is published — Owner's Manual, pp. 300–301.[1]
- **Spark plugs:** 60,000 mi (96,000 km); extreme service may require more frequent replacement — Owner's Manual, pp. 300–301.[1]

## Variant warnings

- This sheet is only for the 1.5L LSD engine, 8-speed automatic and AWD configuration. The FWD vehicle uses a CVT, a smaller engine-oil quantity and no AWD rear-axle/transfer-case service path — Owner's Manual, pp. 304, 307–308.[1]
- EPA vehicle 48089 independently identifies the 2025 Equinox AWD as a 1.5L turbo four-cylinder with an 8-speed automatic and regular gasoline.[6]
- Confirm the transfer-case and rear-axle products in current VIN-specific GM service information before service; the owner manual does not fully specify the rear-axle product.[1]
- Tire pressure always comes from the actual vehicle placard.

# 2025 Toyota Highlander Hybrid — 2.5L hybrid, eCVT, AWD

> Verify the VIN, A25A-FXS engine, AWD hybrid configuration, trim/wheel package, tire placard, seating/body configuration, nickel-metal-hydride traction battery, installed 12-volt battery, lamp package and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** ILSAC GF-6B multigrade engine oil; SAE 0W-16 recommended. SAE 0W-20 is a temporary substitute and must be replaced with 0W-16 at the next oil change — Owner's Manual, pp. 494–495.[2]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.5 US qt (4.3 L; 3.8 Imp. qt.); without filter 4.2 US qt (4.0 L; 3.5 Imp. qt.) — Owner's Manual, p. 494.[2]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Toyota Super Long Life Coolant or equivalent high-quality ethylene-glycol coolant using long-life hybrid organic-acid technology and containing no silicate, amine, nitrite or borate — Owner's Manual, p. 495.[2]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** Gasoline-engine circuit 10.5 US qt (9.9 L; 8.7 Imp. qt.); power-control-unit circuit 2.0 US qt (1.9 L; 1.7 Imp. qt.) — Owner's Manual, p. 495.[2]
- **Hybrid transmission fluid:** Toyota Genuine ATF WS; 4.2 US qt (4.0 L; 3.5 Imp. qt.) reference capacity. Routine drain-and-fill quantity is **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, p. 496.[2]
- **Brake fluid:** SAE J1703 / FMVSS No. 116 DOT 3 or SAE J1704 / FMVSS No. 116 DOT 4; service capacity is not published — Owner's Manual, p. 497.[2]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable; AWD is provided by the rear electric motor rather than a conventional transfer case.[2]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** No separate service specification is published; the front final drive is integrated with the hybrid transaxle. Independent quantity is **Needs research**.[2]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Toyota Genuine ATF WS; 1.8 US qt (1.7 L; 1.5 Imp. qt.) reference capacity for the rear electric motor/differential. Routine drain-and-fill quantity remains **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, p. 496.[2]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 76 ft-lb (103 N·m; 10.5 kgf·m) for listed 18- and 20-inch packages — Owner's Manual, pp. 497–498.[2]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire and Loading Information label on the actual vehicle. The manual lists 36 psi front/rear for documented 18- and 20-inch packages; the physical placard remains authoritative — Owner's Manual, pp. 497–498.[2]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 5,000 miles/6 months. The owner manual provides a diagram, but the extracted text does not safely preserve the directional pattern — Owner's Manual, tire section; Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 36–45.[2][4]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles normally; inspect more often under dirt/dust conditions. Exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[4]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles; heavy traffic, urban, desert, dusty or dirt-road use may shorten life. Exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[4]
- **Oil filter:** Replace at each scheduled oil change; exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[4]
- **12-volt battery:** Central-degassing LN2 battery, at least 55 Ah (20HR) and 345 A CCA, with a handle and correctly connected exhaust hose/plug; the manual also publishes a 12.0-V-or-higher open-voltage check and 5-A maximum charging rate — Owner's Manual, pp. 483 and 496.[2]
- **Traction battery:** Nickel-metal hydride, 40 modules at 7.2 V, 6.5 Ah (3HR), nominal 288 V — Owner's Manual, p. 494.[2]
- **Spark plugs:** DENSO FC16HR-Q8 iridium; 0.031 in. (0.8 mm) gap. Do not adjust — Owner's Manual, p. 496.[2]
- **Replaceable exterior bulbs:** Front side marker 5 W, front turn signal 7444NA 28 W, rear turn signal 21 W, back-up 16 W, license-plate 5 W and parking light 7444NA 8 W are listed; other exterior positions may be LED assemblies and require installed-equipment confirmation — Owner's Manual, p. 498 and bulb-replacement section.[2]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Normal maximum 10,000 miles/12 months; 5,000 miles/6 months for primarily dirt/dusty-road use or repeated sub-five-mile freezing-weather trips — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[4]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 5,000 miles/6 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[4]
- **Brake fluid:** Inspect level/condition with scheduled fluid checks; no routine replacement interval is published in the inspected guide — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[4]
- **Engine and cabin air filters:** Replace every 30,000 miles; inspect or replace earlier for qualifying dusty conditions — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[4]
- **HV battery cooling intake filter:** Visually inspect every 5,000 miles and clean if necessary; clean every 20,000 miles — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[4]
- **Engine coolant:** First replace at 100,000 miles/120 months, then every 50,000 miles/60 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 40–45.[4]
- **Inverter/power-control-unit coolant:** First replace at 150,000 miles/180 months, then every 50,000 miles/60 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 42–45.[4]
- **Hybrid transmission fluid:** Inspect at listed chart points; replace under qualifying towing/car-top-carrier/heavy-loading use at specified 60,000-mile multiples. Routine drain quantity remains **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[4]
- **Rear electric differential fluid:** Inspect at listed chart points; replace under qualifying towing/car-top-carrier/heavy-loading use at specified 60,000-mile multiples. Routine drain quantity remains **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[4]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 120,000 miles — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 42–45.[4]

## Variant warnings

- **Official-source conflict:** Toyota's 2025 owner manual specifies a 288-V nickel-metal-hydride traction battery, while EPA IDs 48981 and 48979 report `259V Li-Ion` in the electric-motor field. Do not use the traction-battery chemistry or EPA-record mapping without VIN/model-code confirmation and cleaner Toyota applicability evidence.[2][14][15]
- EPA lists Highlander Hybrid AWD under IDs 48981 and 48979, separating standard and Limited/Platinum groupings; confirm trim/wheel package and physical tire placard.[14][15]
- Do not use gasoline Highlander transmission, coolant, spark-plug, transfer-case or differential specifications.[1][2]
- Confirm installed 12-volt battery, seating/body configuration and bulb-versus-LED equipment.[2]

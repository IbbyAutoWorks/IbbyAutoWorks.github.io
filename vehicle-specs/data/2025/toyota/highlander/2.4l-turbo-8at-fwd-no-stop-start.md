# 2025 Toyota Highlander — 2.4L turbo, 8AT, FWD, no Stop & Start

> Verify the VIN, T24A-FTS engine, FWD configuration, absence of Stop & Start equipment, tire placard, wheel/tire package, seating/body configuration, installed battery, lamp package and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** ILSAC GF-6A multigrade engine oil; SAE 0W-20 recommended. SAE 5W-20 is a temporary substitute and must be replaced with 0W-20 at the next oil change — Owner's Manual, p. 474.[1]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 5.6 US qt (5.3 L; 4.7 Imp. qt.); without filter 5.3 US qt (5.0 L; 4.4 Imp. qt.) — Owner's Manual, p. 474.[1]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Toyota Super Long Life Coolant or equivalent high-quality ethylene-glycol coolant using long-life hybrid organic-acid technology and containing no silicate, amine, nitrite or borate — Owner's Manual, p. 475.[1]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** Gasoline-engine circuit without Stop & Start 11.7 US qt (11.1 L; 9.8 Imp. qt.); intercooler circuit 3.3 US qt (3.1 L; 2.7 Imp. qt.) — Owner's Manual, p. 475.[1]
- **Automatic-transmission fluid:** Toyota Genuine ATF WS; 7.7 US qt (7.3 L; 6.4 Imp. qt.) reference capacity. Routine drain-and-fill quantity is **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, p. 476.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** SAE J1703 / FMVSS No. 116 DOT 3 or SAE J1704 / FMVSS No. 116 DOT 4; service capacity is not published — Owner's Manual, p. 477.[1]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to FWD.[1]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** No separate service specification is published; the front final drive is integrated with the automatic transaxle. Independent quantity is **Needs research**.[1]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to FWD.[1]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 76 ft-lb (103 N·m; 10.5 kgf·m) for listed 18- and 20-inch packages — Owner's Manual, pp. 477–479.[1]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire and Loading Information label on the actual vehicle. The manual lists 36 psi front/rear for the documented 18- and 20-inch packages; the physical placard remains authoritative — Owner's Manual, pp. 477–479.[1]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 5,000 miles/6 months. The owner manual provides a diagram, but the extracted text does not safely preserve the directional pattern — Owner's Manual, tire section; Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 36–43.[1][3]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles normally; inspect more often under dirt/dust conditions. Exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[3]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles; heavy traffic, urban, desert, dusty or dirt-road use may shorten life. Exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[3]
- **Oil filter:** Replace at each scheduled oil change; exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[3]
- **Battery specification:** Central-degassing LN3 battery, at least 70 Ah (20HR) and 603 A CCA; the manual also publishes a 12.3-V-or-higher open-voltage check and 5-A maximum charging rate. Confirm the installed battery and venting arrangement — Owner's Manual, pp. 464–465 and 476.[1]
- **Spark plugs:** NGK DILZKAR8E7H iridium; 0.028 in. (0.7 mm) gap. Do not adjust — Owner's Manual, p. 475.[1]
- **Replaceable exterior bulbs:** Front side marker 5 W, front turn signal 7444NA 28 W, rear turn signal 21 W, back-up 16 W, license-plate 5 W and parking light 7444NA 8 W are listed; other exterior positions may be LED assemblies and require installed-equipment confirmation — Owner's Manual, p. 479 and bulb-replacement section.[1]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Normal maximum 10,000 miles/12 months; 5,000 miles/6 months for primarily dirt/dusty-road use or repeated sub-five-mile freezing-weather trips — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[3]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 5,000 miles/6 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[3]
- **Brake fluid:** Inspect level/condition with scheduled fluid checks; no routine replacement interval is published in the inspected guide — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[3]
- **Engine and cabin air filters:** Replace every 30,000 miles; inspect or replace earlier for qualifying dusty conditions — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[3]
- **Engine/intercooler coolant:** First replace at 100,000 miles/120 months, then every 50,000 miles/60 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 40–43.[3]
- **Automatic-transmission fluid:** Inspect at listed chart points; replace under qualifying towing/heavy-loading service and under the guide's extensive-idling/low-speed fleet-use condition at specified chart points. Exact routine drain quantity remains **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[3]
- **Transfer case/rear differential:** Not applicable to FWD.[1]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace every 40,000 miles — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[3]

## Variant warnings

- This sheet is only for EPA ID 48913, which identifies FWD without Stop & Start. Do not use the Stop-&-Start sheet's 12.3-US-qt engine-coolant value.[1][12]
- Confirm tire package, physical placard, seating/body configuration, installed battery and bulb-versus-LED equipment.[1]

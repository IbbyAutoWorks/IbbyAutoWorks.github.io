# 2025 Toyota Tacoma — 2.4L turbo, 8AT, RWD, Stop & Start, EPA 48871

> Verify the VIN, EPA vehicle ID grouping, T24A-FTS engine, 8-speed automatic, RWD, Stop & Start equipment, model/certification code, trim/package, tire placard, wheel material, installed battery, lamp package and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** ILSAC GF-6A multigrade engine oil; SAE 0W-20 recommended. SAE 5W-20 is a temporary substitute and must be replaced with 0W-20 at the next oil change — Owner's Manual, pp. 613–614.[1]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 5.9 US qt (5.6 L; 4.9 Imp. qt.); without filter 5.6 US qt (5.3 L; 4.7 Imp. qt.) — Owner's Manual, p. 613.[1]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Toyota Super Long Life Coolant or equivalent high-quality ethylene-glycol coolant using long-life hybrid organic-acid technology and containing no silicate, amine, nitrite or borate — Owner's Manual, p. 614.[1]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** Exact model-code mapping remains **Needs research**. The owner manual lists 12.6 US qt (11.9 L) for TZNA41L-PRZSTA, TZNA41L-CRZSTA, TZNA46L-PRZSTA and TZNA46L-CRZSTA, and 12.8 US qt (12.1 L) for other listed gasoline configurations — Owner's Manual, pp. 614–615.[1]
- **Automatic-transmission fluid:** Toyota Genuine ATF WS; 12.2 US qt (11.5 L; 10.1 Imp. qt.) reference capacity. Routine service quantity is **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, p. 615.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** FMVSS No. 116 DOT 3 / SAE J1703 or DOT 4 / SAE J1704; service capacity is not published — Owner's Manual, p. 617.[1]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this EPA-confirmed rear-wheel-drive configuration.[1]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this EPA-confirmed rear-wheel-drive configuration.[1]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Toyota Genuine Differential Gear Oil LT 75W-85 GL-5 or equivalent; 5.0 US qt (4.8 L; 4.2 Imp. qt.) for manual-transmission, TRD Sport or TRD Off-Road packages; otherwise 4.8 US qt (4.5 L; 4.0 Imp. qt.). Exact package mapping is **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, p. 617.[1]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** Aluminum wheels 97 ft-lb (131 N·m); steel wheels 154 ft-lb (209 N·m) where listed. Some package tables list only 97 ft-lb; match wheel material and package — Owner's Manual, pp. 618–620.[1]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Check the Tire and Loading Information label on the actual vehicle. The manual publishes package-dependent values from 30 to 35 psi for road tires plus separate spare values; the placard is authoritative — Owner's Manual, pp. 618–620.[1]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 5,000 miles/6 months. The owner manual's diagram must be visually matched to tire/spare configuration before restating a pattern — Owner's Manual, tire section; Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[1][3]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles normally; inspect more often for dirt/dust operation. Exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[3]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles; heavy traffic, urban, desert, dusty or dirt-road use may shorten life. Exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[3]
- **Oil filter:** Replace with each scheduled oil change; exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[3]
- **12-volt battery:** The owner manual publishes an open-voltage check (12.3 V or higher after the specified headlamp load) and 5-A maximum charging rate, but not a safe extracted replacement group/CCA specification. Confirm the installed battery/VIN — Owner's Manual, p. 615.[1]

- **Spark plugs:** NGK DILZKAR8E7H iridium; 0.027 in. (0.7 mm) gap. Do not adjust — Owner's Manual, p. 615.[1]
- **Replaceable exterior bulbs:** 7444NA 28/8 W front turn-signal/parking bulbs are listed; other exterior positions may be LED assemblies and require installed-equipment confirmation — Owner's Manual, p. 620 and bulb-replacement section.[1]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Normal maximum 10,000 miles/12 months; 5,000 miles/6 months for primarily dirt/dusty-road use or repeated sub-five-mile freezing-weather trips — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[3]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 5,000 miles/6 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[3]
- **Brake fluid:** Inspect level/condition with scheduled fluid checks; no routine replacement interval is published in the inspected guide — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[3]
- **Engine and cabin air filters:** Replace every 30,000 miles; inspect/replace earlier for dusty conditions or cabin-filter loading — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[3]
- **Engine/intercooler coolant:** First replace at 100,000 miles/120 months, then every 50,000 miles/60 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 40–43.[3]
- **Automatic-transmission fluid:** Inspect at the charted intervals; replace under qualifying towing/heavy-loading service, with automatic fluid also covered for qualifying extensive-idling/low-speed fleet use. Exact routine service quantity remains **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[3]
- **Transfer/front/rear differential fluids:** Not applicable to transfer/front differential on this RWD sheet; rear oil remains subject to inspection and qualifying severe-service replacement. — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[3]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 40,000 miles and repeat at the charted interval — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[3]

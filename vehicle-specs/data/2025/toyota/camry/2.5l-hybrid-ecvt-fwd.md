# 2025 Toyota Camry — 2.5L hybrid, eCVT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, AXVH80L model code, installed tire size, tire placard, lamp package and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Toyota Genuine Motor Oil or equivalent meeting JASO GLV-1; SAE 0W-8 is recommended. SAE 0W-16 may be used only when 0W-8 is unavailable and must be replaced with 0W-8 at the next oil change — Owner's Manual, pp. 486–487.[1]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.5 US qt (4.3 L; 3.8 Imp. qt.); without filter 4.2 US qt (4.0 L; 3.5 Imp. qt.) — Owner's Manual, p. 486.[1]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Toyota Super Long Life Coolant or a similar high-quality ethylene-glycol coolant using long-life hybrid organic-acid technology and containing no silicate, amine, nitrite or borate — Owner's Manual, p. 488.[1]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** Gasoline-engine circuit 6.4 US qt (6.1 L; 5.4 Imp. qt.); power-control-unit circuit 1.9 US qt (1.8 L; 1.6 Imp. qt.) — Owner's Manual, p. 488.[1]
- **Hybrid transmission fluid:** Toyota Genuine e-Transaxle Fluid TE; reference capacity 3.88 US qt (3.67 L; 3.23 Imp. qt.). Toyota directs owners to a dealer when replacement is necessary — Owner's Manual, p. 489.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** FMVSS No. 116 DOT 3 / SAE J1703 or DOT 4 / SAE J1704; routine replacement capacity is not published — Owner's Manual, p. 489.[1]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD configuration.[1]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** No separate service specification is published; the front final drive is within the hybrid transaxle. Treat any independent quantity as **Needs research**.[1]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD configuration.[1]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 76 ft-lb (103 N·m; 10.5 kgf·m) — Owner's Manual, pp. 490–491.[1]
- **Cold tire pressure:** The actual vehicle's Tire and Loading Information label is authoritative. The manual lists 35 psi front/rear for 18- and 19-inch packages and either 35 psi or 38 psi front/rear for the two listed 16-inch package types; do not choose between the 16-inch values without the placard — Owner's Manual, pp. 490–491.[1]
- **Tire rotation:** Rotate at every 5,000-mile/6-month scheduled service. The owner manual supplies a diagram and requires wheel-position registration after rotation; the diagram's exact directional pattern remains **Needs visual shop confirmation** before use — Owner's Manual, p. 396; Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[1][2]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Replace at 30,000 miles/36 months under the normal schedule; inspect more frequently for primarily dirt/dusty/muddy-road or melted-snow-road operation. Exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 40–49.[2]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles/36 months; heavy traffic, dirt roads and urban/desert/dusty operation can shorten life. Exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 40–49.[2]
- **Oil filter:** Replace with every scheduled oil change; exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 40–49.[2]
- **Battery:** 12-volt battery charging rate is 5 A maximum and open voltage should be 12.0 V or higher at 68°F after the manual's preparation procedure. Group size, CCA and reserve capacity are **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, p. 488.[1]
- **Spark plugs:** DENSO FC16HR-Q8 iridium plugs; 0.031 in. (0.8 mm) gap. Toyota says not to adjust the gap — Owner's Manual, p. 488.[1]
- **Replaceable bulbs:** Front turn-signal/parking bulb 7444NA (28/8 W), rear turn-signal bulb WY21W (21 W), and back-up bulb W16W (16 W) when the vehicle has the bulb-type assemblies. The manual identifies the remaining exterior lamps, including LED-type alternatives for those positions, as dealer-replaced LED assemblies — Owner's Manual, pp. 418–421 and 492.[1]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Normal maximum is 10,000 miles/12 months. Replace at 5,000 miles/6 months when the majority of driving is under the guide's special conditions, including dirt/dusty/muddy or melted-snow roads and qualifying towing, car-top-carrier or heavy-loading operation — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[2]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 5,000 miles/6 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[2]
- **Brake fluid:** Inspect fluid level and condition at scheduled services; no routine time/mileage replacement interval is published in the inspected guide — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 40–49.[2]
- **Engine air filter:** Replace at 30,000 miles/36 months; inspect more often under listed severe road conditions — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 40–49.[2]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace at 30,000 miles/36 months; replace more frequently when operating conditions shorten filter life — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 40–49.[2]
- **Engine coolant:** First replace at 100,000 miles/120 months, then every 50,000 miles/60 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 43–45.[2]
- **Power-control-unit/inverter coolant:** First replace at 150,000 miles/180 months, then every 50,000 miles/60 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 44–45.[2]
- **Hybrid transmission fluid:** Inspect at the guide's scheduled intervals. Under qualifying towing, car-top-carrier or heavy-loading use, replace at 60,000 miles/72 months and again at 120,000 miles/144 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 40–45.[2]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 120,000 miles/144 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 44–45.[2]

## Variant warnings

- This sheet is limited to the AXVH80L front-wheel-drive model-code family shown in the exact 2025 owner manual; AXVH85L AWD has a separate rear electric motor and rear differential specification.[1]
- Confirm the installed 16-, 18- or 19-inch tire package and use the driver's-door placard rather than selecting pressure from this sheet.[1]
- Confirm whether each turn-signal/parking or back-up position uses the replaceable-bulb or LED assembly before ordering parts.[1]

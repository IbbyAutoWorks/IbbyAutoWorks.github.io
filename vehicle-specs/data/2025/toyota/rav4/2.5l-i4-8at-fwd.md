# 2025 Toyota RAV4 — 2.5L gasoline, 8-speed automatic, FWD

> Verify the VIN, A25A-FKS engine, front-wheel-drive configuration, oil-cooler equipment, battery type, tire package, tire placard, lamp package and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** ILSAC GF-6B multigrade oil; SAE 0W-16 is recommended. SAE 0W-20 may be used only when 0W-16 is unavailable and must be replaced with 0W-16 at the next oil change — Owner's Manual, pp. 498–499.[3]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.5 US qt (4.3 L; 3.8 Imp. qt.); without filter 4.2 US qt (4.0 L; 3.5 Imp. qt.) — Owner's Manual, p. 498.[3]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Toyota Super Long Life Coolant or equivalent high-quality ethylene-glycol coolant using long-life hybrid organic-acid technology and containing no silicate, amine, nitrite or borate — Owner's Manual, p. 499.[3]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 7.3 US qt (6.9 L; 6.1 Imp. qt.) without engine-oil cooler or 7.4 US qt (7.0 L; 6.2 Imp. qt.) with engine-oil cooler. Confirm installed equipment before filling — Owner's Manual, p. 499.[3]
- **Automatic-transmission fluid:** Toyota Genuine ATF WS; reference total capacity 7.8 US qt (7.4 L; 6.5 Imp. qt.). Toyota directs owners to a dealer when replacement is necessary; a routine drain/fill quantity is **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, p. 500.[3]
- **Brake fluid:** FMVSS No. 116 DOT 3 / SAE J1703 or DOT 4 / SAE J1704; service capacity is not published — Owner's Manual, p. 502.[3]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD configuration.[3]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** No separate service specification is published; the front final drive is within the automatic transaxle. Treat an independent quantity as **Needs research**.[3]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD configuration.[3]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 76 ft-lb (103 N·m; 10.5 kgf·m) for every listed 17-, 18- and 19-inch package — Owner's Manual, pp. 503–507.[3]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire and Loading Information label on the actual vehicle. The manual lists multiple 17-inch type values from 33 to 36 psi and 18-/19-inch values from 33 to 35 psi; do not select among them without the placard and installed package — Owner's Manual, pp. 503–507 and tire-label procedure on p. 417.[3]
- **Tire rotation:** Rotate at every 5,000-mile/6-month scheduled service and initialize/update TPWS position information afterward. The rotation diagram must be visually confirmed before stating a directional pattern — Owner's Manual, pp. 408–412; Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 36–43.[3][4]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Replace at 30,000-mile intervals under the normal chart; inspect more frequently for primarily dirt/dusty-road operation. Exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[4]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles; heavy traffic, dirt roads, urban, desert or dusty use may require earlier replacement. Exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[4]
- **Oil filter:** Replace with each scheduled oil change; exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[4]
- **Battery:** The manual lists equipment-dependent replacement minimums: Type A LN3, 65 Ah/603 A CCA; Type B LN2, 60 Ah/360 A CCA; or Type C LN2, 60 Ah/563 A CCA. Exact Type A/B/C mapping is **Needs research** and must be confirmed from the installed battery/VIN — Owner's Manual, pp. 489–490.[3]
- **Spark plugs:** DENSO FC16HR-Q8 iridium; 0.031 in. (0.8 mm) gap. Do not adjust the gap — Owner's Manual, p. 500.[3]
- **Replaceable exterior bulbs:** Depending on installed lamp type: W5W front side marker, 7444NA front turn-signal/parking or turn-signal, WY21W rear turn signal, W16W back-up and 5 W outer foot light. Other listed exterior positions are dealer-replaced LED assemblies — Owner's Manual, pp. 432–438 and 507–508.[3]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Normal maximum 10,000 miles/12 months; 5,000 miles/6 months when the majority of driving meets Toyota's listed special conditions, including dirt/dusty roads, towing/car-top-carrier/heavy loading, extensive idling/low-speed commercial use, or repeated sub-five-mile freezing-weather trips — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 36–43 and 44–46.[4]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 5,000 miles/6 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 36–43.[4]
- **Brake fluid:** Inspect level/condition at scheduled services; no routine replacement interval is published in the inspected guide — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[4]
- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles; inspect more often under dirt/dusty-road conditions — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[4]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles; shorten interval when operating conditions reduce filter life — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[4]
- **Engine coolant:** First replace at 100,000 miles/120 months, then every 50,000 miles/60 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 40–43.[4]
- **Automatic-transmission fluid:** Inspect at 30,000-mile chart points. The guide calls for replacement under qualifying towing/heavy-loading and extensive-idling/low-speed use at selected 60,000-mile chart points; verify the exact row before service — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[4]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 120,000 miles/144 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 41–43.[4]

## Variant warnings

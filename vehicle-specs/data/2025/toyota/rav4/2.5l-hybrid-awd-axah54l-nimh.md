# 2025 Toyota RAV4 Hybrid — 2.5L hybrid, AWD, AXAH54L NiMH

> Verify the VIN, AXAH54L model code, A25A-FXS engine, nickel-metal-hydride traction battery, tire placard, lamp package and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** ILSAC GF-6B multigrade oil; SAE 0W-16 recommended. SAE 0W-20 is a temporary substitute only and must be replaced with 0W-16 at the next oil change — Hybrid Owner's Manual, pp. 498–499.[6]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.5 US qt (4.3 L; 3.8 Imp. qt.); without filter 4.2 US qt (4.0 L; 3.5 Imp. qt.) — Hybrid Owner's Manual, p. 498.[6]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Toyota Super Long Life Coolant or equivalent high-quality ethylene-glycol coolant using long-life hybrid organic-acid technology and containing no silicate, amine, nitrite or borate — Hybrid Owner's Manual, p. 500.[6]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** Gasoline-engine circuit 6.4 US qt (6.1 L; 5.4 Imp. qt.); power-control-unit circuit 1.7 US qt (1.6 L; 1.4 Imp. qt.) — Hybrid Owner's Manual, p. 500.[6]
- **Hybrid transmission fluid:** Toyota Genuine ATF WS; reference capacity 4.1 US qt (3.9 L; 3.4 Imp. qt.). Routine drain/fill quantity is **Needs research** — Hybrid Owner's Manual, p. 501.[6]
- **Brake fluid:** FMVSS No. 116 DOT 3 / SAE J1703 or DOT 4 / SAE J1704; service capacity is not published — Hybrid Owner's Manual, p. 501.[6]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable; AWD propulsion uses a rear electric motor/rear differential rather than a conventional transfer case.[6]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** No separate service specification is published; the front final drive is within the hybrid transaxle. Treat an independent quantity as **Needs research**.[6]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Toyota Genuine ATF WS; reference capacity 1.8 US qt (1.7 L; 1.5 Imp. qt.) — Hybrid Owner's Manual, p. 501.[6]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 76 ft-lb (103 N·m; 10.5 kgf·m) for listed 17- and 18-inch packages — Hybrid Owner's Manual, pp. 502–504.[6]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire and Loading Information label. The manual lists package-dependent 33 or 36 psi values; do not select without the actual placard — Hybrid Owner's Manual, pp. 502–504 and p. 420.[6]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 5,000 miles/6 months; initialize/update TPWS position information afterward. Diagram direction remains **Needs visual confirmation** — Hybrid Owner's Manual, pp. 411–417; Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 36–43.[6][7]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles normally; inspect more often under dirt/dust conditions. Exact part number is **Needs research** — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[7]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles; severe environment can shorten life. Exact part number is **Needs research** — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[7]
- **Oil filter:** Replace at each scheduled oil change; exact part number is **Needs research** — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[7]
- **12-volt battery:** Equipment-dependent replacement minimums are Type A LN1, 45 Ah/285 A CCA; Type B LN1, 45 Ah/286 A CCA; or Type C LN2, 55 Ah/345 A CCA. Exact mapping is **Needs research**; preserve the vent hose/plug arrangement — Hybrid Owner's Manual, pp. 487–489.[6]
- **Traction battery:** AXAH54L uses a nickel-metal-hydride battery, 34 modules at 7.2 V, 6.5 Ah (3HR), nominal 244.8 V — Hybrid Owner's Manual, p. 498.[6]
- **Spark plugs:** DENSO FC16HR-Q8 iridium; 0.031 in. (0.8 mm) gap; do not adjust — Hybrid Owner's Manual, p. 500.[6]
- **Replaceable exterior bulbs:** Package-dependent W5W front side marker, 7444NA front turn-signal/parking or turn-signal, WY21W rear turn signal, W16W back-up and 5 W outer foot light. Remaining listed exterior positions are LED assemblies — Hybrid Owner's Manual, pp. 439–441 and 504.[6]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** 10,000 miles/12 months normally; 5,000 miles/6 months under Toyota's listed dirt/dust, towing/heavy-loading, extensive-idling/low-speed commercial or repeated short freezing-trip conditions — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 36–46.[7]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 5,000 miles/6 months — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 36–43.[7]
- **Brake fluid:** Inspect level/condition; no routine replacement interval is published — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[7]
- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles; inspect more often under dirt/dust conditions — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[7]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles; shorten as operating conditions require — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[7]
- **HV battery cooling intake filter:** Visually inspect every 5,000 miles and clean if necessary; clean every 20,000 miles — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[7]
- **Engine coolant:** First replace at 100,000 miles/120 months, then every 50,000 miles/60 months — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 40–43.[7]
- **Inverter/power-control-unit coolant:** First replace at 150,000 miles/180 months, then every 50,000 miles/60 months — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 42–43.[7]
- **Hybrid transmission and rear differential:** Inspect at 30,000-mile chart points; under qualifying towing/car-top-carrier/heavy-loading use, replace at 60,000 and 120,000 miles — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[7]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 120,000 miles/144 months — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 42–43.[7]

## Variant warnings

- This sheet requires AXAH54L and the nickel-metal-hydride traction battery. AXAL54L lithium-ion is separate.[6]
- Confirm 12-volt battery Type A/B/C, tire package and bulb-versus-LED equipment.[6]

# 2025 Toyota RAV4 — 2.5L gasoline, 8-speed automatic, Dynamic Torque Control AWD

> Verify the VIN, A25A-FKS engine, Dynamic Torque Control AWD system, oil-cooler equipment, battery type, tire placard, lamp package and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** ILSAC GF-6B multigrade oil; SAE 0W-16 recommended. SAE 0W-20 is a temporary substitute only and must be replaced with 0W-16 at the next oil change — Owner's Manual, pp. 498–499.[3]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.5 US qt (4.3 L; 3.8 Imp. qt.); without filter 4.2 US qt (4.0 L; 3.5 Imp. qt.) — Owner's Manual, p. 498.[3]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Toyota Super Long Life Coolant or equivalent high-quality ethylene-glycol coolant using long-life hybrid organic-acid technology and containing no silicate, amine, nitrite or borate — Owner's Manual, p. 499.[3]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 7.3 US qt (6.9 L; 6.1 Imp. qt.) without engine-oil cooler or 7.4 US qt (7.0 L; 6.2 Imp. qt.) with engine-oil cooler; confirm equipment — Owner's Manual, p. 499.[3]
- **Automatic-transmission fluid:** Toyota Genuine ATF WS; reference total capacity 7.8 US qt (7.4 L; 6.5 Imp. qt.). Routine drain/fill quantity is **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, p. 500.[3]
- **Brake fluid:** FMVSS No. 116 DOT 3 / SAE J1703 or DOT 4 / SAE J1704; service capacity is not published — Owner's Manual, p. 502.[3]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Toyota Genuine Differential Gear Oil LT 75W-85 GL-5 or equivalent; reference capacity 0.48 US qt (0.45 L; 0.40 Imp. qt.) — Owner's Manual, pp. 500–501.[3]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** No separate service specification is published; the front final drive is within the automatic transaxle. Treat an independent quantity as **Needs research**.[3]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Toyota Genuine Differential Gear Oil LT 75W-85 GL-5 or equivalent; reference capacity 0.5 US qt (0.5 L; 0.4 Imp. qt.) — Owner's Manual, p. 501.[3]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 76 ft-lb (103 N·m; 10.5 kgf·m) — Owner's Manual, pp. 503–507.[3]
- **Cold tire pressure:** The actual Tire and Loading Information label is authoritative. The manual lists package-dependent values from 33 to 36 psi; do not select without the placard — Owner's Manual, pp. 503–507 and p. 417.[3]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 5,000 miles/6 months; initialize/update TPWS position information afterward. Diagram direction remains **Needs visual confirmation** — Owner's Manual, pp. 408–412; Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 36–43.[3][4]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles normally; inspect more often for primarily dirt/dusty-road use. Exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[4]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles; heavy traffic, dirt, urban, desert or dusty operation may shorten life. Exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[4]
- **Oil filter:** Replace at each scheduled oil change; exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[4]
- **Battery:** Equipment-dependent minimums are Type A LN3, 65 Ah/603 A CCA; Type B LN2, 60 Ah/360 A CCA; or Type C LN2, 60 Ah/563 A CCA. Exact mapping is **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, pp. 489–490.[3]
- **Spark plugs:** DENSO FC16HR-Q8 iridium; 0.031 in. (0.8 mm) gap; do not adjust — Owner's Manual, p. 500.[3]
- **Replaceable exterior bulbs:** Package-dependent W5W front side marker, 7444NA front turn-signal/parking or turn-signal, WY21W rear turn signal, W16W back-up and 5 W outer foot light. Remaining listed exterior positions are LED assemblies — Owner's Manual, pp. 432–438 and 507–508.[3]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** 10,000 miles/12 months normally; 5,000 miles/6 months under Toyota's listed dirt/dust, towing/heavy-loading, extensive-idling/low-speed commercial or repeated short freezing-trip conditions — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 36–46.[4]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 5,000 miles/6 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 36–43.[4]
- **Brake fluid:** Inspect level/condition; no routine replacement interval is published — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[4]
- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles; inspect more often under dirt/dust conditions — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[4]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles; shorten as operating conditions require — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[4]
- **Engine coolant:** First replace at 100,000 miles/120 months, then every 50,000 miles/60 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 40–43.[4]
- **Transmission / transfer / rear differential:** Inspect sealed units at scheduled chart points. The guide adds replacement under qualifying severe use; the precise table cadence should be visually confirmed before service rather than inferred from text extraction — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[4]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 120,000 miles/144 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 41–43.[4]

## Variant warnings

- Confirm **Dynamic Torque Control AWD**. Dynamic Torque Vectoring AWD uses different transfer and rear-differential oil/capacities and has its own sheet.[3]
- Confirm engine-oil-cooler, battery Type A/B/C, tire package and bulb/LED equipment.[3]

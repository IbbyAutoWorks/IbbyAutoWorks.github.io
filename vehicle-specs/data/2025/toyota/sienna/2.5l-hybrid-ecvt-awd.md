# 2025 Toyota Sienna — 2.5L hybrid, eCVT, AWD

> Verify the VIN, A25A-FXS engine, AWD configuration, nickel-metal-hydride traction battery, tire placard, tow-package/equipment, installed 12-volt battery, lamp package and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** ILSAC GF-6B multigrade engine oil; SAE 0W-16 recommended. SAE 0W-20 is a temporary substitute only and must be replaced with 0W-16 at the next oil change — Owner's Manual, pp. 689–690.[5]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.5 US qt (4.3 L; 3.8 Imp. qt.); without filter 4.2 US qt (4.0 L; 3.5 Imp. qt.) — Owner's Manual, pp. 689–690.[5]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Toyota Super Long Life Coolant or equivalent high-quality ethylene-glycol coolant using long-life hybrid organic-acid technology and containing no silicate, amine, nitrite or borate — Owner's Manual, p. 691.[5]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** Gasoline-engine circuit 11.4 US qt (10.8 L; 9.5 Imp. qt.); power-control-unit circuit 2.0 US qt (1.9 L; 1.7 Imp. qt.) — Owner's Manual, p. 691.[5]
- **Hybrid transmission fluid:** Toyota Genuine ATF WS; 4.2 US qt (4.0 L; 3.5 Imp. qt.) reference capacity. Routine drain-and-fill quantity is **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, pp. 691–692.[5]
- **Brake fluid:** FMVSS No. 116 DOT 3 / SAE J1703 or DOT 4 / SAE J1704; service capacity is not published — Owner's Manual, p. 692.[5]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable; the hybrid system does not use a conventional transfer case.[5]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** No separate service specification is published; the front final drive is integrated with the hybrid transaxle. Independent quantity is **Needs research**.[5]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Toyota Genuine ATF WS; 1.8 US qt (1.7 L; 1.5 Imp. qt.) reference capacity. Routine drain-and-fill quantity remains **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, p. 692.[5]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 76 ft-lb (103 N·m; 10.5 kgf·m) for listed 17-, 18- and 20-inch packages — Owner's Manual, pp. 693–694.[5]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire and Loading Information label. The manual lists 35 psi front/rear for 17- and 18-inch packages and 36 psi front/rear for 20-inch packages; the actual placard remains authoritative — Owner's Manual, pp. 693–694.[5]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 5,000 miles/6 months. The owner manual supplies a diagram, but its directional pattern requires visual confirmation before restatement — Owner's Manual, tire section; Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[5][6]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles normally; inspect more often under dirt/dust conditions. Exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[6]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles; heavy traffic, urban, desert, dusty or dirt-road use may shorten life. Exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[6]
- **Oil filter:** Replace at each scheduled oil change; exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[6]
- **12-volt battery:** The owner manual publishes an open-voltage check but not a safe extracted group-size/CCA replacement specification. Confirm the installed battery/VIN; exact replacement specification is **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, p. 691.[5]
- **Traction battery:** Nickel-metal hydride, 40 modules at 7.2 V, 6.5 Ah (3HR), nominal 288 V — Owner's Manual, p. 689.[5]
- **Spark plugs:** DENSO FC16HR-Q8 iridium; 0.031 in. (0.8 mm) gap. Do not adjust — Owner's Manual, p. 691.[5]
- **Replaceable exterior bulbs:** WY21W 21 W rear turn signals, W16W 16 W back-up lights and W5W 5 W license-plate lights are listed; other exterior positions may be LED assemblies and require installed-equipment confirmation — Owner's Manual, pp. 694–695 and bulb-replacement section.[5]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Normal maximum 10,000 miles/12 months; 5,000 miles/6 months for primarily dirt/dusty-road use or repeated sub-five-mile freezing-weather trips. Other special-condition inspections apply for towing/heavy loading — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[6]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 5,000 miles/6 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[6]
- **Brake fluid:** Inspect level/condition with scheduled fluid checks; no routine replacement interval is published in the inspected guide — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[6]
- **Engine and cabin air filters:** Replace every 30,000 miles; inspect/replace earlier as dusty conditions or cabin-filter loading require — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[6]
- **HV battery cooling intake filter:** Visually inspect every 5,000 miles and clean if necessary; clean every 20,000 miles — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[6]
- **Engine coolant:** First replace at 100,000 miles/120 months, then every 50,000 miles/60 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 40–45.[6]
- **Inverter/power-control-unit coolant:** First replace at 150,000 miles/180 months, then every 50,000 miles/60 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 42–45.[6]
- **Hybrid transmission fluid:** Inspect at selected chart points; replace under qualifying towing/car-top-carrier/heavy-loading use at specified 60,000- and 120,000-mile points — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 40–45.[6]
- **Rear differential fluid:** Inspect at selected chart points; replace under qualifying towing/car-top-carrier/heavy-loading use at specified 60,000- and 120,000-mile points — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 40–45.[6]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 120,000 miles/144 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 42–45.[6]

## Variant warnings

- AWD rear electric motor/differential service is required; do not use the FWD sheet.[5]
- Confirm tire package, physical placard, installed 12-volt battery, towing/equipment configuration and bulb-versus-LED equipment.[5]

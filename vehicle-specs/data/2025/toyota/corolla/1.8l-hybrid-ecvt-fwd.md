# 2025 Toyota Corolla Hybrid — 1.8L hybrid, eCVT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, 2ZR-FXE engine, FWD configuration, lithium-ion traction battery, tire placard, installed 12-volt battery, lamp package and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** ILSAC GF-6B multigrade engine oil; SAE 0W-16 recommended. SAE 0W-20 is a temporary substitute only and must be replaced with 0W-16 at the next oil change — Hybrid Owner's Manual, pp. 418–419.[2]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.4 US qt (4.2 L; 3.7 Imp. qt.); without filter 4.1 US qt (3.9 L; 3.4 Imp. qt.) — Hybrid Owner's Manual, p. 418.[2]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Toyota Super Long Life Coolant or equivalent high-quality ethylene-glycol coolant using long-life hybrid organic-acid technology and containing no silicate, amine, nitrite or borate — Hybrid Owner's Manual, p. 420.[2]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** Gasoline-engine circuit 5.9 US qt (5.6 L; 4.9 Imp. qt.); power-control-unit circuit 1.6 US qt (1.5 L; 1.3 Imp. qt.) — Hybrid Owner's Manual, p. 420.[2]
- **Hybrid transmission fluid:** Toyota Genuine e-Transaxle Fluid TE; 3.2 US qt (3.0 L; 2.6 Imp. qt.) reference capacity. Routine drain-and-fill quantity is **Needs research** — Hybrid Owner's Manual, pp. 420–421.[2]
- **Brake fluid:** FMVSS No. 116 DOT 3 / SAE J1703 or DOT 4 / SAE J1704; service capacity is not published — Hybrid Owner's Manual, p. 421.[2]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable; the hybrid system does not use a conventional transfer case.[2]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** No separate service specification is published; the front final drive is integrated with the hybrid transaxle. Independent quantity is **Needs research**.[2]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD configuration.[2]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 76 ft-lb (103 N·m; 10.5 kgf·m) for all listed packages — Hybrid Owner's Manual, pp. 422–424.[2]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire and Loading Information label. The manual lists multiple package-dependent front/rear values from 30 to 38 psi; do not choose among them without the installed tires and actual placard — Hybrid Owner's Manual, pp. 422–424.[2]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 5,000 miles/6 months. The owner manual supplies a diagram, but its directional pattern requires visual confirmation before restatement — Hybrid Owner's Manual, tire section; Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[2][4]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles normally; inspect more often under dirt/dust conditions. Exact part number is **Needs research** — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[4]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles; heavy traffic, urban, desert, dusty or dirt-road use may shorten life. Exact part number is **Needs research** — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[4]
- **Oil filter:** Replace at each scheduled oil change; exact part number is **Needs research** — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[4]
- **12-volt battery:** The manual publishes an open-voltage check but not a safe extracted group-size/CCA replacement specification. Confirm the installed battery/VIN; exact replacement specification is **Needs research** — Hybrid Owner's Manual, p. 420.[2]
- **Traction battery:** Lithium-ion, 56 cells at 3.7 V, 4.08 Ah, nominal 207.2 V — Hybrid Owner's Manual, p. 418.[2]
- **Spark plugs:** DENSO FC16HR-CY9 iridium; 0.035 in. (0.9 mm) gap. Do not adjust — Hybrid Owner's Manual, p. 420.[2]
- **Replaceable exterior bulbs:** Rear side marker 5 W, rear turn signal 21 W and back-up 16 W are listed as replaceable bulbs; other exterior positions may be LED assemblies and require installed-equipment confirmation — Hybrid Owner's Manual, p. 424 and bulb-replacement section.[2]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Normal maximum 10,000 miles/12 months; 5,000 miles/6 months for primarily dirt/dusty-road use or repeated sub-five-mile freezing-weather trips. Other special-condition inspections apply for towing/heavy loading and long extensive-idling/low-speed use — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[4]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 5,000 miles/6 months — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[4]
- **Brake fluid:** Inspect level/condition with scheduled fluid checks; no routine replacement interval is published in the inspected guide — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[4]
- **Engine and cabin air filters:** Replace every 30,000 miles; inspect/replace earlier as dusty conditions or cabin-filter loading require — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[4]
- **HV battery cooling intake filter:** Visually inspect every 5,000 miles and clean if necessary; clean every 20,000 miles — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–45.[4]
- **Engine coolant:** First replace at 100,000 miles/120 months, then every 50,000 miles/60 months — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 40–45.[4]
- **Inverter/power-control-unit coolant:** First replace at 150,000 miles/180 months, then every 50,000 miles/60 months — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 42–45.[4]
- **Hybrid transmission fluid:** Inspect at selected chart points; replace under qualifying towing/car-top-carrier/heavy-loading use at specified 60,000- and 120,000-mile points — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 40–45.[4]
- **Rear differential fluid:** Not applicable to this FWD configuration.[2]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 120,000 miles/144 months — Hybrid Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 42–45.[4]

## Variant warnings

- This FWD sheet has no rear electric motor/differential service; AWD is separate.[2]
- Confirm lithium-ion traction battery identity, tire package, physical placard, installed 12-volt battery and bulb-versus-LED equipment.[2]

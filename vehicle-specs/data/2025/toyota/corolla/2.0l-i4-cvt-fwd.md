# 2025 Toyota Corolla — 2.0L gasoline, CVT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, M20A-FKS engine, sedan body, CVT, tire package, tire placard, installed 12-volt battery, lamp package and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** JASO GLV-1; SAE 0W-8 is recommended. SAE 0W-16 may be used only when 0W-8 is unavailable and must be replaced with 0W-8 at the next oil change — Owner's Manual, pp. 388–389.[1]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.4 US qt (4.2 L; 3.7 Imp. qt.); without filter 4.1 US qt (3.9 L; 3.4 Imp. qt.) — Owner's Manual, p. 388.[1]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Toyota Super Long Life Coolant or equivalent high-quality ethylene-glycol coolant using long-life hybrid organic-acid technology and containing no silicate, amine, nitrite or borate — Owner's Manual, p. 389.[1]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 6.8 US qt (6.4 L; 5.6 Imp. qt.) reference capacity — Owner's Manual, p. 389.[1]
- **CVT fluid:** Toyota Genuine CVT Fluid FE; 9.0 US qt (8.5 L; 7.5 Imp. qt.) is a reference total capacity, not a routine drain-and-fill quantity. Toyota directs owners to a dealer when replacement is necessary — Owner's Manual, p. 390.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** FMVSS No. 116 DOT 3 / SAE J1703 or DOT 4 / SAE J1704; service capacity is not published — Owner's Manual, p. 390.[1]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD configuration.[1]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** No separate service specification is published; the front final drive is integrated with the CVT. Independent service quantity is **Needs research**.[1]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable.[1]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 76 ft-lb (103 N·m; 10.5 kgf·m) for listed 15-, 16- and 18-inch packages — Owner's Manual, pp. 391–392.[1]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire and Loading Information label on the actual vehicle. The manual lists package-dependent front/rear values of 33/30 psi, 35/33 psi or 33/30 psi; do not choose among them without matching the installed tires and placard — Owner's Manual, pp. 391–392.[1]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 5,000 miles/6 months. The owner manual supplies a diagram, but its directional pattern requires visual confirmation before restatement — Owner's Manual, tire section; Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 36–43.[1][3]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles under the normal chart; inspect more often for primarily dirt/dusty-road operation. Exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[3]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles; heavy traffic, urban, desert, dusty or dirt-road use may shorten life. Exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[3]
- **Oil filter:** Replace with each scheduled oil change; exact part number is **Needs research** — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[3]
- **Battery specification:** The owner manual publishes charging/open-voltage checks but not a safe extracted group-size/CCA replacement specification. Confirm the installed battery/VIN; exact replacement specification is **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, p. 389.[1]
- **Spark plugs:** DENSO FC20HR-Q8 iridium; 0.032 in. (0.8 mm) gap. Do not adjust the gap — Owner's Manual, p. 389.[1]
- **Replaceable exterior bulbs:** Package-dependent front side marker 5 W, 7444NA 28/8 W front turn-signal/parking, rear side marker 5 W, rear turn signal 21 W and back-up 16 W; other exterior positions may be LED assemblies and require installed-equipment confirmation — Owner's Manual, p. 392 and bulb-replacement section.[1]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Normal maximum 10,000 miles/12 months; 5,000 miles/6 months when the vehicle is mainly driven on dirt/dusty roads or repeated sub-five-mile freezing-weather trips. The guide also identifies towing/heavy loading and extensive idling/low-speed commercial use as special conditions for additional maintenance — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 36–43.[3]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 5,000 miles/6 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–43.[3]
- **Brake fluid:** Inspect level/condition with scheduled fluid checks; no routine replacement interval is published in the inspected guide — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[3]
- **Engine and cabin air filters:** Replace every 30,000 miles; shorten inspection/replacement based on dusty conditions and cabin-filter loading — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–46.[3]
- **Engine coolant:** First replace at 100,000 miles/120 months, then every 50,000 miles/60 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 40–43.[3]
- **CVT fluid:** Inspect at selected chart points. Under qualifying towing/heavy-loading or extensive-idling/low-speed use, replace at the specified 60,000-mile chart points; exact later repetition should be visually confirmed before service — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 40–43.[3]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 120,000 miles/144 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 42–43.[3]

## Variant warnings

- This sheet is for the gasoline Corolla sedan with M20A-FKS and CVT; it excludes the 1.8L hybrid system and Corolla Hatchback.[1]

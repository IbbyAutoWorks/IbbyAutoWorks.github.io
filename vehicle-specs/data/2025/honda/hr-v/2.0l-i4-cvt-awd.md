# 2025 Honda HR-V — 2.0L inline-4, CVT, AWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, trim, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Honda Motor Oil or API-certified SAE 0W-20 — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[4]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.4 US qt (4.2 L); 4.2 US qt (4.0 L) without filter — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[4]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Honda Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant Type 2 — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[4]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 1.60 US gal (6.04 L) change quantity including the remaining 0.17 US gal (0.65 L) in the reserve tank; 1.74 US gal (6.59 L) total including reserve — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[4]
- **Transmission fluid:** Honda HCF-2 Transmission Fluid — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[4]
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** 3.7 US qt (3.5 L) change quantity for AWD — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[4]
- **Brake fluid:** Honda Heavy Duty Brake Fluid DOT 3 — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[4]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Maintenance Minder sub-item 3 confirms transfer-fluid service for applicable AWD vehicles, but an exact fluid specification and quantity remain **Needs research**.[5]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** No separately labeled front-differential service fill was accepted from the exact-year source; independent quantity is **Needs research**.[4]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Honda DPSF-II; 1.318 US qt (1.247 L) change quantity — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[4]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 80 lbf-ft (108 N·m) — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[4]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Check the label on the driver's doorjamb; the physical vehicle placard controls. Trim- and tire-package-specific reference values remain **Needs research**.[4]
- **Tire rotation:** Rotate when Maintenance Minder sub-item 1 appears. For non-directional tires, move the rear tires straight forward and cross the front tires to the rear; directional tires move front-to-rear on the same side — Owner's Manual, **Tire Rotation** and **Maintenance Minder**.[5][6]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Replace under Maintenance Minder sub-item 2; primarily dusty operation requires replacement every 15,000 mi (24,000 km). Exact service part number is **Needs research**.[5]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace under sub-item 2; severe urban soot, dusty or deicing-road operation requires replacement every 15,000 mi (24,000 km). Exact service part number is **Needs research**.[5]
- **Oil filter:** Replace with engine oil under Maintenance Minder sub-item 0; exact service part number is **Needs research**.[5]
- **Battery specification:** Exact 12-volt battery type, group size, CCA and Honda service part number are **Needs research**.
- **Spark plugs and gap:** NGK DILKAR7H11GS or DILKAR7G11GS, or DENSO DXE22HQR-D11S; exact gap is **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[4]
- **Exterior bulbs versus LED assemblies:** The exact-year specifications identify the exterior lamps as LED assemblies; serviceable module/assembly identifiers remain **Needs research**.[4]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow Maintenance Minder main item A for oil and sub-item 0 for oil/filter. If the oil-life message does not appear within 12 months after reset, change the oil annually.[5]
- **Brake fluid:** Replace every 3 years; Maintenance Minder sub-item 7 may also appear.[5]
- **Engine and cabin air filters:** Replace under sub-item 2, with the 15,000-mile severe-condition rules above.[5]
- **Coolant:** Replace under Maintenance Minder sub-item 5; no fixed normal-use mileage was accepted from the cited page.[5]
- **Transmission/transfer/rear differential:** Replace CVT and transfer fluids under sub-item 3. Under mountainous, very-low-speed or trailer-towing severe use, replace transmission fluid every 25,000 mi (40,000 km). Replace rear-differential fluid under sub-item 6; for repeated low-speed mountain towing, replace at 7,500 mi (12,000 km), then every 15,000 mi (24,000 km).[5]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace under sub-item 4 and inspect valve clearance; no fixed normal-use mileage was accepted from the cited page.[5]

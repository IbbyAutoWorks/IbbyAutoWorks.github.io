# 2025 Honda Civic Sedan Hybrid — 2.0L hybrid, eCVT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, hybrid system, transmission/drive unit, trim, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Honda Motor Oil or API-certified SAE 0W-20 — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.0 US qt (3.8 L); 3.7 US qt (3.5 L) without filter — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Honda Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant Type 2 for both engine and high-voltage systems — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** Engine-coolant change quantity 1.32 US gal (5.01 L) including the 0.13-US-gal (0.51-L) reserve tank and 1.47 US gal (5.57 L) total including reserve. High-voltage-system coolant change quantity 1.05 US gal (3.99 L) and total 1.09 US gal (4.11 L) — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Transmission fluid:** Honda Automatic Transmission Fluid ATF DW-1 for the hybrid drive unit — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** 1.9 US qt (1.8 L) change quantity — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Honda Heavy Duty Brake Fluid DOT 3 — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD identity.[1][8]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** No separately labeled front-differential service fill was accepted from the exact-year source; independent quantity is **Needs research**.[1]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD identity.[1][8]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 80 lbf-ft (108 N·m) — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Check the label on the driver's doorjamb; the physical vehicle placard controls. Trim- and tire-package-specific reference values remain **Needs research**.[1]
- **Tire rotation:** Rotate when Maintenance Minder sub-item 1 appears. For non-directional tires, move the rear tires straight forward and cross the front tires to the rear; directional tires move front-to-rear on the same side — Owner's Manual, **Tire Rotation** and **Maintenance Minder**.[2][3]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Replace under Maintenance Minder sub-item 2; primarily dusty operation requires replacement every 15,000 mi (24,000 km). Exact service part number is **Needs research**.[2]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace under sub-item 2; severe urban soot, dusty or deicing-road operation requires replacement every 15,000 mi (24,000 km). Exact service part number is **Needs research**.[2]
- **Oil filter:** Replace with engine oil under Maintenance Minder sub-item 0; exact service part number is **Needs research**.[2]
- **Battery specification:** High-voltage battery chemistry is lithium-ion; exact 12-volt replacement group size, CCA and Honda service part number are **Needs research**.[1]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** NGK DILZKAR7G11Y; exact gap is **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow Maintenance Minder main item A for oil and sub-item 0 for oil/filter. If the oil-life message does not appear within 12 months after reset, change the oil annually.[2]
- **Brake fluid:** Replace every 3 years; Maintenance Minder sub-item 7 may also appear.[2]
- **Engine and cabin air filters:** Replace under sub-item 2, with the 15,000-mile severe-condition rules above.[2]
- **Coolant:** Replace engine coolant under Maintenance Minder sub-item 5; a separate fixed high-voltage-coolant interval is **Needs research**.[2]
- **Hybrid drive-unit fluid:** Replace under sub-item 3. Under mountainous, very-low-speed or high-temperature severe use, replace at 47,500 mi (76,000 km) or three years, then every 30,000 mi (48,000 km) or two years.[2]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace under sub-item 4 and inspect valve clearance; no fixed normal-use mileage was accepted from the cited page.[2]

## Variant warnings

- This sheet covers only the 2.0L two-motor hybrid/eCVT Civic Sedan; do not apply it to the non-hybrid 2.0L/CVT or turbocharged/manual Si.[1][8]
- The engine and high-voltage cooling circuits have separate capacities and must not be combined.[1]
- Tire pressure must come from the actual vehicle placard.[1]

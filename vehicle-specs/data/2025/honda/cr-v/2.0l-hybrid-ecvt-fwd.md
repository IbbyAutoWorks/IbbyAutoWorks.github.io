# 2025 Honda CR-V Hybrid — 2.0L hybrid, eCVT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, hybrid system, drive unit, drivetrain, trim, tire placard, installed 12-volt battery, lamp package and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Honda Motor Oil or premium-grade SAE 0W-20 detergent oil with the API Certification Seal — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[3]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.0 US qt (3.8 L); 3.7 US qt (3.5 L) without filter — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[3]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Honda Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant Type 2, 50/50 premix, for both engine and high-voltage cooling circuits — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[3]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** Engine circuit 1.39 US gal (5.25 L) change quantity including the 0.15-US-gal (0.58-L) reserve tank and 1.60 US gal (6.06 L) total including reserve for 2WD. High-voltage circuit 1.08 US gal (4.10 L) change quantity and 1.11 US gal (4.21 L) total — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[3]
- **Transmission/drive-unit fluid:** Honda Automatic Transmission Fluid ATF DW-1 — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[3]
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** 2.0 US qt (1.9 L) change quantity for 2WD/FWD — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[3]
- **Brake fluid:** Honda Heavy Duty Brake Fluid DOT 3 — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[3]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD identity.[9]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** No separate service fill was accepted outside the hybrid drive-unit entry; independent quantity is **Needs research**.[3]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD identity.[9]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** Needs research; an exact hybrid-manual locator was not accepted in this run.
- **Cold tire pressure:** The actual driver-doorjamb tire-information label controls. The manual lists 235/60R18 103H and 235/55R19 101H road tires at 33 psi (230 kPa) front/rear, and the T155/90D17 101M compact spare at 60 psi (420 kPa); match installed equipment and placard — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[3]
- **Tire rotation:** Rotate when Maintenance Minder sub-item 1 appears. Non-directional tires use the manual's front-to-rear/cross pattern; directional tires move front-to-rear on the same side. Calibrate TPMS after rotation — Owner's Manual, **Tire Rotation** and **Maintenance Minder**.[4][7]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Replace under Maintenance Minder sub-item 2; for primarily dusty driving, replace every 15,000 mi (24,000 km). Exact part number is **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.[4]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace under sub-item 2; replace every 15,000 mi (24,000 km) under the manual's severe air-quality conditions. Exact part number is **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.[4]
- **Oil filter:** Replace with engine oil under Maintenance Minder sub-item 0; exact part number is **Needs research**.[4]
- **Battery specification:** High-voltage battery type is lithium-ion. Exact 12-volt replacement group size, CCA and Honda service part number are **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[3]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** NGK DILZKAR7G11Y; exact gap is **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[3]
- **Exterior bulbs versus LED assemblies:** Low/high beam, front turn signal, parking/DRL, front side marker, fog light, side turn repeater, stop/tail, rear side marker, back-up, high-mount stop and license-plate positions are listed as LED. Rear turn signal is the replaceable 21 W bulb — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[3]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow Maintenance Minder main item A for oil and sub-item 0 for oil/filter. If the oil-life message does not appear within 12 months after reset, change the oil annually — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.[4]
- **Brake fluid:** Replace every 3 years; Maintenance Minder sub-item 7 may also appear — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.[4]
- **Engine and cabin air filters:** Replace under sub-item 2, with the 15,000-mile severe-condition rules above — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.[4]
- **Coolant:** Replace engine coolant under Maintenance Minder sub-item 5; a fixed high-voltage-coolant interval is **Needs research**.[4]
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Replace transmission/drive-unit fluid under sub-item 3. Under mountainous, very-low-speed or high-temperature severe use, replace at 47,500 mi (76,000 km) or 3 years, then every 30,000 mi (48,000 km) or 2 years. Rear-differential service is not applicable to this FWD sheet — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.[4]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace under sub-item 4 and inspect valve clearance at the same service; no fixed normal-use mileage was accepted from the cited page.[4]

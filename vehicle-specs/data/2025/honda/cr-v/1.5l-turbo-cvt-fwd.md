# 2025 Honda CR-V — 1.5L turbocharged inline-4, CVT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, trim, tire placard, installed battery, lamp package and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Honda Motor Oil or premium-grade SAE 0W-20 detergent oil with the API Certification Seal — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 3.7 US qt (3.5 L); 3.4 US qt (3.2 L) without filter — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Honda Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant Type 2, 50/50 premix — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 1.84 US gal (6.97 L) change quantity including the 0.13-US-gal (0.49-L) reserve tank — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Transmission fluid:** Honda HCF-2 Transmission Fluid only — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** 3.7 US qt (3.5 L) change quantity for the 2WD/FWD CVT — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Honda Heavy Duty Brake Fluid DOT 3 — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD identity; no separate transfer-case service entry is published in the cited specifications.[1][8]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** No separate front-differential service fill is published outside the CVT entry; independent quantity is **Needs research**.[1]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD identity.[8]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 80 lbf-ft (108 N·m; 11 kgf·m) — Owner's Manual, **Changing a Flat Tire**.[6]
- **Cold tire pressure:** The actual driver-doorjamb tire-information label controls. The manual lists 235/65R17 104H at 32 psi (220 kPa) front/rear, 235/60R18 103H at 33 psi (230 kPa) front/rear and the T155/90D17 101M compact spare at 60 psi (420 kPa); match installed equipment and placard — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Tire rotation:** Rotate when Maintenance Minder sub-item 1 appears. Non-directional tires use the manual's front-to-rear/cross pattern; directional tires move front-to-rear on the same side. Calibrate TPMS after rotation — Owner's Manual, **Tire Rotation** and **Maintenance Minder**.[2][5]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Replace under Maintenance Minder sub-item 2; for primarily dusty driving, replace every 15,000 mi (24,000 km). Exact part number is **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.[2]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace under sub-item 2; replace every 15,000 mi (24,000 km) under the manual's severe air-quality conditions. Exact part number is **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.[2]
- **Oil filter:** Replace with engine oil under Maintenance Minder sub-item 0; exact part number is **Needs research**.[2]
- **Battery specification:** Exact replacement group size, CCA and Honda service part number are **Needs research**.
- **Spark plugs and gap:** NGK DILKAR8P8SY; exact gap is **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Exterior bulbs versus LED assemblies:** Low/high beam, front turn signal, parking/DRL, front side marker, fog light, side turn repeater, stop/tail, rear side marker, back-up, high-mount stop and license-plate positions are listed as LED. Rear turn signal is the replaceable WY21W 21 W bulb — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the Maintenance Minder; sub-item 0 replaces oil and filter. If the oil-life message does not appear within 12 months after reset, change the oil annually — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.[2]
- **Brake fluid:** Replace every 3 years; Maintenance Minder sub-item 7 may also appear — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.[2]
- **Engine and cabin air filters:** Replace under sub-item 2, with the 15,000-mile severe-condition rules above — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.[2]
- **Coolant:** Replace under Maintenance Minder sub-item 5; no fixed normal-use mileage was accepted from the cited page.[2]
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Replace CVT fluid under sub-item 3. Under mountainous, very-low-speed or trailer-towing severe use, replace transmission fluid every 25,000 mi (40,000 km). Rear-differential service is not applicable to this FWD sheet — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.[2]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace under sub-item 4 and inspect valve clearance at the same service; no fixed normal-use mileage was accepted from the cited page.[2]

# 2025 Honda Accord Hybrid — 2.0L inline-4 hybrid, eCVT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, hybrid trim, tire placard, installed battery, lamp package and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Honda Motor Oil or premium-grade SAE 0W-20 detergent oil with the API Certification Seal — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.0 US qt (3.8 L); 3.7 US qt (3.5 L) without filter — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Honda Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant Type 2, 50/50 premix, for both gasoline-engine and high-voltage-system cooling circuits — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Gasoline-engine cooling-system capacity:** 1.77 US gal (6.69 L) change quantity including the 0.19-US-gal (0.71-L) reserve tank — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **High-voltage-system coolant capacity:** 1.92 US qt (1.82 L) change quantity — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Hybrid drive-unit fluid:** Honda ATF DW-1 — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Hybrid drive-unit capacity/service quantity:** 2.0 US qt (1.9 L) change quantity — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Honda Heavy Duty Brake Fluid DOT 3 — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.[1]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this front-wheel-drive hybrid identity.[1][3][4]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** No separate front-differential service fill is published outside the hybrid drive-unit entry; independent quantity is **Needs research**.[1]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this front-wheel-drive hybrid identity.[3][4]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Replace under Maintenance Minder sub-item 2; for primarily dusty driving, replace every 15,000 mi (24,000 km). Exact part number is **Needs research** — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.[2]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the Maintenance Minder; sub-item 0 replaces oil and filter. If the oil-life message does not appear within 12 months after reset, change the oil annually — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.[2]
- **Brake fluid:** Replace every 3 years; Maintenance Minder sub-item 7 may also appear — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.[2]
- **Engine and cabin air filters:** Replace under sub-item 2, with the 15,000-mile severe-condition rules above — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.[2]
- **Coolant:** Replace under Maintenance Minder sub-item 5; no fixed normal-use mileage was accepted from the cited page.[2]
- **Hybrid drive-unit fluid:** Replace under sub-item 3. Under mountainous, very-low-speed driving, replace at 47,500 mi (76,000 km) or 3 years, then every 30,000 mi (48,000 km) or 2 years — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.[2]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace under sub-item 4 and inspect valve clearance at the same service; no fixed normal-use mileage was accepted from the cited page.[2]

## Variant warnings

- EPA vehicle ID 48505 identifies the 2025 Accord Hybrid 2.0L/FWD record; EPA vehicle ID 48497 separately identifies the 2025 Accord Hybrid Sport/Touring 2.0L/FWD record. Confirm trim and tire package rather than collapsing placard-dependent values.[3][4]
- Do not apply 1.5L turbo gasoline Accord oil, coolant, CVT or ignition data to this hybrid sheet.[1]
- Tire pressure is equipment-specific and the physical placard is authoritative.[1]

# 2025 Mazda CX-5 — 2.5L turbo, 6-speed automatic, AWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, turbo/i-stop equipment, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** SAE 5W-30 engine oil carrying the API “Certified For Gasoline Engines” mark — Owner's Manual, printed p. 6-23 (PDF p. 510).[1]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 5.1 US qt (4.8 L), approximate — Owner's Manual, printed p. 9-8 (PDF p. 659).[1]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Mazda FL-22 type coolant — Owner's Manual, printed p. 9-7 (PDF p. 658).[1]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 9.0 US qt (8.5 L), approximate — Owner's Manual, printed p. 9-8 (PDF p. 659).[1]
- **Transmission fluid:** Mazda Genuine ATF-FZ for SKYACTIV-G 2.5T — Owner's Manual, printed p. 9-7 (PDF p. 658).[1]
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** 8.5 US qt (8.0 L), published approximate total capacity; exact drain-and-fill service quantity remains Needs research — Owner's Manual, printed p. 9-8 (PDF p. 659).[1]
- **Brake fluid:** SAE J1703 or FMVSS 116 DOT 3 — Owner's Manual, printed p. 9-7 (PDF p. 658).[1]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Mazda Long Life Hypoid Gear Oil SG1; 0.42 US qt (0.40 L), approximate — Owner's Manual, printed pp. 9-7–9-8 (PDF pp. 658–659).[1]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Integrated/front-drive service quantity is not separately published; Needs research.[1]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Mazda Long Life Hypoid Gear Oil SG1; 0.37 US qt (0.35 L), approximate — Owner's Manual, printed pp. 9-7–9-8 (PDF pp. 658–659).[1]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 80–108 lb-ft (108–147 N·m) — Owner's Manual, printed p. 9-13 (PDF p. 664).[1]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the physical tire-pressure placard. The manual lists 34 psi front/rear for P225/65R17 and 35 psi front/rear for P225/55R19 in the U.S./Canada, but the installed-tire placard controls — Owner's Manual, printed p. 9-12 (PDF p. 663).[1]
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Normal Schedule 1: every 7,500 mi (12,000 km). Severe Schedule 2: every 5,000 mi (8,000 km). Exclude the temporary spare; directional/asymmetric/studded tires rotate front-to-rear only — Owner's Manual, printed pp. 6-5–6-6 and 6-39 (PDF pp. 492–494 and 526–527); Scheduled Maintenance, pp. 6-5–6-6.[1][2]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Exact replacement part number Needs research. The tables call for replacement and shorter service in dusty/sandy use; exact Schedule 1 column alignment needs visual shop confirmation — Scheduled Maintenance, pp. 6-5–6-7.[2]
- **Cabin air filter:** Exact replacement part number Needs research. Severe Schedule 2 specifies every 30,000 mi (48,000 km) or 2 years; normal-table column alignment needs visual shop confirmation — Scheduled Maintenance, pp. 6-5–6-7.[2]
- **Oil filter:** Exact replacement part number Needs research.[1]
- **Battery specification:** The EPA identity has no stop-start. The U.S./Canada no-i-stop table lists 55D23L or 75D23L, both 12 V 65 Ah/20HR; exact equipment mapping must be confirmed from the installed battery — Owner's Manual, printed p. 9-6 (PDF p. 657).[1][5]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Mazda Genuine PY8V-18-110 for SKYACTIV-G 2.5T; gap Needs research — Owner's Manual, printed p. 9-6 (PDF p. 657).[1]
- **Exterior bulb types:** All exterior lamps are LED integrated units; individual exterior bulbs are not replaceable — Owner's Manual, printed pp. 6-43 and 9-11 (PDF pp. 531 and 662).[1]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the flexible oil monitor; maximum 7,500 mi (12,000 km) or 12 months, and service within 600 mi/15 days of the warning. Severe fixed schedule is every 5,000 mi (8,000 km) or 6 months — Scheduled Maintenance, pp. 6-4–6-7.[2]
- **Brake fluid:** Inspect level and system per the schedule; no fixed U.S. replacement interval was stated in the inspected Schedule 1/2 table. Fluid specification remains DOT 3 — Owner's Manual, printed p. 9-7; Scheduled Maintenance, pp. 6-5–6-7.[1][2]
- **Engine air filter:** Replace per the exact schedule table and earlier in dusty/sandy use; exact normal-use mileage needs visual shop confirmation.[2]
- **Cabin air filter:** Severe use: 30,000 mi (48,000 km) or 2 years; normal-use mileage needs visual shop confirmation.[2]
- **Coolant:** First replacement at 120,000 mi (192,000 km) or 10 years, then every 60,000 mi (96,000 km) or 5 years — Scheduled Maintenance, pp. 6-5–6-7.[2]
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** The owner manual says periodic ATF replacement is unnecessary and calls for transfer-case/rear-differential replacement after submersion; shop/VIN-specific severe-service guidance Needs research — Owner's Manual, printed p. 9-7 (PDF p. 658).[1]
- **Spark plugs:** 40,000 mi (64,000 km) for SKYACTIV-G 2.5T — Scheduled Maintenance, pp. 6-5–6-7.[2]

## Variant warnings

- EPA vehicle 48539 identifies a 2025 CX-5 4WD, turbocharged 2.5L SIDI, Automatic (S6), regular gasoline, without cylinder deactivation and without stop-start.[5]
- Do not apply naturally aspirated 0W-20, 4.8-qt oil, 8.1-qt coolant, 0.48-qt transfer-case or 75,000-mile spark-plug values to this turbo sheet.[1][2]
- Published transmission and axle quantities are approximate capacities, not confirmed routine drain-and-fill amounts.[1]
- Tire pressure always comes from the actual vehicle placard.

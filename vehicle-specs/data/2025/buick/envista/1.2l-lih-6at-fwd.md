# 2025 Buick Envista — 1.2L LIH turbo inline-3, 6-speed automatic, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, option/RPO codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** SAE 0W-20 full-synthetic engine oil meeting GM dexos1 — Owner's Manual, pp. 195–196.[1]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.2 qt (4.0 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 263.[1]
- **Coolant type/specification:** 50/50 mixture of clean drinkable water and DEX-COOL coolant meeting GM Standard GMW3420 — Owner's Manual, pp. 200–201.[1]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 6.7 qt (6.3 L), entire system — Owner's Manual, p. 263.[1]
- **Transmission fluid:** DEXRON-VI automatic transmission fluid — Owner's Manual, p. 260.[1]
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Needs research; the owner manual does not publish a 6-speed service-fill quantity.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** GM-approved DOT 4 hydraulic brake fluid — Owner's Manual, pp. 205–206, 260.[1]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this front-wheel-drive variant.[1][2]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; no separate front-final-drive product or service quantity is published in the owner manual.[1]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this front-wheel-drive variant.[1][2]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 100 lb-ft (140 N·m) — Owner's Manual, p. 263.[1]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire and Loading Information label/vehicle placard for the installed tire package; the physical placard is the authority — Owner's Manual, pp. 142, 223–224.[1]
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate every 7,500 mi (12,000 km), use the illustrated pattern, exclude the compact spare, reset pressures to the placard and relearn TPMS positions — Owner's Manual, pp. 228–229, 255–257.[1]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research; follow the engine-air-filter life messages and reset the life system after replacement — Owner's Manual, pp. 198–199, 256–257.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research; replace every 22,500 mi (36,000 km) or 24 months, sooner under the listed environmental conditions — Owner's Manual, p. 257.[1]
- **Oil filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research.[1]
- **Battery specification:** Stop/Start is confirmed for the exact EPA identity, but replacement number, battery construction, group and CCA require the original battery label/current parts information — Owner's Manual, p. 206; EPA record.[1][2]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Exact plug part number needs research; factory gap 0.024–0.028 in (0.6–0.7 mm), and re-gapping is not recommended — Owner's Manual, p. 264.[1]
- **Exterior bulb types:** Replaceable liftgate stop/turn lamps use W21W and back-up lamps use W16W; tailgate inboard taillamps, taillamps and multiple front lamp functions use LED assemblies — Owner's Manual, pp. 209–210.[1]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the oil-life system; change within 600 mi (1,000 km) of the message and at least annually. If accidentally reset, service within 3,000 mi (5,000 km) of the last change — Owner's Manual, pp. 197, 257.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Every 5 years — Owner's Manual, p. 258.[1]
- **Engine air filter:** Follow the filter-life messages; severe dust or environmental conditions may require earlier service — Owner's Manual, pp. 198–199, 257.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Every 22,500 mi (36,000 km) or 24 months, whichever comes first; shorter intervals may be required — Owner's Manual, p. 257.[1]
- **Coolant:** Drain and fill at 150,000 mi (240,000 km) or 6 years, whichever comes first — Owner's Manual, pp. 200, 257.[1]
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Severe service: change automatic-transmission fluid every 45,000 mi (72,000 km). No separate FWD final-drive interval is published — Owner's Manual, p. 258.[1]
- **Spark plugs:** 60,000 mi (96,000 km); extreme conditions may require more frequent replacement — Owner's Manual, pp. 257–258.[1]

## Variant warnings

- This sheet is only for VIN code 2, 1.2L LIH, regular gasoline, 6-speed automatic and FWD. Do not apply it to the VIN P LBP flex-fuel variant — Owner's Manual, pp. 182–184, 263–264.[1]
- EPA vehicle 47780 identifies a regular-gasoline, 1.2L turbo three-cylinder, 6-speed automatic, FWD, Stop/Start Envista identity; confirm VIN code 2 before using this sheet.[2]
- Tire pressure always comes from the actual vehicle placard.

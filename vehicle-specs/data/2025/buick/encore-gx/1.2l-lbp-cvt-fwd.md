# 2025 Buick Encore GX — 1.2L LBP turbo inline-3, CVT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, option/RPO codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** SAE 0W-20 full-synthetic engine oil meeting GM dexos1 — Owner's Manual, pp. 227–228.[1]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.2 qt (4.0 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 301.[1]
- **Coolant type/specification:** 50/50 mixture of clean drinkable water and DEX-COOL coolant — Owner's Manual, p. 298.[1]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 6.7 qt (6.3 L), entire system — Owner's Manual, pp. 301–302.[1]
- **Transmission fluid:** GM High Performance CVT Fluid; obtain the current product through a GM dealer — Owner's Manual, p. 298.[1]
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Needs research; the owner manual does not publish a CVT service-fill quantity.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** DOT 4 hydraulic brake fluid — Owner's Manual, p. 298.[1]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD sheet.[1][2]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; no separate front-final-drive service fluid or capacity is published in the owner manual.[1]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD sheet.[1][2]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 100 lb-ft (140 N·m) — Owner's Manual, p. 301.[1]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire and Loading Information label/vehicle placard for the installed tire package; the physical placard is the authority — Owner's Manual, pp. 163, 260, 265–266.[1]
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate every 7,500 mi (12,000 km), use the illustrated pattern, exclude the compact spare, reset pressures to the placard and relearn TPMS positions — Owner's Manual, pp. 265–266, 292–293.[1]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research; follow the engine-air-filter life messages and reset the life system after replacement — Owner's Manual, pp. 229–230, 294.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research; replace every 22,500 mi (36,000 km) or 24 months, sooner under the listed environmental conditions — Owner's Manual, p. 294.[1]
- **Oil filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research.[1]
- **Battery specification:** EPA identifies Stop/Start on this exact variant; the owner manual specifies an AGM 12-volt battery for Stop/Start-equipped vehicles. Exact replacement number, group and CCA require the original battery label/current parts information — Owner's Manual, p. 239.[1][2]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Exact plug part number needs research; factory gap 0.024–0.028 in (0.60–0.70 mm), and re-gapping is not recommended — Owner's Manual, p. 302.[1]
- **Exterior bulb types:** Uplevel rear lamp assemblies use LEDs; base-level liftgate inboard taillamps, stop/taillamps, turn signals and rear back-up lamps have service procedures for replaceable bulbs. Exact trade numbers and full trim mapping need research — Owner's Manual, pp. 243–246.[1]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the oil-life system; change within 600 mi (1,000 km) of the message and at least annually. If accidentally reset, service within 3,000 mi (5,000 km) of the last change — Owner's Manual, pp. 228, 294.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Every 5 years — Owner's Manual, p. 295.[1]
- **Engine air filter:** Follow the filter-life messages; severe dust or environmental conditions may require earlier service — Owner's Manual, pp. 229–230, 294.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Every 22,500 mi (36,000 km) or 24 months, whichever comes first; shorter intervals may be required — Owner's Manual, p. 294.[1]
- **Coolant:** Drain and fill at 150,000 mi (240,000 km) or 6 years, whichever comes first — Owner's Manual, p. 294.[1]
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Severe service: change CVT fluid and filter every 45,000 mi (72,000 km). No FWD axle or transfer-case interval is published — Owner's Manual, p. 295.[1]
- **Spark plugs:** 60,000 mi (96,000 km); extreme conditions may require more frequent replacement — Owner's Manual, pp. 294–295.[1]
- **Oil-pump drive belt and timing belt:** 150,000 mi (240,000 km) or 15 years, whichever comes first, for the 1.2L LBP — Owner's Manual, p. 294.[1]

## Variant warnings

- This sheet is only for VIN code P, 1.2L LBP, MRG CVT and FWD. The 1.3L L3T has different oil/coolant capacities and spark gap; the AWD 1.3L uses a 9-speed automatic, transfer case and rear axle — Owner's Manual, pp. 294–302.[1]
- EPA vehicle 47786 identifies this exact variant as a 1.2L turbo three-cylinder, variable-ratio automatic, FWD, Stop/Start, gasoline-or-E85 flex-fuel vehicle.[2]
- Tire pressure always comes from the actual vehicle placard.

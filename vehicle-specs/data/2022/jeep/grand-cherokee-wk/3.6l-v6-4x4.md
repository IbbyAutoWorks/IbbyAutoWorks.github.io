# 2022 Jeep Grand Cherokee WK — 3.6L Pentastar V6, 8-speed automatic, 4x4

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, option/RPO codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Full-synthetic SAE 0W-20 meeting FCA material standard MS-6395 and carrying the API certification mark — Owner's Manual, pp. 257, 309.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 6 qt (5.6 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 309.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Mopar 10 Year/150,000 Mile OAT coolant meeting MS.90032 — Owner's Manual, pp. 266, 309.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 10.4 qt (9.9 L), including heater and recovery bottle filled to MAX — Owner's Manual, p. 309.
- **Transmission fluid:** Mopar ZF 8 & 9 Speed ATF or equivalent — Owner's Manual, p. 310.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Needs research; the owner manual does not provide a service-fill quantity.
- **Brake fluid:** Mopar DOT 3 brake fluid meeting SAE J1703 — Owner's Manual, p. 310.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Mopar ATF+4; capacity needs research and depends on the verified single-speed/two-speed transfer case — Owner's Manual, p. 310.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Mopar GL-5 Synthetic Axle Lubricant SAE 75W-85; capacity needs research — Owner's Manual, p. 310.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Mopar GL-5 Synthetic Axle Lubricant SAE 75W-85; capacity needs research — Owner's Manual, p. 310.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 130 lb-ft (176 N·m) — Owner's Manual, p. 305.
- **Cold tire pressure:** Check the vehicle Tire and Loading Information placard; installed tire size/load specification controls — Owner's Manual, tire-pressure section.
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate at the first sign of irregular wear, including before the oil-change indicator if needed — Owner's Manual, p. 251. The exact pattern needs research for the installed tire configuration.

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Mopar-certified replacement filter; exact part number needs research — Owner's Manual, pp. 257–258.
- **Cabin air filter:** Replaceable filter behind the glove compartment; exact part number needs research — Owner's Manual, pp. 259–260.
- **Oil filter:** Mopar oil filter or equivalent meeting/exceeding SAE/USCAR-36 — Owner's Manual, pp. 257, 309.
- **Battery specification:** 12-volt maintenance-free battery; exact group size and rating need research — Owner's Manual, p. 255.
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Needs research.
- **Exterior bulb types:** Needs research; identify replaceable bulbs versus LED assemblies by lamp option.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the automatic oil-change indicator; never exceed 10,000 mi, 12 months or 350 engine hours — Owner's Manual, p. 250.
- **Brake fluid:** Needs research; no fixed replacement interval was proven in the inspected owner-manual sections.
- **Engine air filter:** Replace at 30,000-mi steps shown in the maintenance plan; inspect at every oil change in dusty/off-road use — Owner's Manual, pp. 251–252.
- **Cabin air filter:** Every 12,000 mi (19,000 km) — Owner's Manual, p. 252.
- **Coolant:** 10 years or 150,000 mi (240,000 km), whichever comes first — Owner's Manual, pp. 253, 266.
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Inspect transfer case and axle fluids per the maintenance plan; change axle/transfer-case fluids more frequently for police, taxi, fleet, off-road or frequent trailer towing — Owner's Manual, pp. 252–253.
- **Spark plugs:** Maintenance-plan mileage marker needs a clearer table extraction before use; leave as `Needs research`.

## Variant warnings

- This is the legacy WK body, not the 2022 WL/new-body Grand Cherokee or Grand Cherokee L.
- Confirm the installed transfer case before ordering fluid or determining capacity.

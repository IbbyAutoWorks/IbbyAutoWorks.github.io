# 2024 Ford Explorer — 2.3L EcoBoost, 10-speed automatic, RWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, option codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** SAE 5W-30 meeting Ford WSS-M2C961-A1; Motorcraft SAE 5W-30 is recommended. SAE 0W-30 meeting WSS-M2C963-A1 is the manual's alternative for the described extremely cold/high-altitude conditions — Owner's Manual, pp. 389–390.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 5.2 US qt (4.9 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 389.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Motorcraft Yellow Prediluted Antifreeze/Coolant meeting Ford WSS-M97B57-A2 — Owner's Manual, p. 389.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 14.1 US qt (13.3 L) without trailer-towing equipment; 15.2 US qt (14.4 L) with trailer-towing equipment — Owner's Manual, p. 389. Confirm installed cooling/towing equipment before filling.
- **Transmission fluid:** Motorcraft MERCON ULV Automatic Transmission Fluid meeting Ford WSS-M2C949-A — Owner's Manual, p. 388.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** 12.6 US qt (11.9 L) approximate **dry-fill** capacity; the manual says the actual amount can vary during fluid changes. A routine drain-and-fill quantity remains `Needs research` — Owner's Manual, p. 388.
- **Brake fluid:** Motorcraft DOT 4 LV High Performance Motor Vehicle Brake Fluid meeting Ford WSS-M6C65-A2 — Owner's Manual, p. 391.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this RWD sheet.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this RWD sheet.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Motorcraft SAE 75W-85 Premium Synthetic Hypoid Gear Lubricant meeting Ford WSS-M2C942-A; 1.9 US qt (1.8 L) for two-wheel drive — Owner's Manual, p. 393.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 150 lb-ft (204 N·m), M14 × 1.5 wheel nuts. Retorque within 100 mi (160 km) after wheel disturbance — Owner's Manual, p. 379.
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire Label on the B-pillar/driver-door edge or the Safety Compliance Certification Label for this exact tire/load configuration; the actual vehicle placard controls — Owner's Manual, pp. 350–351.
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate at every oil-change interval and follow the manual's applicable rear-wheel-drive rotation diagram; reset/retrain tire-pressure monitoring as instructed when needed — Owner's Manual, pp. 364, 483.

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Motorcraft FA-1884 — Owner's Manual, p. 385.
- **Cabin air filter:** Motorcraft FP-100 — Owner's Manual, p. 385.
- **Oil filter:** Motorcraft FL-910-S; if unavailable, Ford specifies an oil filter meeting SAE/USCAR-36 — Owner's Manual, p. 385.
- **Battery specification:** Motorcraft BAGM-48H6-760 — Owner's Manual, p. 385.
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Motorcraft SP-594; specified gap 0.028–0.031 in (0.7–0.8 mm) — Owner's Manual, pp. 381, 385.
- **Exterior bulb types:** Rear direction indicator 3757NAK/PY27/7W, reverse lamp 21W and license-plate lamp 5W are replaceable. Front side marker/signature/direction-indicator/DRL/headlamp/fog lamps, side direction indicators, rear side markers, brake/tail lamps and center high-mounted stop lamp are LED assemblies; Ford states LED lamps are not serviceable — Owner's Manual, pp. 415–416.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the Intelligent Oil-Life Monitor; example ranges are 7,500–10,000 mi (12,000–16,000 km) normal, 5,000–7,500 mi (8,000–12,000 km) severe and 3,000–5,000 mi (4,800–8,000 km) extreme. Never exceed one year or 10,000 mi (16,000 km) — Owner's Manual, pp. 482–484.
- **Brake fluid:** Replace every 3 years — Owner's Manual, p. 484.
- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 mi (48,000 km); inspect/replace more often in dusty, sandy or heavy-commercial use — Owner's Manual, pp. 484, 486–487.
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 20,000 mi (32,000 km); inspect/replace more often in dusty, sandy or heavy-commercial use — Owner's Manual, pp. 484, 486–487.
- **Coolant:** Initial replacement at 10 years or 200,000 mi (322,000 km), then every 5 years or 100,000 mi (160,000 km) — Owner's Manual, pp. 484–485.
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Change automatic-transmission fluid every 150,000 mi (240,000 km). Ford lists no normal scheduled rear-axle fluid change; service is message/condition based and is required when indicated, after submersion, leakage or qualifying extreme/severe duty — Owner's Manual, pp. 485, 487.
- **Spark plugs:** Replace every 100,000 mi (160,000 km); replace every 60,000 mi (96,000 km) for trailer towing/car-top-carrier use or extensive idling/heavy-commercial use — Owner's Manual, pp. 484–486.

## Variant warnings

- This sheet is for the 2.3L EcoBoost, 10-speed automatic and RWD combination only. Do not use AWD front-axle, transfer-case or rear-axle quantities.
- The 12.6-US-qt transmission figure is an approximate dry-fill capacity, not a routine drain-and-fill instruction.
- Cooling capacity changes with trailer-towing equipment.
- Tire pressure must come from the actual vehicle placard.
- Recall applicability remains VIN/equipment specific; the 3.3L-hybrid battery-cable campaign does not establish applicability to this 2.3L gasoline sheet.

# 2024 Ford Bronco Sport — 2.0L EcoBoost, 8-speed automatic, 4WD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, option codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** SAE 5W-30 meeting Ford WSS-M2C961-A1; SAE 0W-30 meeting WSS-M2C963-A1 is the manual's extremely-cold-climate alternative — Owner's Manual, pp. 347–348.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 5.5 US qt (5.2 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 348.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Motorcraft Yellow Prediluted Antifreeze/Coolant meeting Ford WSS-M97B57-A2 — Owner's Manual, p. 350.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 9.0 US qt (8.5 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 350.
- **Transmission fluid:** Motorcraft MERCON ULV Automatic Transmission Fluid meeting Ford WSS-M2C949-A — Owner's Manual, p. 353.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Needs research; the owner manual gives the fluid specification but no fill quantity.
- **Brake fluid:** Motorcraft DOT 4 LV High Performance Motor Vehicle Brake Fluid meeting Ford WSS-M6C65-A2 or ISO 4925 Class 6 — Owner's Manual, pp. 353–354.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Needs research; the manual mentions power-transfer-unit service messages but does not publish a fluid specification/capacity in the cited tables.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; confirm the installed rear drive unit and do not infer another Ford model's fill.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 100 lb-ft (135 N·m), M12 × 1.5; retorque within 100 mi (160 km) after wheel disturbance — Owner's Manual, p. 340.
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire and Loading label on the B-pillar or driver-door edge; the actual vehicle placard controls — Owner's Manual, tire-label section.
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Follow the manual's four-tire diagram and exclude a dissimilar spare; adjust pressure afterward. Exact interval remains `Needs research` because the manual directs scheduled intervals to Ford's VIN-specific maintenance site — Owner's Manual, p. 328 and pp. 415–418.

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Motorcraft FA-1939 — Owner's Manual, p. 344.
- **Cabin air filter:** Motorcraft FP-100-A — Owner's Manual, p. 344.
- **Oil filter:** Motorcraft FL-910-S; Ford permits a filter meeting SAE/USCAR-36, Filter Type B when Motorcraft is unavailable — Owner's Manual, p. 344.
- **Battery specification:** Motorcraft BEF-48H6-A; BAGM-48H6-760 is listed as an alternative. Configure the battery-management system for the replacement — Owner's Manual, p. 344.
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Motorcraft SP-594; 0.028–0.031 in (0.7–0.8 mm) — Owner's Manual, pp. 343–344.
- **Exterior bulb types:** Replaceable Y16W rear turn, P27 stop and 21W reverse bulbs are listed. Headlamp, fog, front/rear side-marker, mirror-turn, high-mounted brake, license-plate and tail lamps are non-serviceable LED assemblies — Owner's Manual, pp. 299–301.

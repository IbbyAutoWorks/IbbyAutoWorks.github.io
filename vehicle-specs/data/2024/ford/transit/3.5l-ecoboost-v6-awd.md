# 2024 Ford Transit — 3.5L EcoBoost V6, 10-speed automatic, AWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, option/RPO codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** SAE 5W-30 meeting Ford WSS-M2C961-A1; Motorcraft SAE 5W-30 is recommended. SAE 0W-30 meeting WSS-M2C963-A1 is the manual's alternative for the extremely cold/high-altitude conditions described in the manual — Owner's Manual, pp. 458–459.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 6.0 US qt (5.7 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 458.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Motorcraft Yellow Prediluted Antifreeze/Coolant, Ford specification WSS-M97B57-A2 — Owner's Manual, p. 461.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 13.31 US qt (12.6 L) without auxiliary heater; 14.27 US qt (13.5 L) with auxiliary heater — Owner's Manual, p. 461. Confirm rear/auxiliary heat equipment before filling.
- **Transmission fluid:** Needs research; the owner manual identifies the 10-speed automatic and Motorcraft FT-216 transmission filter but does not provide a transmission-fluid specification in the cited capacity tables.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Needs research; no drain-and-fill or overhaul quantity was found in the exact-year owner manual.
- **Brake fluid:** Motorcraft DOT 4 LV High Performance Motor Vehicle Brake Fluid meeting Ford WSS-M6C65-A2 or ISO 4925 Class 6 — Owner's Manual, p. 468.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Needs research. The exact-year owner manual calls for transfer-case fluid replacement on four-wheel-drive vehicles at the normal 150,000-mi (240,000-km) interval but does not provide the fluid specification or service quantity in the cited capacity tables — Owner's Manual, pp. 531, 537.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research. The exact-year owner manual calls for front-axle fluid replacement on four-wheel-drive vehicles at the normal 150,000-mi (240,000-km) interval but does not provide the fluid specification or service quantity in the cited capacity tables — Owner's Manual, pp. 531, 537.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Motorcraft SAE 75W-85 Premium Synthetic Hypoid Gear Lubricant meeting WSS-M2C942-A. Conventional differential: 2.3 US qt (2.13–2.2 L); limited-slip differential: 2.1–2.2 US qt (2.01–2.08 L), plus 0.2 US qt (148–178 mL) Motorcraft XL-3 friction modifier meeting EST-M2C118-A — Owner's Manual, p. 468. Confirm the installed axle/differential before service.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 150 lb-ft (204 N·m), M14 × 1.5 lug nuts — Owner's Manual, p. 451.
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire Label on the B-pillar/driver-door edge or the Safety Compliance Certification Label for this vehicle's tire, axle and load configuration; the actual vehicle placard controls — Owner's Manual, pp. 410, 421–422 and 429–431.
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate at every oil-change interval; use the manual's rotation diagram and retrain TPMS after rotation when front and rear placard pressures differ — Owner's Manual, pp. 427 and 530–531.

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Motorcraft FA-1916 — Owner's Manual, p. 455.
- **Cabin air filter:** Motorcraft FP-74 — Owner's Manual, p. 455.
- **Oil filter:** Motorcraft FL-500S; if unavailable, Ford specifies a filter meeting SAE/USCAR-36 Performance Specifications, Filter Type C — Owner's Manual, p. 455.
- **Battery specification:** Motorcraft BAGM-48H6-760 — Owner's Manual, p. 455.
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Motorcraft SP-580-X; specified gap 0.030–0.033 in (0.75–0.85 mm) — Owner's Manual, pp. 453 and 455.
- **Exterior bulb types:** Depending on installed lamp package: D5S 25 W HID high/low; H11 55 W low beam/DRL; 9005 60 W high beam; H11 55 W cornering; H8 35 W fog; 3457NAK 21 W parking/turn; PY24W 24 W turn; W5W 5 W side marker; W5WL 5 W clearance; W16W 16 W or WY5W 5 W mirror turn; P21/5W 21/5 W rear stop/tail; P21W 21 W reverse/rear fog; PY21W 21 W rear turn; W5W 5 W license/marker lamps. DRLs, high-mounted stoplamp, grille markers and some side markers are LED; Ford states LED lamps are not serviceable — Owner's Manual, pp. 388–399. Confirm the installed lamp package before ordering.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the Intelligent Oil-Life Monitor. The manual's examples are 7,500–10,000 mi (12,000–16,000 km) for normal 3.5L EcoBoost use, 5,000–7,500 mi (8,000–12,000 km) for severe use and 3,000–5,000 mi (4,800–8,000 km) for extreme use. Never exceed one year or 10,000 mi (16,000 km) for this engine; service within two weeks or 500 mi (800 km) of the message — Owner's Manual, pp. 524–531.
- **Brake fluid:** Replace every 3 years — Owner's Manual, p. 531.
- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 mi (48,000 km); inspect/replace more often in dusty, sandy, off-road or heavy-commercial use — Owner's Manual, pp. 531 and 535–537.
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 20,000 mi (32,000 km); exposure to dusty/dirty conditions can require more frequent replacement — Owner's Manual, p. 531.
- **Coolant:** Initial change at 10 years or 200,000 mi (320,000 km), then every 5 years or 100,000 mi (160,000 km) — Owner's Manual, p. 531.
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Normal schedule changes automatic-transmission fluid/filter, front-axle fluid, rear-axle fluid and transfer-case fluid every 150,000 mi (240,000 km); the manual marks the front-axle and transfer-case items for four-wheel-drive vehicles. Ford also states axle fluid level checks/changes are not otherwise required unless a leak is suspected or the axle has been submerged — Owner's Manual, pp. 531 and 537.
- **Spark plugs:** Replace every 100,000 mi (160,000 km); for towing and extensive-idling/heavy-commercial use, replace every 60,000 mi (96,000 km) — Owner's Manual, pp. 531 and 535–536.

## Variant warnings

- This sheet is for the twin-turbocharged 3.5L EcoBoost V6 and all-wheel drive only; do not use the naturally aspirated 3.5L Duratec oil capacity, spark plug or oil-life limits.
- The owner manual establishes AWD/four-wheel-drive front-axle and transfer-case maintenance applicability but does not publish those components' fluid specifications or service quantities in the cited capacity tables. Obtain current VIN-specific Ford service information before service.
- Rear-axle lubricant quantity depends on conventional versus limited-slip differential. Confirm the axle by VIN/build data before service.
- Cooling capacity depends on auxiliary-heater equipment.
- Tire pressure and axle/load limits always come from the actual vehicle labels.
- Transit Trail tire/steering campaigns are not generalized to every AWD Transit; confirm Trail equipment and VIN applicability.

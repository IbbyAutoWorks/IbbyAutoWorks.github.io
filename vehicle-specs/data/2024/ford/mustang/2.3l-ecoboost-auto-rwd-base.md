# 2024 Ford Mustang — 2.3L EcoBoost, 10-speed automatic, RWD, base

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, performance-package status, option codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** SAE 5W-30 meeting Ford WSS-M2C961-A1; SAE 0W-30 meeting WSS-M2C963-A1 is the manual's extremely-cold-climate alternative — Owner's Manual, pp. 369–370.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 6.0 US qt (5.7 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 369.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Motorcraft Yellow Prediluted Antifreeze/Coolant meeting Ford WSS-M97B57-A2 — Owner's Manual, pp. 372–373.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 10.46 US qt (9.9 L) for the base vehicle. The performance-vehicle quantity is 10.99 US qt (10.4 L) and is excluded from this sheet — Owner's Manual, p. 372.
- **Transmission fluid:** Motorcraft MERCON ULV Automatic Transmission Fluid meeting Ford WSS-M2C949-A — Owner's Manual, p. 379.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** 13.10 US qt (12.4 L) published capacity; routine drain-and-fill quantity remains `Needs research` — Owner's Manual, p. 379.
- **Brake fluid:** Motorcraft DOT 4 LV High Performance Motor Vehicle Brake Fluid meeting Ford WSS-M6C65-A2 or ISO 4925 Class 6 — Owner's Manual, p. 380.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Motorcraft SAE 75W-85 Premium Synthetic Hypoid Gear Lubricant meeting Ford WSS-M2C942-A; 1.6 US qt (1.5 L). For complete refill of a limited-slip axle, include 3.28 fl oz (97 mL) XL-3 friction modifier meeting EST-M2C118-A in the total capacity — Owner's Manual, pp. 380–381.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 150 lb-ft (204 N·m), M14 × 1.5; retorque within 100 mi (160 km) after wheel disturbance — Owner's Manual, p. 361.
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire Label on the driver-side B-pillar or driver-door edge; the actual vehicle placard controls — Owner's Manual, p. 327.
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate at every oil-change interval using the rear-wheel-drive diagram; inspect wear/tread and adjust placard pressure afterward — Owner's Manual, pp. 345, 436–438.

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Motorcraft FA-2067 — Owner's Manual, p. 367.
- **Cabin air filter:** Motorcraft FP-78 — Owner's Manual, p. 367.
- **Oil filter:** Motorcraft FL-2127; Ford permits a filter meeting SAE/USCAR-36, Filter Type B when Motorcraft is unavailable — Owner's Manual, p. 367.
- **Battery specification:** Motorcraft BAGM-48H6-760 — Owner's Manual, p. 367.
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Motorcraft SP-597-X; 0.026–0.030 in (0.65–0.75 mm) — Owner's Manual, pp. 363, 367.
- **Exterior bulb types:** All exterior lamps are LED assemblies and are not serviceable items — Owner's Manual, p. 312.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the Intelligent Oil-Life Monitor. Example ranges are 7,000–10,000 mi normal, 5,000–7,500 mi severe and 3,000–5,000 mi extreme; never exceed one year or 10,000 mi — Owner's Manual, pp. 436–438.
- **Brake fluid:** Replace every 3 years — Owner's Manual, p. 438.
- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 mi (48,000 km); dusty/sandy use requires more frequent inspection/replacement — Owner's Manual, pp. 438, 440–442.
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 20,000 mi (32,000 km); inspect/service more frequently in dusty or heavy-use conditions — Owner's Manual, pp. 438, 440–442.
- **Coolant:** Initial replacement at 10 years or 200,000 mi (320,000 km), then every 5 years or 100,000 mi (160,000 km) — Owner's Manual, p. 439.
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Automatic transmission fluid/filter and rear-axle fluid at 150,000 mi (240,000 km) under the normal schedule; axle fluid also requires service after submersion or an overtemperature message. Track use requires rear-axle fluid every 500 mi (800 km) — Owner's Manual, pp. 438–441.
- **Spark plugs:** Replace every 100,000 mi (160,000 km); every 60,000 mi (96,000 km) for extensive idling/heavy-commercial use — Owner's Manual, pp. 438, 440.

## Variant warnings

- This sheet uses the base/non-performance-package 2.3L cooling capacity. Confirm package/equipment before filling.
- The 13.10-US-qt transmission figure is a published total capacity, not a verified routine drain-and-fill quantity.
- Rear-axle friction modifier applies to a complete refill of a limited-slip axle and is included in the total capacity.
- Track/high-speed use has much shorter oil and rear-axle service requirements.

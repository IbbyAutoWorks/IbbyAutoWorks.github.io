# 2024 Ford Mustang — 5.0L V8, 6-speed manual, RWD

> Verify the VIN, engine/output version, transmission, body configuration, performance/handling package, stop-start equipment, option codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** SAE 5W-30 meeting Ford WSS-M2C961-A1; SAE 0W-30 meeting WSS-M2C963-A1 is the manual's extremely-cold-climate alternative — Owner's Manual, pp. 370–371.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 10.0 US qt (9.5 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 370.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Motorcraft Yellow Prediluted Antifreeze/Coolant meeting Ford WSS-M97B57-A2 — Owner's Manual, pp. 373–374.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 11.7 US qt (11.1 L) for the 5.0L with manual transmission — Owner's Manual, p. 374.
- **Transmission fluid:** Motorcraft Dual Clutch Transmission Fluid meeting Ford WSS-M2C200-D2 — Owner's Manual, pp. 377–378.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** 2.5 US qt (2.4 L) approximate total volume/capacity; the manual says actual amount can vary between fluid changes — Owner's Manual, pp. 377–378.
- **Clutch fluid:** Motorcraft DOT 4 LV High Performance Motor Vehicle Brake Fluid meeting Ford WSS-M6C65-A2 — Owner's Manual, p. 378.
- **Brake fluid:** Motorcraft DOT 4 LV High Performance Motor Vehicle Brake Fluid meeting Ford WSS-M6C65-A2 or ISO 4925 Class 6 — Owner's Manual, p. 380.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Motorcraft SAE 75W-85 Premium Synthetic Hypoid Gear Lubricant meeting Ford WSS-M2C942-A; 1.6 US qt (1.5 L). For complete refill of a limited-slip axle, include 3.28 fl oz (97 mL) XL-3 friction modifier meeting EST-M2C118-A in the total capacity — Owner's Manual, pp. 380–381.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 150 lb-ft (204 N·m), M14 × 1.5; retorque within 100 mi (160 km) after wheel disturbance — Owner's Manual, p. 361.
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire Label on the driver-side B-pillar or driver-door edge; the actual vehicle placard controls — Owner's Manual, pp. 327, 339.
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate at every oil-change interval only when the fitted tire sizes permit the applicable rear-wheel-drive pattern. Staggered front/rear sizes are not crossed between axles; inspect wear and restore placard pressure — Owner's Manual, pp. 345, 436–438.

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Motorcraft FA-2066 — Owner's Manual, p. 368.
- **Cabin air filter:** Motorcraft FP-78 — Owner's Manual, p. 368.
- **Oil filter:** Motorcraft FL-500-S; Ford permits a filter meeting SAE/USCAR-36, Filter Type C when Motorcraft is unavailable — Owner's Manual, p. 368.
- **Battery specification:** Motorcraft BAGM-48H6-760 — Owner's Manual, p. 368.
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Motorcraft SP-589; 0.049–0.053 in (1.25–1.35 mm) — Owner's Manual, pp. 363, 368.
- **Exterior bulb types:** All exterior lamps are LED assemblies and are not serviceable items — Owner's Manual, p. 312.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the Intelligent Oil-Life Monitor. Example ranges are 7,000–10,000 mi normal, 5,000–7,500 mi severe and 3,000–5,000 mi extreme; never exceed one year or 10,000 mi — Owner's Manual, pp. 436–438.
- **Brake/clutch fluid:** Replace brake fluid every 3 years. The manual does not publish a separate clutch-fluid replacement interval — Owner's Manual, p. 438.
- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 mi (48,000 km); dusty/sandy use requires more frequent inspection/replacement — Owner's Manual, pp. 438, 440–442.
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 20,000 mi (32,000 km); inspect/service more frequently in dusty or heavy-use conditions — Owner's Manual, pp. 438, 440–442.
- **Coolant:** Initial replacement at 10 years or 200,000 mi (320,000 km), then every 5 years or 100,000 mi (160,000 km) — Owner's Manual, p. 439.
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** The normal schedule lists manual-transmission fluid at 150,000 mi (240,000 km), trailer/car-top-carrier use at 60,000 mi (96,000 km), and dusty/sandy operation at 50,000 mi (80,000 km). The capacity section separately calls the transmission oil “filled-for-life” with no service requirement; treat this as an unresolved internal OEM-manual conflict and follow current VIN-specific Ford service information. Rear-axle fluid is listed at 200,000 mi (320,000 km), after submersion/overtemperature, and every 500 mi (800 km) for track use — Owner's Manual, pp. 377–378, 438–442.
- **Spark plugs:** Replace every 100,000 mi (160,000 km); every 60,000 mi (96,000 km) for trailer/car-top-carrier use or extensive idling/heavy-commercial use — Owner's Manual, pp. 438, 440.

## Variant warnings

- The manual's 2.5-US-qt figure is approximate total transmission volume/capacity; actual service fill can vary.
- **Source conflict:** the capacity section says manual-transmission oil is filled for life with no service requirement, while the scheduled-maintenance tables specify normal and severe replacement intervals. Do not resolve this conflict by inference; check current VIN-specific Ford service information.
- Campaign 24V394000 concerns a manual-clutch pressure line and therefore remains a VIN-specific recall check for this manual-transmission sheet; it is not a service specification.
- Ford's owner manual does not map EPA stop-start option IDs or distinguish Dark Horse/high-output service applicability in these tables. Confirm VIN, engine output and package before use.

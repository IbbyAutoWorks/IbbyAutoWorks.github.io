# 2024 Ford Maverick — 2.5L hybrid, eCVT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, high-voltage system, option codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** SAE 0W-20 meeting Ford WSS-M2C962-A1 — Owner's Manual, pp. 372–373.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 5.7 US qt (5.4 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 373.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Motorcraft Yellow Prediluted Antifreeze/Coolant meeting Ford WSS-M97B57-A2 for both circuits — Owner's Manual, p. 374.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 11.7 US qt (11.1 L) high-temperature circuit and 4.8 US qt (4.5 L) low-temperature circuit — Owner's Manual, p. 374. Keep the circuits distinct.
- **Transmission fluid:** Needs research; the owner manual's capacity tables do not publish an eCVT fluid specification.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Needs research.
- **Brake fluid:** Motorcraft DOT 4 LV High Performance Motor Vehicle Brake Fluid meeting Ford WSS-M6C65-A2 or ISO 4925 Class 6 — Owner's Manual, p. 379.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD hybrid sheet.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; do not assume a separate service fill for the hybrid transaxle.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD hybrid sheet.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 148 lb-ft (200 N·m), M14 × 1.5; retorque within 100 mi (160 km) after wheel disturbance — Owner's Manual, p. 365.
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire Label on the driver-side B-pillar or driver-door edge; the actual vehicle placard controls — Owner's Manual, p. 335.
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Follow the FWD diagram and exclude a dissimilar spare; check/adjust pressure afterward. Exact interval remains `Needs research` because this manual directs scheduled intervals to Ford's VIN-specific maintenance site — Owner's Manual, pp. 352–353, 456–459.

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Motorcraft FA-1948 — Owner's Manual, pp. 369–370.
- **Cabin air filter:** Motorcraft FP-89 — Owner's Manual, p. 370.
- **Oil filter:** Motorcraft FL-910-S; Ford permits a filter meeting SAE/USCAR-36, Filter Type B when Motorcraft is unavailable — Owner's Manual, pp. 369–370.
- **Battery specification:** 12V Motorcraft BXT-99RT4-A — Owner's Manual, p. 369. High-voltage traction-battery service specifications remain `Needs research` and are not represented by this 12V part number.
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Motorcraft SP-530; 0.049–0.053 in (1.25–1.35 mm) — Owner's Manual, pp. 368–369.
- **Exterior bulb types:** Replaceable 5W front side marker, 4257NA front turn, WT21-7W rear stop/tail/turn, WT21W reverse and 912 center cargo/high-mounted lamp are listed; headlamps, high-series rear side marker, license-plate, cargo-box and underhood lamps are non-serviceable LED assemblies — Owner's Manual, pp. 321–326.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the Intelligent Oil-Life Monitor; service within two weeks or 500 mi (800 km) of the message. The manual notes hybrids can exceed 10,000 mi, but also says never exceed one year or 10,000 mi between oil changes; follow the displayed message and current VIN-specific Ford schedule — Owner's Manual, pp. 456–457.

## Variant warnings

- This is the 2.5L hybrid/eCVT/FWD sheet; do not use 2.0L EcoBoost oil, coolant, filter, battery or spark-plug data.
- The high- and low-temperature cooling circuits have separate capacities.
- The listed battery is the 12V battery, not the high-voltage traction battery.
- Tire pressure must come from the actual vehicle placard; NHTSA campaign 24V266000 concerns incorrect labels on certain vehicles, so VIN recall status must also be checked.

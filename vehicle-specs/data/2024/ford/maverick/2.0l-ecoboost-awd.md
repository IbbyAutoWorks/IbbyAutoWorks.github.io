# 2024 Ford Maverick — 2.0L EcoBoost, 8-speed automatic, AWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, option codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** SAE 5W-30 meeting Ford WSS-M2C961-A1; SAE 0W-30 meeting WSS-M2C963-A1 is the manual's extremely-cold-climate alternative — Owner's Manual, pp. 371–372.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 5.5 US qt (5.2 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 371.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Motorcraft Yellow Prediluted Antifreeze/Coolant meeting Ford WSS-M97B57-A2 — Owner's Manual, pp. 373–374.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 9.0 US qt (8.5 L) without trailer-tow package; 9.5 US qt (9.02 L) with trailer-tow package — Owner's Manual, p. 373.
- **Transmission fluid:** Needs research; the exact-year owner manual does not publish the automatic-transmission fluid specification in its capacity tables.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Needs research.
- **Brake fluid:** Motorcraft DOT 4 LV High Performance Motor Vehicle Brake Fluid meeting Ford WSS-M6C65-A2 or ISO 4925 Class 6 — Owner's Manual, p. 379.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Needs research; verify the installed AWD power-transfer unit and current OEM service information.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; do not assume a separate service fill for the transaxle.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; verify the installed rear drive unit.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 148 lb-ft (200 N·m), M14 × 1.5; retorque within 100 mi (160 km) after wheel disturbance — Owner's Manual, p. 365.
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire Label on the driver-side B-pillar or driver-door edge; the actual vehicle placard controls — Owner's Manual, p. 335.
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Follow the four-tire diagram and exclude a dissimilar spare; check/adjust pressure afterward. Exact interval remains `Needs research` because this manual directs scheduled intervals to Ford's VIN-specific maintenance site — Owner's Manual, pp. 352–353, 456–459.

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Motorcraft FA-1939 — Owner's Manual, p. 369.
- **Cabin air filter:** Motorcraft FP-89 — Owner's Manual, p. 369.
- **Oil filter:** Motorcraft FL-910-S; Ford permits a filter meeting SAE/USCAR-36, Filter Type B when Motorcraft is unavailable — Owner's Manual, p. 369.
- **Battery specification:** Motorcraft BAGM-48H6-760; configure the battery-management system for the replacement battery — Owner's Manual, p. 369.
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Motorcraft SP-594; 0.028–0.031 in (0.7–0.8 mm) — Owner's Manual, pp. 367, 369.
- **Exterior bulb types:** Replaceable 5W front side marker, 4257NA front turn, WT21-7W rear stop/tail/turn, WT21W reverse and 912 center cargo/high-mounted lamp are listed; headlamps, high-series rear side marker, license-plate, cargo-box and underhood lamps are non-serviceable LED assemblies — Owner's Manual, pp. 321–326.

## Variant warnings

- This sheet is AWD only. The owner manual does not publish service-fill specifications/capacities for the AWD power-transfer or rear-drive units; do not borrow Escape or Bronco Sport values.
- Cooling capacity depends on trailer-tow equipment.
- Tire pressure must come from the actual vehicle placard; NHTSA campaign 24V266000 concerns incorrect labels on certain vehicles, so VIN recall status must also be checked.

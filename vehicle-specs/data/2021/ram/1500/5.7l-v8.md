# 2021 Ram 1500 — 5.7L HEMI V8 gasoline

> Verify the VIN, engine, eTorque/Stop-Start equipment, transmission, drivetrain, transfer-case model, axle ratio, limited-slip/Max Tow equipment, body configuration, option codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** API-certified SAE 5W-20 meeting FCA Material Standard MS-6395 — Owner's Manual, pp. 310 and 361.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 7.0 qt (6.6 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 360.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Mopar 10-Year/150,000-Mile OAT coolant meeting MS.90032; maintain at least a 50/50 mixture with distilled/deionized water — Owner's Manual, pp. 319–320 and 361.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 18.3 qt (17.3 L) for the 5.7L engine — Owner's Manual, p. 360.
- **Transmission fluid:** Mopar ZF 8 & 9 Speed ATF, or an equivalent meeting the manufacturer's requirement — Owner's Manual, p. 363.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Needs research; the owner manual states that level checking requires special service tools and does not publish a service-fill quantity — Owner's Manual, p. 322.
- **Brake fluid:** Mopar DOT 3 brake fluid; the owner-manual table is the authority. The SAE suffix was omitted because the text extraction was not clear enough for a safe transcription — Owner's Manual, pp. 322 and 363.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** 48-11 active on-demand two-speed (4WD Auto): Mobilfluid LT; 48-12 part-time two-speed (without 4WD Auto): Shell Spirax S2 ATF A389. Confirm transfer-case model; capacity needs research. Not applicable to 2WD — Owner's Manual, p. 363.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Mopar GL-5 synthetic axle lubricant SAE 75W-85; capacity needs research. Not applicable to 2WD — Owner's Manual, p. 363.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** 3.21/3.55 axle: Mopar synthetic SAE 75W-90, MS-A0160; 3.92 axle: Mopar synthetic SAE 75W-140, MS-8985; Max Tow 3.92 axle: Dana SAE 80W-90. Limited-slip axles require 5 oz (148 mL) Mopar Limited Slip Additive MS-10111. Main axle capacity needs research — Owner's Manual, pp. 322 and 363.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 130 lb-ft (176 N·m) for M14 x 1.50 wheel nuts; torque in the specified star pattern — Owner's Manual, p. 353.
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire and Loading Information placard on the driver-side B-pillar/rear edge of the driver door; check all tires including the spare at least monthly — Owner's Manual, pp. 339 and 342.
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate at every oil-change interval and at the first sign of irregular wear; the suggested non-directional pattern is rearward cross — Owner's Manual, pp. 299 and 348.

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Use a high-quality Mopar-certified filter; inspect for dirt/debris and replace as required. Exact service part number needs research — Owner's Manual, pp. 311–312.
- **Cabin air filter:** Located behind the glove compartment; exact service part number needs research — Owner's Manual, pp. 314–316.
- **Oil filter:** Full-flow disposable filter; Mopar is recommended, and alternatives should meet or exceed SAE/USCAR-36. Exact service part number needs research — Owner's Manual, pp. 311 and 361.
- **Battery specification:** Maintenance-free 12-volt battery. Confirm the installed battery label and whether the truck has eTorque/Stop-Start before ordering; exact group/rating needs research — Owner's Manual, pp. 306–309.
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Needs research; the inspected owner manual provides a replacement interval but not a verified part number or gap for this sheet.
- **Exterior bulbs:** Halogen reflector low beam H11LL; high beam 9005LL; front turn/position 7444NA; front side marker W5W; halogen fog H11LL; base rear tail/turn/stop 7440LL/W21WLL; base backup 7440/W21W; base rear turn 7440NA/WY21W; CHMSL and cargo lamps 921. LED reflector/projector headlamps, LED front position/marker/fog, mirror indicators, premium rear lamps and license-plate lamps are LED assemblies serviced by an authorized dealer — Owner's Manual, pp. 331–332.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the automatic oil-change indicator and service within 500 mi (805 km) of the message. Never exceed 10,000 mi (16,000 km), 12 months or 350 engine hours, whichever comes first — Owner's Manual, p. 299.
- **At every oil-change interval:** Change oil/filter; rotate tires; inspect battery terminals, CV/universal joints, brakes, cooling system, exhaust and the engine air cleaner in dusty/off-road use — Owner's Manual, p. 299.
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 20,000 mi (32,000 km) under the gasoline-engine maintenance plan — Owner's Manual, pp. 300–301.
- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 mi (48,000 km) under the gasoline-engine maintenance plan; inspect more often in dusty/off-road use — Owner's Manual, pp. 299–301.
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 100,000 mi (160,000 km); the interval is mileage-based rather than time-based — Owner's Manual, pp. 300–301.
- **Coolant:** Flush and replace at 10 years or 150,000 mi (240,000 km), whichever comes first — Owner's Manual, pp. 300 and 320.
- **Transmission:** Under normal operation, routine fluid/filter changes are not required; change if contaminated or when the transmission is disassembled — Owner's Manual, p. 322.
- **Axles/transfer case:** Inspect axle surfaces for leakage. Police, taxi, fleet, off-road and frequent trailer-towing use requires additional axle/transfer-case inspection or fluid changes under the maintenance plan; exact mileage marks need a visual table check before use — Owner's Manual, pp. 300–301 and 322–323.

## Variant warnings

- The 5.7L engine was offered with and without eTorque/Stop-Start; confirm the installed system before battery, belt or cooling-system work.
- Transfer-case fluid depends on whether the truck has 4WD Auto (48-11) or the part-time case (48-12); 2WD has no transfer case.
- Rear-axle lubricant depends on axle ratio and Max Tow equipment; limited-slip units also require the specified additive.
- Tire pressure always comes from the actual vehicle placard.

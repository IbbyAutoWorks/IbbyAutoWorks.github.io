# 2021 Nissan Rogue — 2.5L PR25DD, CVT, AWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, option/RPO codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Nissan Motor Oil 0W-20 SN or equivalent; the manual permits synthetic 0W-20 GF-5 SN when the recommended oil is unavailable — Owner's Manual, pp. 10-3, 10-7.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 5-3/8 qt (5.1 L; 4-1/2 Imp. qt.) — Owner's Manual, p. 10-3.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Pre-diluted Genuine Nissan Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant (blue) or equivalent, using a 50/50 antifreeze and demineralized/distilled-water mixture — Owner's Manual, pp. 8-4–8-5, 9-33, 10-3.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 9-1/4 qt (8.8 L; 7-3/4 Imp. qt.) including the reservoir; reservoir listed as 7/8 qt (0.8 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 10-3.
- **Transmission fluid:** Genuine Nissan CVT Fluid NS-3 or equivalent only; do not mix with other fluids — Owner's Manual, p. 10-3.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Needs research; the owner manual does not publish a CVT refill quantity.
- **Brake fluid:** Genuine Nissan Super Heavy Duty Brake Fluid or equivalent DOT 3 — Owner's Manual, p. 10-3.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Genuine Nissan Differential Oil Hypoid Super-S GL-5 synthetic 75W-90 or equivalent; capacity needs research — Owner's Manual, p. 10-3.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research. The owner manual lists AWD differential gear oil but does not establish a separate front-differential service fill for this exact configuration.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** The owner manual lists Genuine Nissan Hypoid Fluid S1 GL-5 75W-80 or equivalent for differential gear oil and LSC Transmission Fluid 12-301 or equivalent for coupling oil; exact component mapping and capacities need current Nissan service information — Owner's Manual, pp. 10-3–10-4.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 83 lb-ft (113 N·m) — Owner's Manual, p. 6-10.
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire and Loading Information label on the driver-side center pillar; it is the authority for the installed tires — Owner's Manual, pp. 6-10, 8-31.
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Perform every 5,000 mi (8,000 km) or 6 months; exact pattern needs confirmation for the installed tire configuration — Owner's Manual, maintenance schedule pp. 9-8 onward.

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Exact part number needs research; normal replacement every 30,000 mi (48,000 km), sooner in dusty use — Owner's Manual, p. 9-10.
- **Cabin air filter:** Exact part number needs research; replace every 15,000 mi (24,000 km) or 18 months — Owner's Manual, p. 9-9 and repeated schedule entries.
- **Oil filter:** Exact part number needs research.
- **Battery specification:** Exact group size, CCA and reserve capacity need research; the jump-start section requires a 12-volt booster battery but does not prove the installed battery rating — Owner's Manual, p. 6-11.
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Exact plug part number and installation gap need research. Replace at 105,000 mi (168,000 km); the maintenance note also calls for replacement if measured gap exceeds 0.053 in (1.35 mm) before the mileage interval — Owner's Manual, p. 9-18.
- **Exterior bulb types:** LED high/low headlamp assemblies; front turn signal 7444NA or LED; W5W front/rear side markers; W21W rear turn signals; LED stop/tail and high-mounted stop lamps; W16W reverse bulbs; W5W license-plate lamp assembly — Owner's Manual, p. 8-29. Confirm installed lamp options before ordering.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the Oil Control System; do not exceed 10,000 mi (16,000 km) or one year for the PR25DD. When the indicator appears, change within two weeks or 500 mi (800 km) — Owner's Manual, Oil Control System section and maintenance schedule pp. 9-8 onward.
- **Brake fluid:** Normal schedule replaces every 20,000 mi (32,000 km) or 24 months; severe schedule replaces every 10,000 mi (16,000 km) or 12 months — Owner's Manual, maintenance schedule pp. 9-8 onward.
- **Engine air filter:** Every 30,000 mi (48,000 km) under normal conditions; more frequently in dusty operation — Owner's Manual, p. 9-10.
- **Cabin air filter:** Every 15,000 mi (24,000 km) or 18 months — Owner's Manual, p. 9-9 and repeated schedule entries.
- **Coolant:** First replacement at 105,000 mi (168,000 km) or 84 months; thereafter every 75,000 mi (120,000 km) or 60 months — Owner's Manual, pp. 9-18, 9-33.
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Inspect CVT, differential, coupling and transfer fluids every 10,000 mi (16,000 km) or 12 months. Severe schedule replaces CVT fluid every 60,000 mi (96,000 km) or 72 months; the owner schedule does not prove fixed replacement intervals for the AWD axle/coupling/transfer fluids — Owner's Manual, maintenance schedule pp. 9-8–9-20.
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 105,000 mi (168,000 km) — Owner's Manual, p. 9-18.

## Variant warnings

- AWD only; confirm the installed AWD rear-drive/coupling hardware before ordering differential or coupling fluid.
- The owner manual names AWD fluids but does not publish service-fill capacities or fully map each named fluid to every front/rear housing; use current VIN-specific Nissan service information.
- This is the PR25DD 2.5L engine, not the KR15DDT 1.5L turbo powertrain appearing in later supplement material.
- Tire pressure always comes from the actual vehicle placard.

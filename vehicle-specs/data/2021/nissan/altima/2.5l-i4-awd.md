# 2021 Nissan Altima — 2.5L PR25DD, CVT, AWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, option/RPO codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Nissan Motor Oil 0W-20 SN or equivalent; when unavailable, the manual permits synthetic 0W-20 GF-5 SN — Owner's Manual, p. 10-2.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 5-3/8 qt (5.1 L; 4-1/2 Imp. qt.), drain and refill — Owner's Manual, p. 10-2.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Pre-diluted Genuine Nissan Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant (blue) or equivalent; factory fill is a 50/50 coolant-and-water mixture — Owner's Manual, pp. 8-4–8-5 and 10-2.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 2-1/4 gal (8.3 L; 1-7/8 Imp. gal.) including the reservoir — Owner's Manual, p. 10-2.
- **Transmission fluid:** Genuine Nissan CVT Fluid NS-3 or equivalent only; do not mix with other fluids — Owner's Manual, p. 10-3.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Needs research; the owner manual does not publish a CVT refill quantity.
- **Brake fluid:** Genuine Nissan Super Heavy Duty Brake Fluid or equivalent DOT 3 — Owner's Manual, p. 10-3.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Needs research; no exact AWD service fluid or quantity is added until confirmed in exact Nissan service information.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; the owner manual does not establish a separate front-final-drive service specification for this AWD/CVT configuration.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; no exact AWD service fluid or quantity is added until confirmed in exact Nissan service information.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 83 lb-ft (113 N·m) — Owner's Manual, pp. 6-10 and 8-38.
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire and Loading Information label on the driver-side center pillar; it is the authority for the installed tires — Owner's Manual, p. 6-10.
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate every 5,000 mi (8,000 km); do not include the spare. Confirm the pattern for the installed tire configuration — Owner's Manual, p. 8-38 and maintenance schedule beginning p. 9-10.

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Exact part number needs research; normal schedule replacement is every 30,000 mi (48,000 km), with more frequent service in dusty conditions — Owner's Manual, pp. 9-13–9-14.
- **Cabin air filter:** Exact part number needs research; the normal schedule replaces the in-cabin microfilter every 10,000 mi (16,000 km) or 12 months — Owner's Manual, p. 9-11 and later repeated schedule entries.
- **Oil filter:** Exact part number needs research.
- **Battery specification:** Exact group size, cold-cranking rating and reserve capacity need research; the manual establishes a 12-volt jump-start source but does not prove the installed battery rating — Owner's Manual, p. 6-11.
- **Spark plugs and gap:** DXE22H11C iridium-tipped plugs; nominal gap 0.043 in (1.1 mm). Do not clean or regap used iridium-tipped plugs — Owner's Manual, pp. 8-17 and 10-9.
- **Exterior bulb types:** Type A headlamps use H11 55 W low beam, HB3 60 W high beam and W5W side markers. Type B low/high beam, park/daytime-running and side-marker lamps are LED assemblies. Other listed replaceable lamps include 7444NA front turn/park, W21/5W rear stop/tail, W21W rear turn, W5W rear side marker, 921 reverse and W5W backup-assembly tail bulbs; fog, mirror-turn, high-mounted-stop and some other lamps are listed as dealer-service/LED assemblies — Owner's Manual, p. 8-26. Confirm the installed lamp package before ordering.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the Oil Control System and never exceed 10,000 mi (16,000 km) or one year for the PR25DD. When the indicator appears, change within two weeks or 500 mi (800 km). If the indicator is reset prematurely or becomes inoperative, change within 5,000 mi (8,000 km) or six months of the last service — Owner's Manual, Oil Control System section and pp. 9-7–9-10.
- **Brake fluid:** Normal schedule replacement every 20,000 mi (32,000 km) or 24 months; severe schedule replacement every 10,000 mi (16,000 km) or 12 months — Owner's Manual, pp. 9-11–9-12.
- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 mi (48,000 km) under the normal schedule; inspect or replace more often in dusty operation — Owner's Manual, pp. 9-6 and 9-13–9-14.
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 10,000 mi (16,000 km) or 12 months under the normal schedule; replace more frequently in prolonged dusty operation — Owner's Manual, pp. 9-6 and 9-11 onward.
- **Coolant:** Initial replacement at 105,000 mi (168,000 km) or 84 months; thereafter every 75,000 mi (120,000 km) or 60 months — Owner's Manual, p. 9-40.
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Inspect CVT fluid, transfer oil and differential gear oil every 10,000 mi (16,000 km) or 12 months under the published schedule; severe-use CVT-fluid replacement is listed at 60,000 mi (96,000 km) or 72 months — Owner's Manual, pp. 9-11–9-18. Exact AWD service fluids and quantities remain unresolved.
- **Spark plugs:** Replace every 105,000 mi (168,000 km); the maintenance note also calls for replacement if measured gap exceeds 0.049 in (1.25 mm) before the periodic interval — Owner's Manual, p. 9-40.

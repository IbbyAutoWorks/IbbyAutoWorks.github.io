## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the Intelligent Oil Life Monitor; normal use is typically 7,500–10,000 mi, and do not exceed 1 year or 10,000 mi — Ford online normal scheduled maintenance.
- **Brake fluid:** Every 3 years — Ford online normal scheduled maintenance.
- **Engine air filter:** Every 30,000 mi under the normal schedule; inspect/replace more often in dusty conditions — Ford online normal scheduled maintenance.
- **Cabin air filter:** Every 20,000 mi under the normal schedule — Ford online normal scheduled maintenance.

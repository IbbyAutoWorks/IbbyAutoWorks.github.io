# 2021 Ford F-150 — 3.5L EcoBoost V6, 10-speed automatic, 4x4

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, option/RPO codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** SAE 5W-30; Ford WSS-M2C961-A1; API SP — Owner's Manual, p. 593.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 6.0 qt (5.7 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 593.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Motorcraft Yellow Prediluted Antifreeze/Coolant; Ford WSS-M97B57-A2 — Owner's Manual, p. 600.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 14.3 qt (13.5 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 600.
- **Transmission fluid:** Needs research for the exact 10-speed service specification and procedure.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Needs research.
- **Brake fluid:** Motorcraft DOT 4 LV High Performance Brake Fluid; Ford WSS-M6C65-A2 / ISO 4925 Class 6 — Owner's Manual, p. 613.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Motorcraft MERCON LV ATF, WSS-M2C938-A; 1.5 qt (1.4 L) for electronic-shift-on-the-fly or torque-on-demand units, or 1.9 qt (1.8 L) for the two-speed mechanical-lock unit — Owner's Manual, p. 614. Confirm the installed transfer case before use.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Motorcraft SAE 75W-85 Premium Synthetic Hypoid Gear Lubricant, WSS-M2C942-A; standard front axle 1.8 qt (1.7 L), or Torsen limited-slip axle 1.64 qt (1.55 L) including 4.0 fl oz (118.5 ml) friction modifier meeting EST-M2C118-A — Owner's Manual, p. 616. Confirm axle equipment.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Motorcraft SAE 75W-85 Premium Synthetic Hypoid Gear Lubricant, WSS-M2C942-A; capacity depends on verified axle size: 8.8-inch 2.0–2.1 qt or 9.75-inch 2.2–2.3 qt — Owner's Manual, p. 616. Confirm axle before use.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 150 lb-ft (204 N·m); re-torque within 100 mi (160 km) after wheel disturbance — Owner's Manual, p. 572.
- **Cold tire pressure:** Check the vehicle Tire and Loading Information placard; cab, tire and payload packages vary — Owner's Manual, tire-pressure section.
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate when indicated by scheduled maintenance/oil-life service; exact pattern depends on tire configuration. Needs research for the diagram applicable to the installed tires.

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Motorcraft FA-1883 — Owner's Manual, p. 574.
- **Cabin air filter:** Motorcraft FP-79 — Owner's Manual, p. 574.
- **Oil filter:** Motorcraft FL-500-S; if unavailable, use a filter meeting SAE/USCAR-36 — Owner's Manual, p. 574.
- **Battery specification:** Standard Motorcraft BAGM-48H6-760; optional BAGM-94RH7-800 — Owner's Manual, p. 574. Confirm installed option.
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Motorcraft SP-578; 0.028–0.031 in (0.7–0.8 mm) — Owner's Manual, pp. 573–574.
- **Exterior bulb types:** Needs research; identify replaceable bulbs versus LED assemblies by lamp option.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the Intelligent Oil Life Monitor; normal use is typically 7,500–10,000 mi, and do not exceed 1 year or 10,000 mi — Ford online normal scheduled maintenance.
- **Brake fluid:** Every 3 years — Ford online normal scheduled maintenance.
- **Engine air filter:** Every 30,000 mi under the normal schedule; inspect/replace more often in dusty conditions — Ford online normal scheduled maintenance.
- **Cabin air filter:** Every 20,000 mi under the normal schedule — Ford online normal scheduled maintenance.
- **Coolant:** Needs research from the exact normal/severe interval table.
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Needs research for normal versus severe/towing service; fluid families and full capacities are listed above, but installed driveline variants must be identified before service.
- **Spark plugs:** Needs research from the exact normal/severe interval table.

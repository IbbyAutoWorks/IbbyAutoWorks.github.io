# 2021 Ford Escape — 2.0L EcoBoost, 8-speed automatic, AWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, option/RPO codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** SAE 5W-30 meeting Ford WSS-M2C961-A1; Ford also requires the recommended viscosity to meet API SP and display the API gasoline-engine certification mark. SAE 0W-30 meeting WSS-M2C963-A1 is the listed alternative at ambient temperatures of -22°F (-30°C) or below — Owner's Manual, pp. 397–398.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 5.5 qt (5.2 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 398.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Motorcraft Yellow Prediluted Antifreeze/Coolant, Ford WSS-M97B57-A2 — Owner's Manual, p. 401.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 8.7 qt (8.2 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 401.
- **Transmission fluid:** Needs research; the exact 8-speed fluid specification is not published in the inspected owner-manual capacity tables.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Needs research; the owner manual does not publish a drain-and-fill quantity.
- **Brake fluid:** DOT 4 LV high-performance brake fluid meeting Ford WSS-M6C65-A2; fill as required — Owner's Manual, p. 407.
- **Transfer case / power transfer unit fluid and capacity:** Needs research; the owner manual identifies the AWD power-transfer-unit lubricant as a service concern after submersion but does not publish an exact fluid specification or capacity — Owner's Manual, AWD section p. 218.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; no separate front-differential specification or capacity is published in the inspected owner manual.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; the owner manual requires rear-axle inspection for AWD but does not publish the exact lubricant or capacity — Owner's Manual, pp. 490 and 492.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 100 lb-ft (135 N·m) for M12 x 1.5 wheel nuts, with clean/rust-free threads; retighten within 100 mi (160 km) after wheel disturbance — Owner's Manual, p. 390.
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire Label on the B-pillar or driver-door edge for the installed tire size; the vehicle placard is the authority — Owner's Manual, pp. 360, 372 and 382–383.
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate at every Intelligent Oil-Life Monitor oil-change interval; for dusty or sandy use rotate every 5,000 mi (8,000 km). Follow the owner-manual diagram, keep AWD tire size/wear matched, and reset/verify TPMS settings where required — Owner's Manual, pp. 367, 374 and 490–493.

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Motorcraft FA-1939; normal replacement every 30,000 mi (48,000 km), with more frequent inspection/replacement in dusty or dirty operation — Owner's Manual, pp. 343, 394, 491 and 493.
- **Cabin air filter:** Motorcraft FP-89; normal replacement every 20,000 mi (32,000 km), more frequently in dusty/dirty operation — Owner's Manual, pp. 147, 394, 491 and 493.
- **Oil filter:** Motorcraft FL-910-S; an alternative must meet SAE/USCAR-36 — Owner's Manual, p. 394.
- **Battery specification:** Motorcraft BAGM-48H6-760 — Owner's Manual, p. 394. Confirm current supersession and installed electrical requirements before ordering.
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Motorcraft SP-578; gap 0.028–0.031 in (0.7–0.8 mm) — Owner's Manual, pp. 392 and 394.
- **Exterior bulb types:** Base headlamp low beam H9; base high beam HB3; base front turn T20 amber; base side marker LED; optional headlamp LED; base fog H8; optional fog LED; tail/brake LED; rear turn T20 amber; reverse T20; rear side marker W5W; license plate and high-mount brake lamps LED. Confirm the installed lamp package before ordering — Owner's Manual, p. 349.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the Intelligent Oil-Life Monitor; normal examples are 7,500–10,000 mi, severe 5,000–7,500 mi and extreme 3,000–5,000 mi. Change within two weeks or 500 mi (800 km) of the message and never exceed one year or 10,000 mi (16,000 km). Dusty/sandy operation calls for 5,000 mi (8,000 km) or six months — Owner's Manual, pp. 487, 490 and 493.
- **Brake fluid:** Replace every 3 years — Owner's Manual, p. 490.
- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 mi (48,000 km) normally; inspect/replace more frequently in dusty or dirty conditions — Owner's Manual, pp. 491 and 493.
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 20,000 mi (32,000 km) normally; inspect/replace more frequently in dusty or dirty conditions — Owner's Manual, pp. 491 and 493.
- **Coolant:** First replacement at 10 years or 200,000 mi (322,000 km), then every 5 years or 100,000 mi (160,000 km) — Owner's Manual, p. 491.
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Inspect automatic-transmission fluid level and AWD rear axle at each oil-change interval. Normal transmission-fluid replacement is 150,000 mi (240,000 km); towing, commercial-idling/low-speed, and dusty/sandy schedules call for 30,000 mi (48,000 km). Under towing, inspect the rear axle frequently and service as required. Exact rear-axle and power-transfer-unit fluid specifications/capacities remain unresolved — Owner's Manual, pp. 490–493.
- **Spark plugs:** Replace every 100,000 mi (160,000 km) normally; every 60,000 mi (96,000 km) for towing or heavy commercial idling/low-speed use — Owner's Manual, pp. 491–492.

## Variant warnings

- All-wheel drive only; do not apply this sheet to a 1.5L EcoBoost, hybrid, plug-in hybrid, or any materially different powertrain.
- AWD tire size and wear must remain appropriately matched; verify the installed set before rotation or replacement.
- Tire pressure always comes from the actual vehicle placard.
- Exact 8-speed transmission, PTU and rear-axle fluid specifications/capacities remain unresolved rather than inferred.

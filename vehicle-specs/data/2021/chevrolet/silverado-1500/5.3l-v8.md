# 2021 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 — 5.3L V8 gasoline

> Verify the VIN, engine RPO (L82/L84), transmission, drivetrain, axle ratio, cooling package, body configuration, option/RPO codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Full-synthetic SAE 0W-20 meeting dexos1 — Owner's Manual, pp. 330 and 418.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 8.0 qt (7.6 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 425.
- **Coolant type/specification:** 50/50 mixture of clean drinkable water and DEX-COOL coolant meeting GM Standard GMW3420 — Owner's Manual, pp. 335 and 418.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** L82: 13.5 qt (12.8 L); L84: 13.8 qt (13.1 L). Confirm the engine RPO before filling — Owner's Manual, p. 424.
- **Transmission fluid:** 6-speed: DEXRON-VI; 8-speed: DEXRON-HP; 10-speed: DEXRON ULV. Confirm the installed transmission RPO before selecting fluid — Owner's Manual, p. 418.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Needs research; the inspected owner manual does not publish a service-fill quantity.
- **Brake fluid:** GM-approved DOT 4 hydraulic brake fluid from a clean, sealed container — Owner's Manual, pp. 342 and 418.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Four-wheel-drive transfer case uses DEXRON-VI; approximate capacity 1.6 qt (1.5 L). Not applicable to 2WD — Owner's Manual, pp. 419 and 426.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Official owner manual directs the reader to the dealer for front-axle lubricant; exact specification and capacity need research — Owner's Manual, p. 418.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Official owner manual directs the reader to the dealer for rear-axle lubricant; exact specification and capacity need research — Owner's Manual, p. 418.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 140 lb-ft (190 N·m) — Owner's Manual, p. 426.
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire and Loading Information label on the driver-side B-pillar/door edge for the installed tires; the placard is the authority, not a generic pressure — Owner's Manual, Tire Pressure and Vehicle Load Limits sections.
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate tires and perform the required services every 7,500 mi (12,000 km), if rotation is recommended for the installed tires; follow the owner-manual rotation guidance and TPMS relearn requirements — Owner's Manual, pp. 410–414.

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Standard-capacity system: GM 84121217 / ACDelco A3246C; high-capacity system: GM 84121219 / ACDelco A3244C. Confirm the installed air-cleaner option — Owner's Manual, p. 419.
- **Cabin air filter:** GM 13508023 / ACDelco CF185 — Owner's Manual, p. 420.
- **Oil filter:** GM 12690385 / ACDelco PF63E for both listed 5.3L V8 engines — Owner's Manual, p. 420.
- **Battery specification:** The original-equipment battery is maintenance-free; use the replacement number on the installed battery label. L84 Stop/Start vehicles require an AGM 12-volt battery rather than a conventional battery — Owner's Manual, pp. 342–343.
- **Spark plugs and gap:** GM 12622441 / ACDelco 41-114; preset gap 0.037–0.043 in (0.95–1.10 mm). The manual says re-gapping is not recommended — Owner's Manual, pp. 420 and 426.
- **Exterior bulb types:** Needs research; lamp construction varies by trim and lighting package, and no exact package-specific bulb table was verified during this run.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the engine-oil-life system. Change the oil and filter within 600 mi (1,000 km) of the `CHANGE ENGINE OIL SOON` message and at least once every 12 months; reset the system after service — Owner's Manual, pp. 331 and 409.
- **Tire rotation/required service:** Every 7,500 mi (12,000 km), subject to the installed tire's rotation eligibility — Owner's Manual, pp. 408 and 410–414.
- **Cabin air filter:** Inspect every 22,500 mi (36,000 km) or two years, whichever comes first; replace when necessary and more often for dust, heavy traffic or poor air quality — Owner's Manual, p. 410.
- **Engine air filter:** Use the engine-air-filter life monitor where equipped; otherwise inspect and replace per the maintenance schedule. The exact fixed fallback mileage needs a clearer exact-year service-table extraction — Owner's Manual, pp. 332–333 and 409–414.
- **Brake fluid:** Replace every five years — Owner's Manual, pp. 413 and 415.
- **Coolant:** Drain and fill at the maintenance-schedule event or every five years, whichever comes first; the coolant itself is described as 5-year/150,000-mi coolant — Owner's Manual, pp. 335 and 413–415.
- **Transmission/transfer-case fluids:** Normal and severe schedules differ. Severe operation includes automatic-transmission fluid/filter service, and 4WD schedules include transfer-case service; exact mileage-column mapping remains `Needs research` because the inspected table extraction did not preserve the marks reliably — Owner's Manual, pp. 412–415.
- **Spark plugs:** The owner manual includes separate scheduled replacement rows for the gasoline engines; exact mileage-column mapping remains `Needs research` pending a visually checked table extraction — Owner's Manual, pp. 412–415.

## Variant warnings

- The 5.3L L82 and L84 are not interchangeable identities: coolant capacity differs, and the L84 Stop/Start configuration requires AGM-battery handling.
- Six-, eight- and ten-speed transmissions require different fluids; confirm the transmission RPO before service.
- Transfer-case data applies only to 4WD; axle lubricant/capacity remains unresolved rather than inferred.
- Tire pressure always comes from the actual vehicle placard.

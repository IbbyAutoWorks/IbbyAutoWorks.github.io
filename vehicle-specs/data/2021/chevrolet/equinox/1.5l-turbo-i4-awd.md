# 2021 Chevrolet Equinox — 1.5L LYX turbo inline-4, 6-speed automatic, AWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, option/RPO codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** SAE 0W-20 engine oil meeting the dexos1 specification; ACDelco dexos1 full synthetic recommended — Owner's Manual, pp. 256, 331.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 5.3 qt (5.0 L) for AWD — Owner's Manual, p. 335.
- **Coolant type/specification:** 50/50 mixture of clean drinkable water and DEX-COOL coolant — Owner's Manual, p. 331.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 7.0 qt (6.6 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 335.
- **Transmission fluid:** DEXRON-VI automatic transmission fluid — Owner's Manual, p. 331.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Needs research.
- **Brake fluid:** DOT 3 hydraulic brake fluid — Owner's Manual, p. 331.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** GM transfer-case fluid; owner manual directs the fluid selection to the dealer. Approximate capacity 0.53 qt (0.5 L) — Owner's Manual, pp. 331, 336. Exact fluid part/spec needs research before service.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Owner manual directs AWD rear-axle fluid selection to the dealer; capacity needs research — Owner's Manual, p. 331.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 100 lb-ft (140 N·m) — Owner's Manual, p. 336.
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire and Loading Information label/vehicle placard; it controls for the installed tires — Owner's Manual, pp. 285, 290.
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Every 7,500 mi (12,000 km); use the manual's illustrated front-to-rear cross pattern and reset TPMS positions as required — Owner's Manual, pp. 290, 324.

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** GM 84390002 / ACDelco A3240C — Owner's Manual, p. 332.
- **Cabin air filter:** GM 13508023 / ACDelco CF185 — Owner's Manual, p. 332.
- **Oil filter:** GM 12696048 / ACDelco PF64 — Owner's Manual, p. 332.
- **Battery specification:** 12-volt negative-ground system; exact group size/CCA needs research — Owner's Manual, jump-start section.
- **Spark plugs and gap:** GM 12683541 / ACDelco 41-156; factory gap 0.024–0.028 in (0.60–0.70 mm), and re-gapping is not recommended — Owner's Manual, pp. 332, 336.
- **Exterior bulb types:** Needs research; identify replaceable bulbs versus LED assemblies by lamp option.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the oil-life system; change within 600 mi of the warning and at least once yearly — Owner's Manual, p. 323.
- **Brake fluid:** Every 5 years — Owner's Manual, p. 326.
- **Engine air filter:** Follow the air-filter life system when equipped; otherwise replace every 4 years, with inspection at each oil change in dusty conditions — Owner's Manual, pp. 324–326.
- **Cabin air filter:** Every 2 years under the schedule, sooner for heavy traffic, dust, poor air quality, allergens, reduced airflow, fogging or odor — Owner's Manual, pp. 325–326.
- **Coolant:** Every 5 years — Owner's Manual, p. 326.
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Normal schedule calls for rear-axle fluid changes at marked mileage points on AWD; severe schedule additionally marks automatic-transmission fluid changes. Exact table mileages remain `Needs research` — Owner's Manual, pp. 325–328.
- **Spark plugs:** Scheduled at the marked normal/severe mileage point; exact mileage extraction remains `Needs research` — Owner's Manual, pp. 325–328.

## Variant warnings

- AWD only. The AWD oil capacity differs from FWD.
- Do not service the transfer case or rear axle from the generic manual description alone; obtain the exact GM fluid specification/current service information first.
- Tire pressure always comes from the actual vehicle placard.

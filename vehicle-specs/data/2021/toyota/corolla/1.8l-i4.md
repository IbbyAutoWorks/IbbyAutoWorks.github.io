# 2021 Toyota Corolla — 1.8L inline-4, CVT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, option/RPO codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** ILSAC GF-5 multigrade engine oil; SAE 0W-20 — Owner's Manual, p. 558.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.4 qt (4.2 L; 3.7 Imp. qt.) — Owner's Manual, p. 558.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Toyota Super Long Life Coolant or a similar high-quality ethylene-glycol, non-silicate/non-amine/non-nitrite/non-borate coolant with long-life hybrid organic-acid technology — Owner's Manual, p. 561.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** Needs research; no exact value is added until the source is confirmed for this exact 1.8L/CVT variant.
- **Transmission fluid:** Toyota Genuine CVT Fluid FE — Owner's Manual, p. 562.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Needs research; the owner manual does not establish a drain-and-fill quantity.
- **Brake fluid:** FMVSS No. 116 DOT 3/SAE J1703 or DOT 4/SAE J1704 — Owner's Manual, p. 564.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this front-wheel-drive sheet.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; the owner manual does not state a separate service quantity for the CVT/front final drive.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this front-wheel-drive sheet.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 76 lb-ft (103 N·m; 10.5 kgf·m) — Owner's Manual, p. 564.
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire and Loading Information label on the driver-side door pillar; the vehicle placard controls for the installed tires — Owner's Manual, tire-information-label section.
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate every 5,000 mi or 6 months; use the pattern applicable to the installed tire configuration — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–54.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** With 0W-20 oil, replace every 10,000 mi/12 months under the normal schedule. Replace every 5,000 mi/6 months when the guide's special operating conditions apply, including repeated short trips in freezing weather, extensive idling/low-speed use, heavy loading/towing or extensive dusty/dirt-road use — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 33, 35 and 38–54.
- **Brake fluid:** Needs research; the inspected maintenance guide does not publish a routine time/mileage replacement interval.
- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 mi/36 months under the normal schedule; inspect more often under listed dusty/dirt-road conditions — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 43, 47, 50 and 54.
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 30,000 mi/36 months under the normal schedule; inspect or replace more often under listed dusty/dirt-road conditions — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 43, 47, 50 and 54.
- **Coolant:** Initial replacement at 100,000 mi/120 months, then every 50,000 mi/60 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, p. 35 and 100,000-mile schedule.
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Inspect CVT fluid as scheduled; replace at 60,000 mi/72 months when the guide's heavy-loading/towing special operating condition applies. Transfer case and rear differential are not applicable to this FWD sheet — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 35 and 47.
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 120,000 mi/144 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, p. 54.

## Variant warnings

- This sheet is limited to the EPA 42582 1.8L gasoline Corolla with automatic variable-ratio transmission and front-wheel drive.
- Do not apply it to the separate 1.8L six-speed manual, 2.0L, hybrid or hatchback configurations.
- Tire pressure always comes from the actual vehicle placard.

# 2021 Toyota Camry AWD LE/SE — 2.5L inline-4, 8-speed automatic

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, option/RPO codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** API SN/RC multigrade engine oil; SAE 0W-16 — Owner's Manual, p. 588.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.8 qt (4.5 L; 4.0 Imp. qt.) — Owner's Manual, p. 588.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Toyota Super Long Life Coolant or a similar high-quality ethylene-glycol, non-silicate/non-amine/non-nitrite/non-borate coolant with long-life hybrid organic-acid technology — Owner's Manual, p. 590.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 76 lb-ft (103 N·m; 10.5 kgf·m) — Owner's Manual, p. 594.
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire and Loading Information label on the driver-side door pillar; the vehicle placard controls for the installed tires — Owner's Manual, tire-information-label section.
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate every 5,000 mi or 6 months; use the pattern applicable to the installed tire configuration — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 38–54.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** With 0W-16 oil, replace every 10,000 mi/12 months under the normal schedule. Replace every 5,000 mi/6 months when the guide's special operating conditions apply, including repeated short trips in freezing weather, extensive idling/low-speed use, heavy loading/towing or extensive dusty/dirt-road use — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 33, 35 and 38–54.
- **Brake fluid:** Needs research; the inspected maintenance guide does not publish a routine time/mileage replacement interval.
- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 mi/36 months under the normal schedule; inspect more often under listed dusty/dirt-road conditions — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 43, 47, 50 and 54.
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 30,000 mi/36 months under the normal schedule; inspect or replace more often under listed dusty/dirt-road conditions — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 43, 47, 50 and 54.
- **Coolant:** Initial replacement at 100,000 mi/120 months, then every 50,000 mi/60 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, p. 35 and 100,000-mile schedule.
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Inspect automatic-transmission, transfer-case and rear-differential fluids as scheduled; replace at 60,000 mi/72 months when the guide's heavy-loading/towing special operating condition applies — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, pp. 35 and 47. Exact AWD service fluids and quantities remain unresolved.
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 120,000 mi/144 months — Warranty & Maintenance Guide, p. 54.

## Variant warnings

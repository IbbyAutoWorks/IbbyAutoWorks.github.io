# 2021 Hyundai Elantra — Smartstream G2.0 Atkinson, IVT, FWD, no stop-start

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, option/RPO codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** SAE 0W-20 meeting API SN PLUS/SP or ILSAC GF-6 — Owner's Manual, pp. 2-11–2-12.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.54 US qt (4.3 L), drain and refill — Owner's Manual, p. 2-11.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Phosphate-based ethylene-glycol coolant for an aluminum radiator, mixed with water — Owner's Manual, p. 2-11.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 7.82 US qt (7.4 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 2-11.
- **Transmission fluid:** Hyundai Genuine SP-CVT1 — Owner's Manual, p. 2-11.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** 6.87 US qt (6.5 L) listed as IVT fluid volume; verify the current Hyundai service procedure before treating this as a drain-and-fill quantity — Owner's Manual, p. 2-11.
- **Brake fluid:** DOT 4; the manual specifies SAE J1704 DOT-4 LV / ISO 4925 Class 6 / FMVSS 116 DOT-4 for best ABS/ESC performance — Owner's Manual, p. 2-11.
- **Brake-fluid system volume:** 0.74–0.85 US qt (0.7–0.8 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 2-11.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this front-wheel-drive sheet.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; the owner manual does not state a separate front-differential service specification for this FWD/IVT configuration.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this front-wheel-drive sheet.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 79–94 lb-ft (107–127 N·m) — Owner's Manual, p. 2-9.
- **Cold tire pressure:** The manual lists 34 psi (235 kPa) front and 31 psi (215 kPa) rear for its three listed full-size tire configurations, but the vehicle Tire Specification and Pressure Label on the driver-side center pillar controls — Owner's Manual, pp. 2-9, 2-14.
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate every 8,000 mi (13,000 km); exact pattern should be confirmed for the installed tire configuration — Owner's Manual, p. 9-8.

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Exact part number needs research; normal schedule replaces every 24,000 mi (39,000 km) — Owner's Manual, p. 9-8.
- **Cabin air filter:** Exact part number needs research; normal schedule replaces every 16,000 mi (26,000 km) or 24 months — Owner's Manual, p. 9-8.
- **Oil filter:** Exact part number needs research.
- **Battery specification:** Exact group size, CCA and reserve capacity need research. This sheet excludes the EPA stop-start variant; the manual separately warns that ISG-equipped vehicles require a genuine Hyundai AGM replacement battery — Owner's Manual, ISG/battery section.
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Exact plug part number and installation gap need research; normal replacement interval is 96,000 mi — Owner's Manual, p. 9-8.
- **Exterior bulb types:** Halogen headlamp Type A uses HB3 60 W for high and low beam; Type B headlamps are LED assemblies. Other listed replaceable exterior bulbs include W5W side markers, PY21W turn signals, Type A rear P21W/5W stop/tail bulbs, Type B 2357NA stop bulbs, W16W reverse bulbs, W5W license-plate bulbs and P21W high-mounted stop bulbs; several alternate lamps are LED assemblies — Owner's Manual, p. 2-8. Confirm the installed lamp package before ordering.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Normal schedule: every 8,000 mi (13,000 km) or 12 months. Severe schedule: every 5,000 mi (8,000 km) or 6 months — Owner's Manual, pp. 9-8, 9-11.
- **Brake fluid:** Inspect every 8,000 mi (13,000 km) or 12 months; replace every 48,000 mi (78,000 km) or 48 months — Owner's Manual, p. 9-10.
- **Engine air filter:** Normal replacement every 24,000 mi (39,000 km); inspect more frequently in dusty/sandy severe use — Owner's Manual, pp. 9-8, 9-11.
- **Cabin air filter:** Normal replacement every 16,000 mi (26,000 km) or 24 months; replace more frequently for severe dust/traffic conditions — Owner's Manual, pp. 9-8, 9-11.
- **Coolant:** First replacement at 120,000 mi or 120 months; then every 30,000 mi or 24 months — Owner's Manual, p. 9-8.
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** IVT fluid has no normal-schedule check/service requirement; severe-use replacement is every 60,000 mi (90,000 km). Transfer case and rear axle are not applicable to this FWD sheet — Owner's Manual, pp. 9-9, 9-11.
- **Spark plugs:** Replace every 96,000 mi under the normal schedule; replace more frequently when severe-use conditions warrant — Owner's Manual, pp. 9-8, 9-11.

## Variant warnings

- This record is the EPA 43046 regular-gasoline 2.0L FWD automatic/IVT configuration without stop-start.
- Do not use its battery note for EPA 43047, the separate stop-start variant.
- Do not use this sheet for Elantra Hybrid, N Line/N, 1.6L turbo or manual-transmission vehicles.
- Tire pressure always comes from the actual vehicle placard.

# 2026 Chevrolet Equinox — 1.5L LSD turbo inline-4, 8-speed automatic, AWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, option/RPO codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** SAE 0W-20 full-synthetic engine oil meeting GM dexos1.[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed pp. 220–221.[4]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 5.3 qt (5.0 L).[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed p. 289.[4]
- **Coolant type/specification:** 50/50 clean drinkable water and DEX-COOL meeting GM Standard GMW3420.[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed pp. 224–225 and 286.[4]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 6.4 qt (6.1 L), based on the entire cooling system and its components.[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed pp. 289–290.[4]
- **Transmission fluid:** DEXRON-VI automatic transmission fluid.[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed p. 286.[4]
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Needs research; the owner manual does not publish an 8-speed service-fill quantity.[4]
- **Brake fluid:** GM-approved DOT 4 hydraulic brake fluid from a clean, sealed container.[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed pp. 229–230 and 286.[4]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** GM Transfer Case Fluid; the exact current product/part specification needs VIN-specific confirmation. Approximate capacity is 0.53 qt (0.5 L).[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed pp. 286 and 289.[4]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; the owner manual does not publish a separately serviceable front-differential specification or capacity.[4]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Product and capacity need research; the owner manual directs rear-axle fluid selection to the dealer/current service information.[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed p. 286.[4]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 100 lb-ft (140 N·m).[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed p. 290.[4]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the vehicle-specific Tire and Loading Information label on the center pillar; the physical placard is the authority for the installed tire package.[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed pp. 162 and 247–248.[4]
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate every 7,500 mi (12,000 km), use the illustrated pattern, exclude the compact spare, reset pressures to the placard and relearn TPMS positions.[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed pp. 252–253 and 280–282.[4]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research; replace according to engine-air-filter-life messages and reset the life system. Dusty service may require earlier inspection/replacement.[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed pp. 222–223 and 282.[4]
- **Cabin air filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research; replace every 22,500 mi (36,000 km) or 24 months, sooner for heavy traffic, poor air quality, dust, allergens, reduced airflow, fogging or odor.[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed p. 282.[4]
- **Oil filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research.[4]
- **Battery specification:** Maintenance-free AGM 12-volt battery; use the replacement number on the original battery label. Group size and CCA remain Needs research.[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed pp. 230–231.[4]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Exact plug part number needs research; factory gap is 0.024–0.028 in (0.60–0.70 mm), and re-gapping is not recommended.[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed p. 290.[4]
- **Exterior bulb types:** All exterior lights use LED light sources; the assemblies contain no serviceable incandescent bulbs and dealer replacement is directed.[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed p. 234.[4]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the oil-life system; change within 600 mi (1,000 km) of the message and at least annually. If accidentally reset, service within 3,000 mi (5,000 km) of the last service.[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed pp. 221 and 282.[4]
- **Brake fluid:** Every 5 years.[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed p. 283.[4]
- **Engine air filter:** Follow the filter-life messages; severe dust may require earlier service.[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed pp. 222–223 and 282.[4]
- **Cabin air filter:** Every 22,500 mi (36,000 km) or 24 months, with shorter intervals under the listed environmental conditions.[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed p. 282.[4]
- **Coolant:** Drain and fill at 150,000 mi (240,000 km) or 6 years, whichever comes first.[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed p. 282.[4]
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Severe service: change the 8-speed automatic-transmission fluid every 45,000 mi (72,000 km) and rear-axle fluid every 75,000 mi (120,000 km). Normal service: rear-axle fluid at 150,000 mi (240,000 km). No fixed transfer-case interval is published.[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed pp. 282–283.[4]
- **Spark plugs:** 60,000 mi (96,000 km); extreme service may require more frequent replacement.[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed pp. 282–283.[4]

## Variant warnings

- This sheet is only for the 1.5L LSD engine, 8-speed automatic and AWD configuration. The FWD vehicle uses a CVT, a smaller engine-oil quantity and no AWD rear-axle/transfer-case service path.[4] Owner's Manual locator: printed pp. 286 and 289–290.[4]
- EPA vehicle 49480 independently identifies the 2026 Equinox AWD as a 1.5L four-cylinder SIDI regular-gasoline vehicle with an 8-speed automatic, all-wheel drive and stop/start.[9]
- Confirm the transfer-case and rear-axle products in current VIN-specific GM service information before service; the owner manual does not fully specify the rear-axle product.[4]
- Tire pressure always comes from the actual vehicle placard.[4]

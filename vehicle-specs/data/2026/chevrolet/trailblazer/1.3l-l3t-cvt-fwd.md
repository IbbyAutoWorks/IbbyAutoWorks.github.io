# 2026 Chevrolet Trailblazer — 1.3L L3T turbo inline-3, CVT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, option/RPO codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** SAE 0W-20 full-synthetic engine oil meeting GM dexos1 — Owner's Manual, pp. 201–202.[5]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.8 qt (4.5 L) — Owner's Manual, p. 283.[5]
- **Coolant type/specification:** 50/50 clean drinkable water and DEX-COOL meeting GM Standard GMW3420 — Owner's Manual, p. 206.[5]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 7.8 qt (7.4 L), entire system — Owner's Manual, p. 283.[5]
- **Transmission fluid:** GM High Performance CVT Fluid; obtain through the dealer/current GM service information — Owner's Manual, p. 280.[5]
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Needs research; the owner manual does not publish a service-fill quantity.[5]
- **Brake fluid:** GM-approved DOT 4 hydraulic brake fluid from a clean, sealed container — Owner's Manual, pp. 211–212, 280.[5]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD configuration.[2]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; no separate front-differential service specification or quantity is published in the owner manual.[5]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD configuration.[2]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 100 lb-ft (140 N·m) — Owner's Manual, p. 284.[5]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the Tire and Loading Information label/vehicle placard for the installed tires; the placard is the authority — Owner's Manual, pp. 147, 235–236.[5]
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate every 7,500 mi (12,000 km); use the illustrated pattern on p. 242 and complete the TPMS relearn after rotation — Owner's Manual, pp. 241–242, 274–276.[5]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research; follow the engine-air-filter life system and replacement procedure — Owner's Manual, pp. 203–205.[5]
- **Cabin air filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research; scheduled replacement is 22,500 mi (36,000 km) or 24 months, whichever comes first — Owner's Manual, p. 276.[5]
- **Oil filter:** Exact replacement part number needs research.[5]
- **Battery specification:** Maintenance-free AGM 12-volt battery; use the replacement number shown on the original battery label. Group size and CCA need research — Owner's Manual, p. 212.[5]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Exact plug part number needs research; factory gap 0.026–0.030 in (0.65–0.75 mm), and re-gapping is not recommended — Owner's Manual, p. 284.[5]
- **Exterior bulb types:** Base headlamp front turn/parking lamp and base/uplevel rear lamp configurations include serviceable bulbs; several lamps and uplevel headlamp/taillamp assemblies are LED. Exact bulb trade numbers need research — Owner's Manual, pp. 217–222.[5]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the oil-life system; change within 600 mi (1,000 km) of the message and at least annually. If accidentally reset, service within 3,000 mi (5,000 km) of the last change — Owner's Manual, pp. 203, 276.[5]
- **Brake fluid:** Every 5 years — Owner's Manual, p. 277.[5]
- **Engine air filter:** Follow the filter-life messages; replace at the next oil change or earliest convenience as directed, and sooner in severe dust — Owner's Manual, pp. 203–205, 276.[5]
- **Cabin air filter:** Every 22,500 mi (36,000 km) or 24 months, sooner for heavy traffic, poor air quality, dust, allergens, reduced airflow, fogging, or odor — Owner's Manual, p. 276.[5]
- **Coolant:** **Source conflict:** the cooling-system narrative says five years or 150,000 mi, while the maintenance schedule says 150,000 mi or six years. Use current VIN-specific GM service information before setting the due date — Owner's Manual, pp. 206, 276.[5]
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Severe service: CVT fluid and filter every 45,000 mi (72,000 km). No normal-service numeric CVT interval is published in this manual — Owner's Manual, pp. 276–277.[5]
- **Spark plugs:** 60,000 mi (96,000 km); extreme conditions may require more frequent replacement — Owner's Manual, pp. 276–277.[5]

## Variant warnings

- This sheet is only for VIN code **L**, 1.3L L3T, CVT (MRG), FWD. The 1.2L LBP and AWD 9-speed configurations have different capacities and drivetrain service requirements — Owner's Manual, pp. 283–284.[5]

# 2026 Nissan Frontier — 3.8L V6, 9-speed automatic, 2WD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, cab/bed/trim equipment, axle hardware, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Nissan Motor Oil 0W-20 SP or equivalent; if unavailable, Nissan permits synthetic 0W-20 meeting API SP and ILSAC GF-6A. Owner Manual, “Recommended fluids/lubricants and capacities,” printed p. 409.[1]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** Approximately 5.0 L (5 1/4 US qt) with filter; approximately 4.7 L (5 US qt) without filter. Owner Manual, printed p. 409.[1]
- **Coolant type/capacity:** Pre-diluted Genuine Nissan Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant (blue) or equivalent; approximate capacity with reservoir is 12.0 L (3 1/8 US gal). Owner Manual, printed p. 409.[1]
- **Automatic transmission fluid:** Genuine Nissan Matic P ATF or equivalent only. Routine service quantity is **Needs research** because the owner manual specifies filling to the proper level but publishes no capacity. Owner Manual, printed p. 409.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Genuine Nissan Super Heavy Duty Brake Fluid or equivalent DOT 3; capacity is not published. Owner Manual, printed p. 410.[1]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Genuine Nissan Differential Oil Hypoid Super-CS GL-5 SAE 75W-90 or equivalent; approximate capacity is 2.01 L (4 1/4 US qt). Owner Manual, printed p. 410.[1]
- **Transfer case and front differential:** Not applicable to this exact 2WD configuration.[1][7]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 98 ft-lb (133 N·m); recheck after 600 miles (1,000 km) following wheel removal and at tire-rotation intervals. Owner Manual, printed pp. 378–379.[1]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Check the Tire and Loading Information label on the driver-side center pillar; the actual vehicle placard is the authority for the cab, bed, tire and load configuration. Owner Manual, printed pp. 371–373 and 418.[1]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 7,500 miles (12,000 km). The manual does not state a universal pattern in the cited passage, so the rotation pattern remains **Needs research**. Owner Manual, printed p. 379.[1]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles/24 months under the standard schedule; dusty operation may require more frequent service. Exact service part number is **Needs research**. Owner Manual, printed pp. 389 and 393.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 15,000 miles/12 months; prolonged dusty operation may require more frequent service. Exact service part number is **Needs research**. Owner Manual, printed pp. 389 and 392.[1]
- **Oil filter:** Genuine Nissan oil filter or equivalent; exact service part number is **Needs research**. Owner Manual, printed pp. 353 and 409.[1]
- **Battery specification:** The owner manual identifies a sealed maintenance-free 12-volt battery and a variable-voltage charging system but does not publish a replacement group, chemistry or CCA. Exact installed-battery specification is **Needs research**. Owner Manual, printed pp. 359–361 and 387.[1]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** FXE22HR-11 with nominal 1.1 mm (0.043 in) gap. Owner Manual, printed p. 414.[1]
- **Replaceable bulbs versus LED assemblies:** The manual states the vehicle uses LED lights and directs unlisted LED/bulb replacement to Nissan. It lists a Type A turn/park bulb 7444NA and high-mounted stop/cargo bulb 912; exact trim/equipment mapping remains **Needs research**. Owner Manual, printed pp. 369–370.[1]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Standard schedule every 7,500 miles/6 months; severe-use schedule every 5,000 miles/6 months. Owner Manual, printed pp. 390–391.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Standard replacement at 30,000 miles/24 months and recurring equivalent milestones; severe-use replacement begins at 10,000 miles/12 months. Owner Manual, printed pp. 391 and 393.[1]
- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles/24 months; dusty use may require more frequent service. Owner Manual, printed pp. 389 and 393.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 15,000 miles/12 months. Owner Manual, printed p. 392.[1]
- **Coolant:** First replacement at 105,000 miles (168,000 km) or 84 months, then every 75,000 miles (120,000 km) or 60 months. Owner Manual, printed p. 404.[1]
- **Automatic transmission fluid:** Inspect at 15,000-mile/12-month schedule points and use CONSULT deterioration data; under severe use, replacement begins at 30,000 miles/24 months. Service quantity remains **Needs research**. Owner Manual, printed pp. 392–393.[1]
- **Rear differential oil:** Inspect at 15,000-mile/12-month schedule points; under severe use, replacement begins at 20,000 miles/24 months. Owner Manual, printed pp. 392–393.[1]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 105,000 miles (168,000 km) or 84 months. Owner Manual, printed p. 404.[1]
- **Severe/off-road operation:** Repeated short trips, freezing short trips, hot stop-and-go use, extensive idling/low-speed commercial use, dusty/rough/muddy/salt roads, towing/camper/roof-carrier use and off-road/deep-water operation trigger additional maintenance. Owner Manual, printed p. 390.[1]

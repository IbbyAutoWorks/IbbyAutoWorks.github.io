# 2026 Nissan Altima — 2.5L PR25DD, CVT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, trim/equipment, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Nissan Motor Oil 0W-20 SP or equivalent; if unavailable, Nissan permits synthetic 0W-20 meeting API SP and ILSAC GF-6A. Owner Manual, printed pp. 471 and 475.[1]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** Approximately 5.2 L (5 1/2 US qt) with filter; approximately 5.0 L (5 1/4 US qt) without filter. Owner Manual, printed p. 471.[1]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Pre-diluted Genuine Nissan Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant (blue) or equivalent. Owner Manual, printed pp. 410–411 and 471.[1]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** Approximately 8.3 L (2 1/4 US gal) with reservoir for the PR25DD engine. Owner Manual, printed p. 471.[1]
- **CVT fluid:** Genuine Nissan CVT Fluid NS-3 or equivalent only. Routine drain/refill quantity is **Needs research** because the owner manual publishes no CVT capacity. Owner Manual, printed pp. 416 and 471.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Genuine Nissan Super Heavy Duty Brake Fluid or equivalent DOT 3; capacity is not published. Owner Manual, printed pp. 416 and 472.[1]
- **Transfer case and differentials:** Not applicable to this front-wheel-drive configuration. Do not import the manual's AWD differential/transfer-case product guidance into this FWD sheet.[1][5]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 83 ft-lb (113 N·m); recheck after 600 miles (1,000 km) following wheel removal and at tire-rotation intervals. Owner Manual, printed pp. 442–443.[1]
- **Cold tire pressure:** The actual Tire and Loading Information label on the driver-side center pillar is the authority. The manual's reference table lists 33 psi (230 kPa) front and rear for the original 215/55R17 and 235/40R19 tires, but the placard must control the installed trim/tire configuration. Owner Manual, printed pp. 432–435 and 482.[1]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 10,000 miles (16,000 km); do not include the temporary spare. The cited passage does not state a universal pattern, so the pattern remains **Needs research**. Owner Manual, printed pp. 442–443 and maintenance tables beginning p. 456.[1]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles/36 months under the published schedule and more frequently during prolonged dusty operation. Exact service part number is **Needs research**. Owner Manual, printed pp. 422, 451 and 459.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 10,000 miles/12 months under the recurring schedule and more frequently during prolonged dusty operation. Exact service part number is **Needs research**. Owner Manual, printed pp. 422 and 456–468.[1]
- **Oil filter:** Genuine Nissan oil filter or equivalent; exact service part number is **Needs research**. Owner Manual, printed pp. 412–415 and 475.[1]
- **Battery specification:** Sealed maintenance-free 12-volt battery with variable-voltage charging; replacement group, chemistry and CCA are **Needs research**. Owner Manual, printed pp. 418–420 and 450.[1]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** DXE22H11C plugs with nominal 1.1 mm (0.043 in) gap. Owner Manual, printed p. 477.[1]
- **Replaceable bulbs versus LED assemblies:** The vehicle uses LED lights and Nissan directs unlisted LED/bulb replacement to a dealer. The manual lists replaceable No. 158 trunk, W21/5W stop/tail, W21W turn, W5W side-marker/tail and No. 921 backup bulbs, with trim/equipment applicability still requiring verification. Owner Manual, printed pp. 430–431.[1]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** The oil-control system calculates the interval. Standard maintenance lists replacement at 10,000 miles/12 months; when the indicator appears, replace within two weeks or 500 miles (800 km). If the system resets prematurely or becomes inoperative, replace within six months or 5,000 miles (8,000 km) of the last change. Owner Manual, printed pp. 454–468.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Replace every 20,000 miles/24 months under the standard schedule; severe-use tables list replacement at each 10,000-mile/12-month major service. Owner Manual, printed pp. 456–468.[1]
- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles/36 months; dusty operation may require more frequent service. Owner Manual, printed pp. 451 and 459.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 10,000 miles/12 months; dusty operation may require more frequent service. Owner Manual, printed pp. 451 and 456–468.[1]
- **Coolant:** The manual agrees on a first mileage of 105,000 miles (168,000 km) and later replacement every 75,000 miles (120,000 km) or 60 months, but conflicts internally on the first time limit: cooling-system text and the schedule footnote state seven years/84 months while the 105,000-mile table heading states 126 months. Treat the first time limit as **conflict — Needs OEM clarification**. Owner Manual, printed pp. 410–411 and 467.[1]
- **CVT fluid:** Inspect at the recurring major services; severe-use replacement is listed at 60,000 miles/72 months and 120,000 miles/144 months. Routine service quantity remains **Needs research**. Owner Manual, printed pp. 452 and 461–468.[1]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 105,000 miles (168,000 km) or 126 months. Owner Manual, printed p. 467.[1]
- **Severe operation:** Repeated short trips, freezing short trips, hot stop-and-go operation, extensive idling/low-speed commercial use, dusty or rough/muddy/salt roads and car-top-carrier use trigger additional maintenance; frequent braking/mountain driving and sustained high speed also advance oil-control timing. Owner Manual, printed pp. 453–454.[1]

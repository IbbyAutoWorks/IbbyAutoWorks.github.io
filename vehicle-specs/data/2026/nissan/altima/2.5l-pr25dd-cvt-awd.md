# 2026 Nissan Altima — 2.5L PR25DD, CVT, AWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, trim/equipment, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Nissan Motor Oil 0W-20 SP or equivalent; if unavailable, Nissan permits synthetic 0W-20 meeting API SP and ILSAC GF-6A.[1] Owner Manual, printed pp. 471 and 475.[1]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** Approximately 5.2 L (5 1/2 US qt) with filter; approximately 5.0 L (5 1/4 US qt) without filter.[1] Owner Manual, printed p. 471.[1]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Pre-diluted Genuine Nissan Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant (blue) or equivalent.[1] Owner Manual, printed pp. 410–411 and 471.[1]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** Approximately 8.3 L (2 1/4 US gal) with reservoir for the PR25DD engine.[1] Owner Manual, printed p. 471.[1]
- **CVT fluid:** Genuine Nissan CVT Fluid NS-3 or equivalent only.[1] Routine drain/refill quantity is **Needs research** because the owner manual publishes no CVT capacity.[1] Owner Manual, printed pp. 416 and 471.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Genuine Nissan Super Heavy Duty Brake Fluid or equivalent DOT 3; capacity is not published.[1] Owner Manual, printed pp. 416 and 472.[1]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Genuine Nissan Differential Oil Hypoid Super GL-5 80W-90 or equivalent conventional non-synthetic oil; service quantity is **Needs research** because the owner manual publishes no transfer-case capacity.[1] Owner Manual, printed p. 472.[1]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** **Needs research.** The owner manual does not establish a separately serviceable front-differential fill apart from the CVT/transfer-case guidance for this exact configuration.[1]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Genuine Nissan Differential Oil Hypoid Super GL-5 80W-90 or equivalent conventional non-synthetic oil; service quantity is **Needs research** because the owner manual publishes no differential capacity.[1] Owner Manual, printed p. 472.[1]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 83 ft-lb (113 N·m); recheck after 600 miles (1,000 km) following wheel removal and at tire-rotation intervals.[1] Owner Manual, printed pp. 442–443.[1]
- **Cold tire pressure:** The actual Tire and Loading Information label on the driver-side center pillar is the authority.[1] The manual's reference table lists 33 psi (230 kPa) front and rear for the original 215/55R17 and 235/40R19 tires, but the placard must control the installed AWD trim/tire configuration.[1] Owner Manual, printed pp. 432–435 and 482.[1]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 10,000 miles (16,000 km); do not include the temporary spare.[1] The cited passage does not state a universal pattern, so the pattern remains **Needs research**.[1] Owner Manual, printed pp. 442–443 and maintenance tables beginning p. 456.[1]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles/36 months under the published schedule and more frequently during prolonged dusty operation.[1] Exact service part number is **Needs research**.[unverified] Owner Manual, printed pp. 422, 451 and 459.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 10,000 miles/12 months under the recurring schedule and more frequently during prolonged dusty operation.[1] Exact service part number is **Needs research**.[unverified] Owner Manual, printed pp. 422 and 456–468.[1]
- **Oil filter:** Genuine Nissan oil filter or equivalent; exact service part number is **Needs research**.[1] Owner Manual, printed pp. 412–415 and 475.[1]
- **Battery specification:** Sealed maintenance-free 12-volt battery with variable-voltage charging; replacement group, chemistry and CCA are **Needs research**.[1] Owner Manual, printed pp. 418–420 and 450.[1]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** DXE22H11C plugs with nominal 1.1 mm (0.043 in) gap.[1] Owner Manual, printed p. 477.[1]
- **Replaceable bulbs versus LED assemblies:** The vehicle uses LED lights and Nissan directs unlisted LED/bulb replacement to a dealer.[1] The manual lists replaceable No. 158 trunk, W21/5W stop/tail, W21W turn, W5W side-marker/tail and No. 921 backup bulbs, with trim/equipment applicability still requiring verification.[1] Owner Manual, printed pp. 430–431.[1]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** The oil-control system calculates the interval.[1] Standard maintenance lists replacement at 10,000 miles/12 months; when the indicator appears, replace within two weeks or 500 miles (800 km).[1] If the system resets prematurely or becomes inoperative, replace within six months or 5,000 miles (8,000 km) of the last change.[1] Owner Manual, printed pp. 454–468.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Replace every 20,000 miles/24 months under the standard schedule; severe-use tables list replacement at each 10,000-mile/12-month major service.[1] Owner Manual, printed pp. 456–468.[1]
- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles/36 months; dusty operation may require more frequent service.[1] Owner Manual, printed pp. 451 and 459.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace every 10,000 miles/12 months; dusty operation may require more frequent service.[1] Owner Manual, printed pp. 451 and 456–468.[1]
- **Coolant:** The manual agrees on a first mileage of 105,000 miles (168,000 km) and later replacement every 75,000 miles (120,000 km) or 60 months, but conflicts internally on the first time limit: cooling-system text and the schedule footnote state seven years/84 months while the 105,000-mile table heading states 126 months.[1] Treat the first time limit as **conflict — Needs OEM clarification**.[1] Owner Manual, printed pp. 410–411 and 467.[1]
- **CVT fluid:** Inspect at the recurring major services; severe-use replacement is listed at 60,000 miles/72 months and 120,000 miles/144 months.[1] Routine service quantity remains **Needs research**.[unverified] Owner Manual, printed pp. 452 and 461–468.[1]
- **Differential/transfer-case fluids:** Inspect at recurring standard major services.[1] Under the severe schedule, replace both differential gear oil and transfer-case oil at the listed 20,000-mile/24-month major intervals.[1] Exact service quantities remain **Needs research**.[unverified] Owner Manual, printed pp. 452 and 456–468.[1]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace at 105,000 miles (168,000 km) or 126 months.[1] Owner Manual, printed p. 467.[1]
- **Severe operation:** Repeated short trips, freezing short trips, hot stop-and-go operation, extensive idling/low-speed commercial use, dusty or rough/muddy/salt roads and car-top-carrier use trigger additional maintenance; frequent braking/mountain driving and sustained high speed also advance oil-control timing.[1] Owner Manual, printed pp. 453–454.[1]

## Variant warnings

- Nissan's official 2026 product page identifies available Intelligent All-Wheel Drive and includes AWD trim/version offerings; confirm the VIN and trim before applying this AWD sheet.[2]
- EPA's 2026 Altima options response currently exposes only vehicle 50064, which is the separate FWD identity. No exact AWD EPA vehicle ID is asserted here.[3]
- The owner manual contains AWD-only differential, transfer-case, propeller-shaft, spare-tire and dynamometer cautions. Do not apply this sheet to the separate FWD configuration.[1]
- Tire size, cold pressure, lamp configuration and other equipment remain VIN/trim/placard checks even when the engine and drivetrain match.[1]

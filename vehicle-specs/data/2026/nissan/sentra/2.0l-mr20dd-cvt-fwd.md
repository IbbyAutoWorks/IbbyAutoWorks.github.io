# 2026 Nissan Sentra — 2.0L MR20DD, CVT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, trim/equipment, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Nissan Motor Oil 0W-20 SP or equivalent; if unavailable, Nissan permits synthetic 0W-20 meeting API SP and ILSAC GF-6A. Owner Manual, “Recommended fluids/lubricants and capacities,” printed p. 446.[1]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** Approximately 4.1 L (4 3/8 US qt) with filter; approximately 3.8 L (4 US qt) without filter. Owner Manual, printed p. 446.[1]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Pre-diluted Genuine Nissan Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant (blue) or equivalent. Owner Manual, printed pp. 396 and 446.[1]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** Approximately 8.2 L (2 1/8 US gal) with reservoir. Owner Manual, printed p. 446.[1]
- **CVT fluid:** Genuine Nissan CVT Fluid NS-3 or equivalent only. Routine drain/refill quantity is **Needs research** because the owner manual publishes no CVT capacity. Owner Manual, printed pp. 400 and 446.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Genuine Nissan Brake Fluid or equivalent DOT 3 or DOT 4; capacity is not published. Owner Manual, printed pp. 400 and 446.[1]
- **Transfer case and differentials:** Not applicable to this front-wheel-drive configuration.[1]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 88.5 ft-lb (120 N·m); recheck after 600 miles (1,000 km) following wheel removal and at tire-rotation intervals. Owner Manual, printed pp. 422–423.[1]
- **Cold tire pressure:** The actual Tire and Loading Information label on the driver-side center pillar is the authority. The manual lists 35 psi for 205/60R16 and 215/50R17 tires and 33 psi for 215/45R18 tires, but the vehicle placard must control the installed trim/tire configuration. Owner Manual, printed pp. 414–417 and 454.[1]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 5,000 miles (8,000 km); do not include the temporary spare. The cited passage does not state a universal pattern, so the pattern remains **Needs research**. Owner Manual, printed pp. 422–423.[1]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles/36 months under the published schedule; prolonged dusty operation may require more frequent service. Exact service part number is **Needs research**. Owner Manual, printed pp. 405, 431 and 435.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace at the scheduled intervals and more frequently during prolonged dusty operation; the first published replacement is 10,000 miles/12 months. Exact service part number is **Needs research**. Owner Manual, printed pp. 405, 431 and 434.[1]
- **Oil filter:** Genuine Nissan oil filter or equivalent; exact service part number is **Needs research**. Owner Manual, printed pp. 398–399 and 449.[1]
- **Battery specification:** Sealed maintenance-free 12-volt battery with variable-voltage charging; replacement group, chemistry and CCA are **Needs research**. Owner Manual, printed pp. 402–403 and 430.[1]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** ILKAR7AE11S iridium-tipped plugs with nominal 1.1 mm (0.043 in) gap. Owner Manual, printed pp. 404 and 450.[1]
- **Replaceable bulbs versus LED assemblies:** The vehicle uses LED lights and Nissan directs unlisted LED/bulb replacement to a dealer. The manual identifies a replaceable trunk bulb, No. 158, 3.4 W; exact trim/equipment mapping for every assembly remains **Needs research**. Owner Manual, printed p. 413.[1]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** The oil-control system calculates the interval. The standard schedule first lists replacement at 10,000 miles/12 months; when the indicator appears, replace within two weeks or 500 miles (800 km). If the system resets prematurely or becomes inoperative, replace within six months or 5,000 miles (8,000 km) of the last change. Owner Manual, printed pp. 432–434.[1]
- **Tire rotation:** Every 5,000 miles (8,000 km). Owner Manual, printed pp. 422–423 and 433.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** The extracted schedule's multi-column layout is not sufficiently unambiguous for a safe fixed interval; exact normal/severe timing is **Needs research** from a clean OEM table or service-information source.[1]
- **Engine air filter:** Replace every 30,000 miles/36 months; dusty operation may require more frequent service. Owner Manual, printed pp. 431 and 435.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** First published replacement at 10,000 miles/12 months, with recurring scheduled replacements; dusty operation may require more frequent service. Owner Manual, printed pp. 431 and 434.[1]
- **Coolant:** First replacement at 105,000 miles (168,000 km) or 126 months, then every 75,000 miles (120,000 km) or 60 months. Owner Manual, printed p. 443.[1]
- **CVT fluid:** For car-top-carrier or rough/muddy-road use, replace every 60,000 miles (96,000 km) or have Nissan CONSULT deterioration data checked; the severe schedule also lists replacement at 60,000 miles/72 months. Service quantity remains **Needs research**. Owner Manual, printed pp. 432 and 438.[1]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace every 60,000 miles (96,000 km) or 72 months. Owner Manual, printed p. 438.[1]
- **Severe operation:** Repeated short trips, freezing short trips, hot stop-and-go operation, extensive idling/low-speed commercial use, dusty or rough/muddy/salt roads, frequent braking/mountain driving, sustained high speed and car-top-carrier use trigger additional maintenance. Owner Manual, printed pp. 432–433.[1]

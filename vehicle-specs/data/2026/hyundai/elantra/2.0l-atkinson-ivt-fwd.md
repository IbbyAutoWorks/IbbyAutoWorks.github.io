# 2026 Hyundai Elantra — 2.0L Atkinson, IVT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, trim, tire placard, installed battery, lamp equipment and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Full-synthetic SAE 0W-20, API SN PLUS/SP or ILSAC GF-6 — 2026 Owner's Manual, Recommended Lubricants and Capacities, PDF pages 696–697 (printed pages 688–689).[1]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.54 US qt (4.3 L), drain and refill — Owner's Manual, PDF page 696 (printed page 688).[1]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Mixture of antifreeze and water; phosphate-based ethylene-glycol coolant for an aluminum radiator — Owner's Manual, PDF pages 619–620 and 696 (printed pages 611–612 and 688).[1]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 7.82 US qt (7.4 L) for Smartstream G2.0 — Owner's Manual, PDF page 696 (printed page 688).[1]
- **Transmission fluid:** MICHANG CVTF SP-CVT1 or Hyundai Genuine CVTF SP-CVT1 — Owner's Manual, PDF page 696 (printed page 688).[1]
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** 6.87 US qt (6.5 L) published IVT volume; exact routine drain-and-fill service quantity Needs research — Owner's Manual, PDF page 696 (printed page 688).[1]
- **Brake fluid:** DOT 4; table volume 0.74–0.85 US qt (0.7–0.8 L). Footnote specifies SAE J1704 DOT-4 LV, ISO 4925 Class 6 and FMVSS 116 DOT-4 — Owner's Manual, PDF pages 611 and 696 (printed pages 603 and 688).[1]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable; official product and owner-manual identity is FWD.[1][4]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** No separately published service fluid or quantity was identified for the IVT/FWD powertrain; Needs research.[1]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable; official product and owner-manual identity is FWD.[1][4]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 79–94 lb-ft (107–127 N·m) — Owner's Manual, tire-rotation section and Tires and Wheels table, PDF pages 637 and 693 (printed pages 629 and 685).[1]
- **Cold tire pressure:** The physical driver's-side center-pillar tire placard is the authority. The manual table publishes 34 psi front/31 psi rear for 195/65R15, 205/55R16 and 225/45R17 packages, and 33 psi front/rear for 235/40R18; verify installed equipment and placard — Owner's Manual, PDF pages 693 and 700 (printed pages 685 and 692).[1]
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate every 8,000 mi (13,000 km) or 12 months; rotate sooner for irregular wear. Follow installed directional/asymmetric-tire restrictions — Owner's Manual, PDF pages 605 and 637–638 (printed pages 597 and 629–630).[1]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Exact replacement part number Needs research. Normal schedule: inspect every 8,000 mi/12 months and replace every 24,000 mi/36 months; severe dusty use requires more frequent inspection — Owner's Manual, PDF pages 605 and 607–608 (printed pages 597 and 599–600).[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Exact replacement part number Needs research. Normal schedule shows replacement every 16,000 mi (26,000 km) or 24 months; severe listed conditions require more frequent inspection/replacement — Owner's Manual, PDF pages 605 and 608 (printed pages 597 and 600).[1]
- **Oil filter:** Exact Hyundai part number Needs research; replace with engine oil at the applicable normal/severe interval — Owner's Manual, PDF pages 604–608 (printed pages 596–600).[1]
- **Battery specification:** The manual illustrates CMF60L-DIN/AGM70L-DIN labels with 12 V, 60/70 Ah, 550/760 A EN and RC 92/120-minute examples, but does not map one label universally to this exact trim/equipment. Verify the installed battery — Owner's Manual, PDF pages 632–634 (printed pages 624–626).[1]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Exact plug part number and gap Need research. Normal replacement interval for Smartstream G2.0 Atkinson is 96,000 mi — Owner's Manual, PDF page 605 (printed page 597).[1]
- **Exterior bulb types:** Equipment-dependent. Published replaceable types include HB3 high/low headlamps on Type A, W5W side marker, PY21W turn signal, P21W/5W stop/tail, W5W tail/side marker/license plate, W16W reverse and P21W high-mounted stop; Type B/C and selected exterior functions use non-single-replaceable LED assemblies — Owner's Manual, PDF pages 660–670 and 691–692 (printed pages 652–662 and 683–684).[1]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Normal: every 8,000 mi (13,000 km) or 12 months. Severe: every 5,000 mi (8,000 km) or 6 months for listed conditions — Owner's Manual, PDF pages 604 and 607 (printed pages 596 and 599).[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Inspect every 8,000 mi (13,000 km) or 12 months; replace every 48,000 mi (78,000 km) or 48 months — Owner's Manual, PDF page 607 (printed page 599).[1]
- **Engine air filter:** Normal replacement every 24,000 mi (39,000 km) or 36 months; inspect more frequently in severe dusty conditions — Owner's Manual, PDF pages 605 and 607 (printed pages 597 and 599).[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Normal replacement every 16,000 mi (26,000 km) or 24 months; inspect/replace more frequently under listed severe conditions — Owner's Manual, PDF pages 605 and 608 (printed pages 597 and 600).[1]
- **Coolant:** First replacement at 120,000 mi (200,000 km) or 120 months, then every 24,000 mi (39,000 km) or 24 months — Owner's Manual, PDF page 606 (printed page 598).[1]
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Normal IVT schedule says no check/no service required. Severe IVT service: replace every 60,000 mi (90,000 km) for listed conditions — Owner's Manual, PDF pages 606 and 608 (printed pages 598 and 600).[1]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace every 96,000 mi for Smartstream G2.0 Atkinson; inspect more frequently under listed severe conditions — Owner's Manual, PDF pages 605 and 607 (printed pages 597 and 599).[1]

## Variant warnings

- Hyundai's official 2026 product page identifies the gasoline SE and SEL-family trims with a 147-hp 2.0L four-cylinder; the manual pairs Smartstream G2.0 with IVT. This sheet does not cover N Line, Hybrid or Elantra N.[1][4]
- Published IVT volume is not confirmed as a routine drain-and-fill amount.[1]
- Lamp types and tire pressures vary by installed trim/equipment; inspect the lamp and use the vehicle placard.[1]
- Tire pressure always comes from the actual vehicle placard.

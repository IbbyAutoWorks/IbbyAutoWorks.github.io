# 2026 Hyundai Tucson — 2.5L GDI, 8-speed automatic, HTRAC AWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, trim, tire placard, installed battery, lamp equipment and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Full-synthetic SAE 0W-20, API SN PLUS/SP or ILSAC GF-6 — Owner's Manual, Recommended Lubricants and Capacities and viscosity sections, printed pages 748–749.[1]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** Needs research. The Owner's Manual publishes 6.13 US qt (5.8 L) as the Smartstream G2.5 GDI drain-and-refill volume, but the table does not explicitly label that quantity “with filter”; do not treat it as a confirmed with-filter quantity without service information — printed page 748.[1]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Ethylene-glycol coolant mixed with water for an aluminum radiator; the maintenance chapter specifies phosphate-based ethylene-glycol coolant and 35–60% antifreeze depending on ambient temperature — printed pages 666–668 and 748.[1]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 8.99 US qt (8.51 L) published volume — Owner's Manual, printed page 748.[1]
- **Transmission fluid:** SK ATF SP4M-1, MICHANG ATF SP4M-1, S-OIL ATF SP4M-1 or Hyundai Genuine ATF SP4-M1 — Owner's Manual, printed page 748.[1]
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** 6.87 US qt (6.5 L) published automatic-transmission volume; exact routine drain-and-fill service quantity Needs research — Owner's Manual, printed page 748.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** SAE J1704 DOT-4 LV, ISO 4925 Class 6 and FMVSS 116 DOT-4; 0.87 ± 0.02 US qt (0.82 ± 0.02 L) published volume — Owner's Manual, printed pages 669 and 748.[1]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** API GL-5 SAE 75W-85 hypoid gear oil (SK HCT-5 Gear Oil 75W-85 or equivalent); 0.65–0.71 US qt (0.62–0.68 L) published volume — Owner's Manual, printed page 748.[1]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** No separate front-differential entry was identified beyond the AWD transfer-case specification; Needs research.[1]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** API GL-5 SAE 75W-85 hypoid gear oil (SK HCT-5 Gear Oil 75W-85 or equivalent); 0.56–0.67 US qt (0.53–0.63 L) published volume — Owner's Manual, printed page 748.[1]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 79–94 lb-ft (107–127 N·m) — Owner's Manual, tire-rotation and Tires and Wheels sections, printed pages 686 and 744.[1]
- **Cold tire pressure:** The physical driver's-side center-pillar tire placard is the authority. The manual publishes 35 psi (240 kPa) for the listed 235/65R17, 235/60R18 and 235/55R19 full-size tire packages; verify installed equipment and placard — printed pages 684–685, 744 and 753.[1]
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate every 8,000 mi (13,000 km) or 12 months, or sooner for irregular wear; inspect tread and pressure and follow installed tire-direction/asymmetry restrictions — printed pages 657 and 686.[1]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Exact replacement part number Needs research. Normal schedule: inspect at 8,000-mile/12-month steps and replace every 24,000 mi (39,000 km) or 36 months; severe dusty/sandy use requires more frequent replacement — printed pages 653 and 658.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Exact replacement part number Needs research. Normal schedule shows replacement every 16,000 mi (26,000 km) or 24 months; severe dusty or polluted use requires more frequent replacement — printed pages 657–658 and 673.[1]
- **Oil filter:** Exact Hyundai part number Needs research; replace with engine oil at the applicable normal/severe interval — printed pages 653 and 660.[1]
- **Battery specification:** The manual illustrates an AGM70L-DIN label with 12 V, 70 Ah (20HR), 120-minute reserve capacity and 760 A CCA (SAE/EN), but warns that the actual vehicle label may differ. Verify the installed battery before replacement — printed page 681.[1]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Exact plug part number, heat range and gap Need research. Normal replacement interval is 96,000 mi (156,000 km) — printed pages 654 and 661.[1]
- **Exterior bulb types:** Headlamps, DRL/parking, front turn signals, side markers/repeaters, high-mounted stop and many higher-trim rear lamps are LED assemblies. Published replaceable lamps include P21/5W tail/stop, PY21W rear turn signal, PY21W reverse, W5W rear side marker and W5W license-plate lamp depending on lamp type/equipment — printed pages 710–722 and 743.[1]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Normal: every 8,000 mi (13,000 km) or 12 months, subject to the Oil Life Management System. Severe: every 5,000 mi (8,000 km) or 6 months — printed pages 652–653 and 658.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Inspect every 8,000 mi (13,000 km) or 12 months; replace every 48,000 mi (78,000 km) or 48 months — printed page 656.[1]
- **Engine air filter:** Normal replacement every 24,000 mi (39,000 km) or 36 months; replace more frequently under listed severe dusty/sandy conditions — printed pages 653 and 658.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Normal replacement every 16,000 mi (26,000 km) or 24 months; replace more frequently under listed severe conditions — printed pages 657–658.[1]
- **Coolant:** First replacement at 120,000 mi (200,000 km) or 120 months, then every 24,000 mi (39,000 km) or 24 months — printed page 656.[1]
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Normal automatic-transmission schedule says no check/no service required. Under severe use, replace automatic-transmission fluid every 60,000 mi (96,000 km), and transfer-case and rear-differential oils every 72,000 mi (120,000 km). Normal schedule calls for periodic AWD transfer-case/rear-differential inspection; replace after submersion — printed pages 656 and 658.[1]
- **Spark plugs:** Replace every 96,000 mi (156,000 km); replace more frequently depending on listed severe conditions — printed pages 654 and 658.[1]

## Variant warnings

- Hyundai's official 2026 Tucson page identifies the gasoline model with 187 hp and available HTRAC AWD; this sheet covers that gasoline AWD path only.[3]
- Do not apply this gasoline 2.5L/8-speed data to Tucson Hybrid or Tucson Plug-in Hybrid variants.[1][3]
- The published engine-oil and transmission volumes are not confirmed routine service-fill quantities beyond the labels used in the owner-manual table.[1]
- Transfer-case and rear-differential products/capacities apply only to the AWD configuration.[1]
- Lamp types vary by installed equipment; inspect the assembly before ordering a bulb.[1]
- Tire pressure always comes from the actual vehicle placard.

# 2026 Hyundai Sonata N Line — 2.5L T-GDI, wet 8-speed DCT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, trim, tire placard, installed battery and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Full-synthetic SAE 0W-30, API SN PLUS/SP or ILSAC GF-6 — Owner's Manual, Recommended lubricants and capacities.[5]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 6.13 US qt (5.8 L), drain and refill — Owner's Manual, Recommended lubricants and capacities.[5]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Ethylene-glycol-base coolant for an aluminum radiator, mixed with distilled water — Owner's Manual, Recommended lubricants and capacities.[5]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 9.3 US qt (8.8 L) for Smartstream G2.5 T-GDI — Owner's Manual, Recommended lubricants and capacities.[5]
- **Transmission fluid:** Wet-DCT gear oil: GS WDCTF HD G. Hydraulic control oil: GS WDCTF HD H — Owner's Manual, Recommended lubricants and capacities.[5]
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Gear-oil volume 3.49–3.59 US qt (3.3–3.4 L); control-oil volume 2.59–2.64 US qt (2.45–2.5 L). Exact routine service procedure/quantity Needs research — Owner's Manual, Recommended lubricants and capacities.[5]
- **Brake fluid:** DOT 4; quantity as needed — Owner's Manual, Recommended lubricants and capacities.[5]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD N Line sheet.[5][14]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** No separately published front-differential service entry was identified for this wet-DCT FWD powertrain; Needs research.[5]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD N Line sheet.[5][14]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 79–94 lb-ft (107–127 N·m) — Owner's Manual, Tires and wheels.[4]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the physical tire-information placard as authority. The manual's table lists 35 psi (240 kPa) front and rear for the 245/40R19 full-size package; installed equipment and placard control — Owner's Manual, Tires and wheels; official N Line product identity.[4][14]
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Every 8,000 mi (13,000 km) or 12 months. The manual illustrates the standard non-directional rotation pattern; directional/asymmetric tire instructions and installed tire design control — Owner's Manual, Normal maintenance schedule and Tire rotation.[6][8]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Exact replacement part number Needs research. Inspect at 8,000-mi/12-month intervals and replace every 24,000 mi (39,000 km) or 36 months under the normal table; replace more frequently for severe dusty use — Owner's Manual, Normal and severe maintenance schedules.[6][7]
- **Cabin air filter:** Exact replacement part number Needs research. Replace every 16,000 mi (26,000 km) or 24 months under the normal table and more frequently under listed severe conditions — Owner's Manual, Normal and severe maintenance schedules.[6][7]
- **Oil filter:** Exact Hyundai replacement part number Needs research.[1]
- **Battery specification:** The manual illustrates an AGM70L-DIN, 12 V, 70 Ah/20HR, RC 120-minute label but warns that the actual vehicle label may differ; verify the installed battery before replacement — Owner's Manual, Battery capacity label.[9]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Exact Hyundai plug part number and gap Need research. Normal replacement interval for Smartstream 2.5 T-GDI is 48,000 mi (78,000 km) — Owner's Manual, Normal maintenance schedule.[6]
- **Exterior bulb types:** Headlamps, DRLs/parking lamps, front turn signals, side repeaters/markers, tail/stop lamps, rear turn signals, rear side markers and high-mounted stop lamp are LED. Reverse lamps are P21W/21W and license-plate lamps are W5W/5W — Owner's Manual, Bulb wattage.[3]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Normal: every 8,000 mi (13,000 km) or 12 months. Severe: every 5,000 mi (8,000 km) or 6 months for the listed conditions — Owner's Manual, Normal and severe maintenance schedules.[6][7]
- **Brake fluid:** Inspect every 8,000 mi (13,000 km) or 12 months; replace every 48,000 mi (78,000 km) or 48 months — Owner's Manual, Normal maintenance schedule.[6]
- **Engine air filter:** Normal replacement every 24,000 mi (39,000 km) or 36 months; more frequently for severe dusty conditions — Owner's Manual, Normal and severe maintenance schedules.[6][7]
- **Cabin air filter:** Normal replacement every 16,000 mi (26,000 km) or 24 months; more frequently under listed severe conditions — Owner's Manual, Normal and severe maintenance schedules.[6][7]
- **Coolant:** First replacement at 120,000 mi (200,000 km) or 120 months, then every 24,000 mi (39,000 km) or 24 months — Owner's Manual, Normal maintenance schedule.[6]
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Normal wet-DCT schedule shows inspection at 32,000 mi/48 months and each 32,000-mi/48-month interval shown thereafter. Under listed severe conditions, replace wet-DCT fluid every 56,000 mi (91,000 km). AWD-only transfer-case and rear-differential intervals do not apply — Owner's Manual, Normal and severe maintenance schedules.[6][7]
- **Spark plugs:** Normal replacement every 48,000 mi (78,000 km); replace more frequently under the listed severe conditions — Owner's Manual, Normal and severe maintenance schedules.[6][7]

## Variant warnings

- Hyundai's official 2026 product page identifies N Line as the 290-hp 2.5L turbo engine with N 8-speed wet dual-clutch transmission; SEL Sport AWD is a separate naturally aspirated identity.[14]
- Do not apply naturally aspirated GDI/8AT, AWD transfer/rear-differential or hybrid data.[5][14]
- Wet-DCT gear oil and hydraulic control oil are separate products and quantities; do not combine them.[5]
- Tire pressure always comes from the actual vehicle placard.

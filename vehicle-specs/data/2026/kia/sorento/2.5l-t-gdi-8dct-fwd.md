# 2026 Kia Sorento — 2.5L turbo GDI, 8-speed wet DCT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, trim, tire placard, installed battery and current OEM service information before use. Two official Kia sources publish different oil and coolant capacity figures; read the conflict notes before service.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Full-synthetic SAE 0W-30, API SN PLUS/SP or ILSAC GF-6 — Owner's Manual, printed pp. 37–38.[1]
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** **Conflict:** the Owner's Manual specifies 6.13 US qt (5.8 L) for an engine-oil drain and refill, while Kia Media's specification table lists 6.8 L as “lubricant oil capacity.” The drain/refill figure is the more service-specific wording, but shop confirmation is required before use — Owner's Manual, printed p. 37; Kia Media, Engine/Electrical specifications.[1][3]
- **Coolant type/specification:** Phosphate-based ethylene-glycol coolant — Owner's Manual, printed p. 37.[1]
- **Cooling-system capacity:** **Conflict:** the Owner's Manual lists 8.88 US qt (8.4 L), while Kia Media lists 3.3 L as engine-coolant capacity. Do not treat these figures as interchangeable; verify the intended circuit/fill procedure in current Kia service information — Owner's Manual, printed p. 37; Kia Media, Engine/Electrical specifications.[1][3]
- **Transmission fluid:** Wet-DCT gear side: Kia Genuine WET DCTF 75W or WET DCT FLUID. Hydraulic-control side: Kia Genuine WET DCT HYDRAULIC OIL or WET DCT HYDRAULIC CONTROL OIL — Owner's Manual, printed p. 37.[1]
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Published volumes are 3.49–3.59 US qt (3.3–3.4 L) gear oil and 2.59–2.64 US qt (2.45–2.5 L) control oil; exact routine service-fill procedure Needs research — Owner's Manual, printed p. 37.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** DOT 4; quantity as required — Owner's Manual, printed p. 37.[1]
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD sheet.[1][2]
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** No separately serviceable front-differential fluid quantity was published in the inspected owner-manual table; Needs research.[1]
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD sheet.[1][2]

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 79–94 lb-ft (107–127 N·m) — Owner's Manual, printed pp. 36 and 764.[1]
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the physical tire-information placard as authority. The manual's general table lists 35 psi (240 kPa) front and rear for the published 17-, 18- and 20-inch full-size tire packages, but installed equipment and placard control — Owner's Manual, printed pp. 36 and 40.[1]
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Every 8,000 mi (13,000 km) or 12 months; asymmetric/directional tires shown in the manual rotate front-to-rear only, and the installed tire design controls the pattern — Owner's Manual, printed pp. 793 and 844.[1]

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Exact replacement part number Needs research. Inspect every 8,000 mi/12 months and replace every 24,000 mi/36 months under the normal schedule; replace more frequently for severe dusty use — Owner's Manual, printed pp. 793–798.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Exact replacement part number Needs research. Inspect at 8,000-mi/12-month alternating intervals and replace every 16,000 mi/24 months under the normal schedule; replace more frequently for severe dusty/polluted/mountain use — Owner's Manual, printed pp. 793–798.[1]
- **Oil filter:** Exact Kia replacement part number Needs research.[1]
- **Battery specification:** Kia Media lists a 60 Ah battery for the 2.5L T-GDI; confirm the label, size, CCA and AGM/conventional construction on the installed battery before replacement — Kia Media, Engine/Electrical specifications.[3]
- **Spark plugs and gap:** Kia Media identifies iridium spark plugs; exact Kia part number and gap Need research. Normal replacement interval is 48,000 mi (78,000 km) — Owner's Manual, printed p. 793; Kia Media, Engine/Electrical specifications.[1][3]
- **Exterior bulb types:** Front lighting is LED. Rear Type A uses 21/5W stop/tail, P21W turn-signal and W5W side-marker bulbs; Rear Type B uses LED stop/tail, turn-signal and side-marker assemblies. Backup bulbs are W16W, high-mounted stop lamp is LED, and license-plate bulbs are W5W. Confirm installed Type A/Type B equipment — Owner's Manual, printed p. 35.[1]

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Normal: every 8,000 mi (13,000 km) or 12 months, subject to the Oil Life Management System. Severe conditions: every 5,000 mi (8,000 km) or 6 months — Owner's Manual, printed pp. 792–798.[1]
- **Brake fluid:** Inspect every 8,000 mi (13,000 km) or 12 months; replace every 48,000 mi (78,000 km) or 48 months — Owner's Manual, printed p. 793.[1]
- **Engine air filter:** Normal replacement every 24,000 mi (39,000 km) or 36 months; more frequently in severe dusty conditions — Owner's Manual, printed pp. 793–798.[1]
- **Cabin air filter:** Normal replacement every 16,000 mi (26,000 km) or 24 months; more frequently for listed severe conditions — Owner's Manual, printed pp. 793–798.[1]
- **Coolant:** First replacement at 120,000 mi (195,000 km) or 120 months, then every 24,000 mi (39,000 km) or 24 months — Owner's Manual, printed p. 793.[1]
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Inspect wet-DCT fluid every 32,000 mi (52,000 km) or 48 months under the normal table. The extracted severe-use row requires visual confirmation of its fixed replacement interval, so that interval remains Needs research. AWD-only rear differential and transfer-case service does not apply to this FWD sheet — Owner's Manual, printed pp. 794 and 797.[1]
- **Spark plugs:** Normal replacement every 48,000 mi (78,000 km); the severe table says more frequently for listed conditions without a fixed shortened mileage — Owner's Manual, printed pp. 793 and 797.[1]

## Variant warnings

- Kia's 2026 pricing matrix lists the 2.5L turbo GDI with wet 8-speed DCT and FWD for EX and SX; do not apply naturally aspirated/8AT, AWD, Hybrid or PHEV data — Kia Media pricing matrix.[2]
- FWD excludes the AWD transfer case and rear differential.[1][2]
- The official Owner's Manual and Kia Media specification page disagree on oil and coolant capacities. This sheet remains `conflict` until current Kia service information clarifies the differing definitions.[1][3]
- Tire pressure always comes from the actual vehicle placard.

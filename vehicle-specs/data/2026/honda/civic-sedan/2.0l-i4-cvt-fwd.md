# 2026 Honda Civic Sedan — 2.0L inline-4, CVT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, trim, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Honda Motor Oil or API-certified SAE 0W-20 — online Owner's Manual, Specifications.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.2 US qt (4.0 L); 3.9 US qt (3.7 L) without filter — online Owner's Manual, Specifications.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Honda Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant Type 2 — online Owner's Manual, Specifications.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 1.43 US gal (5.42 L) change quantity including the remaining 0.17 US gal (0.65 L) in the reserve tank — online Owner's Manual, Specifications.
- **Transmission fluid:** Honda HCF-2 Transmission Fluid — online Owner's Manual, Specifications.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** 2.9 US qt (2.7 L) change quantity for the 2.0L gasoline/CVT configuration — online Owner's Manual, Specifications.
- **Brake fluid:** Honda Heavy Duty Brake Fluid DOT 3 — online Owner's Manual, Specifications.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD sheet.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; do not infer a separate service fill from the CVT.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD sheet.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** Needs research; no exact value was accepted from the retrieved exact-year pages in this run.
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the tire-information label on the driver's doorjamb; the actual vehicle placard controls — online Owner's Manual, Specifications.

For non-directional tires, move the rear tires forward and cross the front tires to the rear; directional tires move front-to-rear on the same side — online Owner's Manual, Tire Rotation and Maintenance Minder.

## Filters, battery, bulbs and ignition

- **Engine air filter:** Exact service part number needs research. Maintenance Minder sub-item 2 calls for replacement; dusty use requires replacement every 15,000 mi (24,000 km) — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Cabin air filter:** Exact service part number needs research. Maintenance Minder sub-item 2 calls for replacement; reduced airflow can require earlier replacement — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Oil filter:** Exact service part number needs research; replace with the engine oil when Maintenance Minder sub-item 0 is displayed — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Battery specification:** Exact 12-volt battery type, group size and CCA need research.
- **Spark plugs and gap:** NGK DILZKAR7G11Y; exact gap needs research — online Owner's Manual, Specifications.
- **Exterior bulb types:** Low/high beams, parking/daytime-running lights, front side markers, front turn signals, brake/tail lights, rear side markers and license-plate lights are LED. Rear turn signals are replaceable 21 W amber bulbs, back-up lights are replaceable 16 W bulbs, and the high-mount brake light is 21 W — online Owner's Manual, Specifications.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow Maintenance Minder main item A for engine oil and sub-item 0 for engine oil/filter; no fixed normal mileage is substituted for the calculated reminder — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Brake fluid:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 7 is displayed — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Engine air filter:** Replace when sub-item 2 is displayed; replace every 15,000 mi (24,000 km) when driven primarily in dusty conditions — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace when sub-item 2 is displayed; inspect/replace sooner when airflow is reduced — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Coolant:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 5 is displayed — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Replace CVT fluid when sub-item 3 is displayed. Under mountainous, very-low-speed or high-temperature severe use, replace transmission fluid every 25,000 mi (40,000 km) — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Spark plugs:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 4 is displayed; inspect valve clearance at the same service — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.

## Variant warnings

- This sheet covers only the 2.0L naturally aspirated gasoline/CVT Civic Sedan LX and Sport powertrain; do not apply it to the two-motor Civic Hybrid or the turbocharged/manual Civic Si.

# 2026 Honda Civic Sedan Hybrid — 2.0L hybrid, eCVT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, hybrid system, transmission/drive unit, trim, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Honda Motor Oil or API-certified SAE 0W-20 — Owner's Manual, p. 674.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.0 US qt (3.8 L); 3.7 US qt (3.5 L) without filter — Owner's Manual, p. 674.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Honda Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant Type 2 for both engine and high-voltage systems — Owner's Manual, pp. 674–675.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** Engine coolant change quantity 1.32 US gal (5.01 L) including the 0.13-US-gal (0.51-L) reserve tank; 1.47 US gal (5.57 L) total including reserve. High-voltage-system coolant change quantity 1.05 US gal (3.99 L) and total 1.09 US gal (4.11 L) — Owner's Manual, pp. 674–675.
- **Transmission fluid:** Honda Automatic Transmission Fluid ATF DW-1 for the hybrid drive unit — Owner's Manual, p. 675.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** 1.9 US qt (1.8 L) change quantity — Owner's Manual, p. 675.
- **Brake fluid:** Honda Heavy Duty Brake Fluid DOT 3 — Owner's Manual, p. 675.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD sheet.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; do not infer a separate service fill from the hybrid drive unit.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD sheet.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 80 lbf-ft (108 N·m) — Owner's Manual, p. 483.
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the label on the driver's doorjamb; the actual vehicle placard controls — Owner's Manual, pp. 677–678.
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate when Maintenance Minder sub-item 1 appears; exact pattern locator needs research — Owner's Manual, p. 643.

- **Engine air filter:** Exact service part number needs research. Maintenance Minder sub-item 2 calls for replacement; dusty use requires replacement every 15,000 mi (24,000 km) — Owner's Manual, pp. 643–644.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow Maintenance Minder main item A for oil and sub-item 0 for oil/filter — Owner's Manual, p. 643.
- **Brake fluid:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 7 appears — Owner's Manual, p. 643.
- **Engine air filter:** Replace when sub-item 2 appears; every 15,000 mi (24,000 km) under dusty conditions — Owner's Manual, pp. 643–644.
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace when sub-item 2 appears; every 15,000 mi (24,000 km) under the manual's severe air-quality conditions — Owner's Manual, pp. 643–644.
- **Coolant:** Replace engine coolant when Maintenance Minder sub-item 5 appears — Owner's Manual, p. 643. A separate fixed high-voltage-coolant interval needs research.
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Replace hybrid drive-unit fluid when Maintenance Minder sub-item 3 appears. Under mountainous, very-low-speed or high-temperature severe use, replace every 47,500 mi (76,000 km) or three years, then every 30,000 mi (48,000 km) or two years — Owner's Manual, pp. 643–644.
- **Spark plugs:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 4 appears; inspect valve clearance at the same service — Owner's Manual, p. 643.

## Variant warnings

- This sheet covers only the Civic Sedan 2.0L two-motor hybrid/eCVT FWD powertrain; do not apply it to the non-hybrid 2.0L/CVT or turbo/manual Si.
- The high-voltage and engine cooling circuits have separate capacities and must not be combined.
- Published change and total quantities are retained as labeled and are not substitutes for procedure-specific service information.

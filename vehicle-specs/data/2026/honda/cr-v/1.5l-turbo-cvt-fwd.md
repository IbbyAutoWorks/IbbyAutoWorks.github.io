# 2026 Honda CR-V — 1.5L turbocharged inline-4, CVT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, option codes, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Honda Motor Oil; premium-grade 0W-20 detergent oil with the API Certification Seal is permitted — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 3.7 US qt (3.5 L) including filter; 3.4 US qt (3.2 L) without filter — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Honda Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant Type 2; 50/50 premix — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 1.84 US gal (6.97 L) change quantity, including the 0.13 US gal (0.49 L) reserve tank — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.
- **Transmission fluid:** Honda HCF-2 Transmission Fluid only; do not mix with other transmission fluids — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** 3.7 US qt (3.5 L) change quantity for the 2WD/FWD CVT — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.
- **Brake fluid:** Honda Heavy Duty Brake Fluid DOT 3 — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Needs research; the exact-year owner-manual Specifications do not list a separate transfer-case service fluid for this 2WD/FWD variant.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; no separate front-differential service fluid/capacity is listed outside the CVT entry in the cited owner-manual Specifications.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD sheet.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 80 lbf·ft (108 N·m, 11 kgf·m) — Owner's Manual, **If a Tire Goes Flat / Changing a Flat Tire**.
- **Cold tire pressure:** The actual driver-doorjamb tire-information label controls. The exact-year Specifications list 235/65R17 104H tires at 32 psi (220 kPa) front and rear; 235/60R18 103H tires at 33 psi (230 kPa) front and rear; compact spare T155/90D17 101M at 60 psi (420 kPa). Confirm the installed tire size and placard before adjustment — Owner's Manual, **Specifications**.
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Rotate when Maintenance Minder sub-item 1 appears. For non-directional tires, use the manual's front-to-rear/cross pattern; directional tires move front-to-rear on the same side. Perform TPMS calibration after each rotation — Owner's Manual, **Tire Rotation** and **Maintenance Minder**.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow the Maintenance Minder. Sub-item 0 calls for engine-oil and oil-filter replacement; if the oil-life message does not appear more than 12 months after reset, change the oil annually — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.
- **Brake fluid:** Replace every 3 years; Maintenance Minder sub-item 7 may also appear — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.
- **Engine air filter:** Replace under sub-item 2; replace every 15,000 mi (24,000 km) when driven primarily in dusty conditions — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace under sub-item 2; shorten the interval in high-dust conditions or when persistent HVAC odor remains after deodorizing — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.
- **Coolant:** Replace under Maintenance Minder sub-item 5; the cited owner-manual page does not publish a fixed normal-use mileage interval — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Replace CVT fluid under sub-item 3. Under mountainous, very-low-speed or trailer-towing severe use, replace transmission fluid every 25,000 mi (40,000 km). Rear-differential service is not applicable to this FWD sheet — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.
- **Spark plugs:** Replace under Maintenance Minder sub-item 4; inspect valve clearance at the same service. The cited owner-manual page does not publish a fixed normal-use mileage interval — Owner's Manual, **Maintenance Minder**.

## Variant warnings

- This sheet covers the gasoline 1.5L turbo/CVT front-wheel-drive powertrain only. Do not use its engine-oil, coolant, transmission-fluid or ignition specifications for a 2026 CR-V hybrid.
- FWD/2WD CVT change quantity differs from AWD, and the FWD variant has no rear-differential service entry.
- Tire pressure is trim/tire-size dependent; the vehicle's placard is authoritative.
- Honda Maintenance Minder codes are condition-based. Do not invent fixed normal-use mileage intervals where the exact-year manual gives only a code.

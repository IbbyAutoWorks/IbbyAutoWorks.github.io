# 2026 Honda Passport — 3.5L V6, 10-speed automatic, AWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, trim, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Honda Motor Oil or API-certified SAE 0W-20 — online Owner's Manual, About Specifications.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 5.8 US qt (5.5 L); 5.5 US qt (5.2 L) without filter — online Owner's Manual, About Specifications.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Honda Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant Type 2 — online Owner's Manual, About Specifications.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 1.76 US gal (6.67 L) change quantity including the remaining 0.209 US gal (0.790 L) in the reserve tank — online Owner's Manual, About Specifications.
- **Transmission fluid:** Honda ATF Type 2.0 — online Owner's Manual, About Specifications.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** 4.4 US qt (4.2 L) change quantity — online Owner's Manual, About Specifications.
- **Brake fluid:** Honda Heavy Duty Brake Fluid DOT 3 — online Owner's Manual, About Specifications.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Honda HGO-1 transfer assembly fluid; 0.58 US qt (0.55 L) change quantity — online Owner's Manual, About Specifications.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; do not infer a separate service fill from the transmission or transfer case.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Honda DPSF-II; 1.76 US qt (1.67 L) change quantity — online Owner's Manual, About Specifications.

The rear turn signal is the replaceable 21 W amber WY21W bulb — online Owner's Manual, About Specifications.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow Maintenance Minder main item A for engine oil and sub-item 0 for engine oil/filter; no fixed normal mileage is substituted for the calculated reminder — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Brake fluid:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 7 is displayed — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Engine air filter:** Replace when sub-item 2 is displayed; replace every 15,000 mi (24,000 km) when driven primarily in dusty conditions — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace when sub-item 2 is displayed; inspect/replace sooner when airflow is reduced — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Coolant:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 5 is displayed — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Replace automatic-transmission and transfer fluids when sub-item 3 is displayed; no exact fixed severe-use transmission mileage was accepted in this run. Replace rear-differential fluid when sub-item 6 is displayed; for repeated low-speed mountain or towing use, replace at 7,500 mi (12,000 km), then every 15,000 mi (24,000 km) — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Spark plugs:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 4 is displayed; the same sub-item calls for timing-belt replacement and water-pump inspection. Under regular operation above 110°F (43°C), below -20°F (-29°C), or trailer towing, replace the timing belt every 60,000 mi (100,000 km) — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.

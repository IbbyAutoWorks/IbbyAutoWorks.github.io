# 2026 Honda Odyssey — 3.5L V6, 10-speed automatic, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, body configuration, mobility-equipment conversion status, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Honda Motor Oil or API-certified SAE 0W-20 — Owner's Manual, p. 722.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 5.7 US qt (5.4 L); 5.4 US qt (5.1 L) without filter — Owner's Manual, p. 722.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Honda Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant Type 2 — Owner's Manual, p. 722.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 1.74 US gal (6.59 L) change quantity including the 0.24-US-gal (0.91-L) reserve tank; 2.06 US gal (7.81 L) total including reserve — Owner's Manual, p. 722.
- **Transmission fluid:** Honda ATF Type 2.0 — Owner's Manual, p. 723.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** 5.5 US qt (5.2 L) change quantity; 9.4 US qt (8.9 L) total — Owner's Manual, p. 723.
- **Brake fluid:** Honda Heavy Duty Brake Fluid DOT 3 — Owner's Manual, p. 723.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Honda HGO-1 transfer-assembly fluid; 0.48 US qt (0.45 L) change quantity — Owner's Manual, p. 723.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; do not infer a separate fill from the transmission or transfer assembly.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD sheet.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow Maintenance Minder main item A for oil and sub-item 0 for oil/filter; service is due when the system displays the item — Owner's Manual, pp. 700–701.
- **Brake fluid:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 7 appears — Owner's Manual, pp. 700–701.
- **Engine air filter:** Replace when sub-item 2 appears; every 15,000 mi (24,000 km) under dusty conditions — Owner's Manual, pp. 700–701.
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace when sub-item 2 appears; every 15,000 mi (24,000 km) under the manual's severe air-quality conditions — Owner's Manual, pp. 700–701.
- **Coolant:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 5 appears — Owner's Manual, pp. 700–701.
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Replace transmission fluid and transfer fluid when sub-item 3 appears. Under the manual's mountainous, low-speed or high-temperature severe use, replace transmission fluid every 30,000 mi (50,000 km) — Owner's Manual, pp. 700–701.
- **Spark plugs:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 4 appears; inspect valve clearance at the same service — Owner's Manual, pp. 700–701.

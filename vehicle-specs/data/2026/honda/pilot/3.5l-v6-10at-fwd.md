# 2026 Honda Pilot — 3.5L V6, 10-speed automatic, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, trim, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Honda Motor Oil or API-certified SAE 0W-20 — online Owner's Manual, Specifications.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 5.6 US qt (5.3 L); 5.3 US qt (5.0 L) without filter — online Owner's Manual, Specifications.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Honda Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant Type 2 — online Owner's Manual, Specifications.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 1.48 US gal (5.60 L) change quantity; 2.14 US gal (8.1 L) total including the 0.21-US-gal (0.8-L) reserve tank — online Owner's Manual, Specifications.
- **Transmission fluid:** Honda ATF Type 2.0 — online Owner's Manual, Specifications.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** 5.7 US qt (5.4 L) change quantity — online Owner's Manual, Specifications.
- **Brake fluid:** Honda Heavy Duty Brake Fluid DOT 3 — online Owner's Manual, Specifications.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD sheet.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; do not infer a separate service fill from the automatic transmission.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD sheet.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 94 lbf-ft (127 N·m) — online Owner's Manual, Specifications.
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the label on the driver's doorjamb; the actual vehicle placard controls — online Owner's Manual, Specifications.
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Maintenance Minder sub-item 1 calls for rotation. For non-directional tires, move the rear tires forward and cross the front tires to the rear; directional tires move front-to-rear on the same side — online Owner's Manual, Tire Rotation and Maintenance Minder.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow Maintenance Minder main item A for oil and sub-item 0 for oil/filter; no fixed normal mileage is substituted for the calculated reminder — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Brake fluid:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 7 is displayed — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Engine air filter:** Replace when sub-item 2 is displayed; replace every 15,000 mi (24,000 km) when driven primarily in dusty conditions — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace when sub-item 2 is displayed; inspect/replace sooner when airflow is reduced — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Coolant:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 5 is displayed — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Replace automatic-transmission fluid when sub-item 3 is displayed. Under sustained mountainous low-speed/high-temperature operation, replace at 60,000 mi (100,000 km), then every 30,000 mi (50,000 km) — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Spark plugs:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 4 is displayed; the same sub-item also calls for timing-belt replacement, water-pump inspection and valve-clearance inspection — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.

> Verify the VIN, engine, transmission, drivetrain, trim, body configuration, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Honda Motor Oil or API-certified SAE 0W-20 — Owner's Manual, p. 679.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 5.7 US qt (5.4 L); 5.4 US qt (5.1 L) without filter — Owner's Manual, p. 679.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Honda Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant Type 2 — Owner's Manual, p. 679.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** 1.93 US gal (7.30 L) change quantity including the 0.23-US-gal (0.87-L) reserve tank; 2.20 US gal (8.33 L) total including reserve — Owner's Manual, p. 679.
- **Transmission fluid:** Honda ATF Type 3.1 — Owner's Manual, p. 680.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** 4.63 US qt (4.38 L) change quantity; 7.33 US qt (6.94 L) total — Owner's Manual, p. 680.
- **Brake fluid:** Honda Heavy Duty Brake Fluid DOT 3 — Owner's Manual, p. 680.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Honda HGO-1 transfer-assembly fluid; 0.58 US qt (0.55 L) change quantity — Owner's Manual, p. 680.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; do not infer a separate service fill from the transmission or transfer assembly.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Honda DPSF-II; 1.92 US qt (1.82 L) change quantity and 2.01 US qt (1.90 L) total — Owner's Manual, p. 680.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow Maintenance Minder main item A for oil and sub-item 0 for oil/filter — Owner's Manual, p. 636.
- **Brake fluid:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 7 appears — Owner's Manual, p. 636.
- **Engine air filter:** Replace when sub-item 2 appears; every 15,000 mi (24,000 km) under dusty conditions — Owner's Manual, pp. 636–637.
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace when sub-item 2 appears; every 15,000 mi (24,000 km) under the manual's severe air-quality conditions — Owner's Manual, pp. 636–637.
- **Coolant:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 5 appears — Owner's Manual, p. 636.
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Replace transmission and transfer fluids when sub-item 3 appears and rear-differential fluid when sub-item 6 appears. Under the manual's severe mountainous/towing/high-temperature use, replace transmission fluid every 30,000 mi (50,000 km); rear-differential fluid is replaced at 7,500 mi (12,000 km), then every 15,000 mi (24,000 km) — Owner's Manual, pp. 636–637.
- **Spark plugs:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 4 appears; inspect valve clearance at the same service — Owner's Manual, p. 636.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 80 lbf-ft (108 N·m) — online Owner's Manual, About Specifications.
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the label on the driver's doorjamb; the actual vehicle placard controls — online Owner's Manual, About Specifications.
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Maintenance Minder sub-item 1 calls for rotation. For non-directional tires, move the rear tires forward and cross the front tires to the rear; directional tires move front-to-rear on the same side — online Owner's Manual, Tire Rotation and Maintenance Minder.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow Maintenance Minder main item A for engine oil and sub-item 0 for engine oil/filter; no fixed normal mileage is substituted for the calculated reminder — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Brake fluid:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 7 is displayed — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Engine air filter:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 2 is displayed; exact severe-use fixed mileage needs research — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 2 is displayed; inspect/replace sooner if airflow is reduced — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Coolant:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 5 is displayed — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Replace CVT fluid when Maintenance Minder sub-item 3 is displayed; exact severe-use fixed mileage needs research — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Spark plugs:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 4 is displayed; the same sub-item calls for valve-clearance inspection — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.

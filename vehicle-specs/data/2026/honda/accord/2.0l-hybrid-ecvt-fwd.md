# 2026 Honda Accord Hybrid — 2.0L inline-4 hybrid, eCVT, FWD

> Verify the VIN, engine, hybrid system, trim, tire placard, and current OEM service information before use. High-voltage hybrid-system service requires appropriate training and safety procedures.

## Fluids and capacities

- **Engine oil type/specification:** Genuine Honda Motor Oil or API-certified SAE 0W-20 — online Owner's Manual, About Specifications.
- **Engine oil capacity with filter:** 4.0 US qt (3.8 L); 3.7 US qt (3.5 L) without filter — online Owner's Manual, About Specifications.
- **Coolant type/specification:** Honda Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant Type 2 for both the engine and high-voltage system coolant circuits — online Owner's Manual, About Specifications.
- **Cooling-system capacity:** Engine coolant change quantity 1.21 US gal (4.6 L), total 1.45 US gal (5.5 L); high-voltage-system coolant change quantity 1.27 US qt (1.2 L), total 1.90 US qt (1.8 L) — online Owner's Manual, About Specifications.
- **Transmission fluid:** Needs research; no exact service-fluid specification was accepted in this run for the two-motor hybrid drive.
- **Transmission capacity/service quantity:** Needs research; do not apply the gas-model HCF-2 CVT fluid or quantity to this hybrid sheet.
- **Brake fluid:** Honda Heavy Duty Brake Fluid DOT 3 — online Owner's Manual, About Specifications.
- **Transfer case fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD sheet.
- **Front differential fluid/capacity:** Needs research; do not infer a separate service fill from the hybrid drive unit.
- **Rear differential fluid/capacity:** Not applicable to this FWD sheet.

## Wheels and tires

- **Wheel fastener torque:** 80 lbf-ft (108 N·m) — online Owner's Manual, About Specifications.
- **Cold tire pressure:** Use the label on the driver's doorjamb; the actual vehicle placard controls — online Owner's Manual, About Specifications.
- **Tire rotation pattern/interval:** Maintenance Minder sub-item 1 calls for rotation. For non-directional tires, move the rear tires forward and cross the front tires to the rear; directional tires move front-to-rear on the same side — online Owner's Manual, Tire Rotation and Maintenance Minder.

## Basic service intervals

- **Oil/filter:** Follow Maintenance Minder main item A for engine oil and sub-item 0 for engine oil/filter; no fixed normal mileage is substituted for the calculated reminder — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Brake fluid:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 7 is displayed — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Engine air filter:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 2 is displayed; exact severe-use fixed mileage needs research — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Cabin air filter:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 2 is displayed; inspect/replace sooner if airflow is reduced — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Coolant:** Replace engine and high-voltage-system coolant when Maintenance Minder sub-item 5 is displayed — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.
- **Transmission/axle/transfer-case fluids:** Needs research; no hybrid-drive-unit fluid service trigger was accepted in this run.
- **Spark plugs:** Replace when Maintenance Minder sub-item 4 is displayed; the same sub-item calls for valve-clearance inspection — online Owner's Manual, Maintenance Minder.

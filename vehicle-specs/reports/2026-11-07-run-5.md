- The Odyssey sheet records Honda's 0W-20 oil specification and 5.7-US-qt oil-with-filter quantity, Type 2 coolant and 1.74-US-gal change quantity, ATF Type 2.0 and 5.5-US-qt change quantity, HGO-1 transfer-assembly fluid, DOT 3 brake fluid, 80-lbf-ft wheel torque, plug identifiers and Maintenance Minder/severe-use triggers.[1]
- The Ridgeline sheet records 0W-20 and 5.7 US qt with filter, Type 2 coolant, ATF Type 3.1 and 4.63-US-qt change quantity, HGO-1 transfer-assembly fluid, DPSF-II rear-differential fluid and 1.92-US-qt change quantity, DOT 3 brake fluid, 80-lbf-ft wheel torque, plug identifiers and normal/severe service triggers.[2]
- Both CR-V Hybrid sheets record separate engine and high-voltage cooling circuits, 0W-20 and 4.0 US qt with filter, ATF DW-1 hybrid-drive-unit service, DOT 3 brake fluid, 80-lbf-ft wheel torque, plug identifier and Maintenance Minder triggers; FWD and AWD coolant/drive-unit quantities remain separate, and only AWD carries the 1.32-US-qt DPSF-II rear-differential change quantity.[3]
- The Civic Sedan Hybrid sheet records separate engine and high-voltage coolant quantities, 0W-20 and 4.0 US qt with filter, ATF DW-1 with a 1.9-US-qt change quantity, DOT 3 brake fluid, 80-lbf-ft wheel torque, plug identifier and Maintenance Minder/severe-use triggers.[4]
- Unsupported filter parts, battery group/CCA data, plug gaps, complete bulb inventories and other unresolved fields remain `Needs research`; no neighboring-year or neighboring-powertrain values were substituted.

## Conflicts and applicability handling

- No direct conflict was found among the accepted exact-year OEM and government sources.
- CR-V Hybrid FWD and AWD remain separate records because their engine-coolant and drive-unit quantities differ, and rear-differential service applies only to AWD.[3][11][12]
- Civic non-hybrid and Si data remain excluded from the Civic Sedan Hybrid sheet.[4][13]
- Odyssey mobility-equipment recall applicability is not generalized to every factory-standard minivan.[7]
- Published change and total quantities are labeled as such and are not presented as proof of every service procedure's refill amount.

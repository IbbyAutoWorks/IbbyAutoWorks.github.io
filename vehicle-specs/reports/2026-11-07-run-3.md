## Mechanical fields

- The 1.5L sheet records Honda's 0W-20 oil specification, 3.7-US-qt oil-with-filter quantity, Honda Type 2 coolant, HCF-2 CVT fluid, DOT 3 brake fluid, 80-lbf-ft wheel torque, placard-controlled tire pressure and Maintenance Minder triggers.[1][2][3]
- The hybrid sheet records Honda's 0W-20 oil specification, 4.0-US-qt oil-with-filter quantity, separate engine and high-voltage coolant change/total quantities, DOT 3 brake fluid, 80-lbf-ft wheel torque, placard-controlled tire pressure and Maintenance Minder triggers.[5][6][7]
- The hybrid battery section distinguishes the lithium-ion high-voltage battery from the serviceable 12-volt lead-acid battery without inventing a group size or CCA rating.[5][8]

## Sources and reference enrichment

- The Rogue AWD sheet now records nine model-level 2021 Rogue campaigns while explicitly excluding campaign 25V437000 as authority for the PR25DD sheet because the official applicability text names 1.5L and 2.0L VC-Turbo engines.[1]
- Three F-150 sheets now record the 29 returned model-level campaigns. Campaign 24V635000 is attached to the 2.7L sheet because the official text names 2.7L/3.0L Nano EcoBoost engines, and it is explicitly excluded from the 3.5L and 5.0L sheets.[2]
- The Escape 1.5L AWD sheet now records four engine-specific campaigns, while the 2.0L AWD sheet preserves the distinction between broader model campaigns and records limited to 1.5L gasoline or 2.5L hybrid/PHEV powertrains.[3]
- The Camry AWD sheet now records campaign 23V865000 with VIN-specific applicability.[4]
- The Equinox AWD sheet now records four returned campaigns covering seat-belt fastening bolts, specified tires, the fuel-pump module and rear-seat LATCH anchorage coating.[5]
- The Silverado 5.3L sheet now records five potentially relevant model/equipment campaigns while explicitly excluding diesel campaigns 24V797000/26V083000 and the 6.2L-specific campaign 25V274000.[6]
- The generic Grand Cherokee API returned 13 campaigns, but it does not expose a WK body field and includes PHEV/4xe and Grand Cherokee L/new-body records; no campaign was generalized to the legacy WK sheet without VIN/body confirmation.[7]
- No TSB was labeled as a recall. No owner complaint was used as mechanical authority.

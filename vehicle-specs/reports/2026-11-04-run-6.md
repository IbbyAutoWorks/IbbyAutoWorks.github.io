## Sources and reference enrichment

- The Toyota RAV4, Camry and Corolla sheets now record NHTSA campaign 23V865000 while retaining VIN-specific applicability warnings.[1][2][3]
- The Hyundai Elantra sheet now records six returned campaigns covering seat-belt pretensioners, windshield bonding, seat-recliner welds and rearview-camera display failure.[4]
- The Nissan Rogue sheet now separates nine PR25DD-relevant model-level campaigns from campaign 25V437000, whose official summary names only 1.5L and 2.0L VC-Turbo engines.[5]
- The Nissan Altima AWD sheet now records campaigns 21V138000 and 23V628000 for the year/make/model lookup.[6]
- The Ford Escape sheet now distinguishes four records explicitly naming 1.5L engines from hybrid/2.5L and broader model-level campaigns.[7]
- The Ford F-150 2.7L sheet now identifies campaign 24V635000 as explicitly including the 2.7L Nano EcoBoost, while keeping the other returned campaigns subject to cab, axle, package, screen, equipment and VIN confirmation.[8]
- The Chevrolet Equinox sheet now records four returned campaigns and adds the previously absent government/reference-enrichment section.[9]
- The Ram 1500 sheet now separates the 5.7L eTorque-specific campaign 23V265000 from DT-wide, option-specific, diesel and Classic records.[10]
- No TSB was labeled as a recall. No owner complaint was used as mechanical authority.

- No conflict was found with existing mechanical sources because the NHTSA records were not used as specification authority.

- The Rogue VC-Turbo campaign and the Ram Classic/diesel records were explicitly prevented from being generalized to the covered PR25DD and DT 5.7L variants.[5][10]
